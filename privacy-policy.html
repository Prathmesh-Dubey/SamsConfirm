Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SAMS Mobile (“we”, “us”) is registered in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what personal data we collect through sams-mobile.com and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit our contact or distributor enquiry forms we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, company, country and message. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or distributor application.
  • To send you the Smart Ex-04 datasheet and launch updates you have requested.
  • To maintain a record of pre-launch interest by market.

Legal basis

We process enquiry data on the basis of legitimate interest (responding to a request you initiated) and, for launch updates, your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your data. Enquiry data is shared only with our hosting and email providers as needed to operate the site, and retained no longer than 24 months after last contact.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Contact info@sams-mobile.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Cookie usage is described in our Cookie Policy. You can change your consent at any time via the preferences button on the site.
