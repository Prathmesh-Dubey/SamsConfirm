Last updated: September 2026

This site uses a small number of cookies. On your first visit you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice at any time via the consent preferences button in the bottom-left corner of the site.

Required for the site to function — they store your cookie consent choice and enable basic security features. These do not store personally identifiable data and cannot be switched off.

Set only with your consent, these help us understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, time on page) so we can improve it. Data is aggregated and not used to identify you.

Functional and advertising cookies

Currently not in active use. If we introduce them, they will only be set with your prior consent and this policy will be updated.

Managing cookies

Besides our consent banner, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect how the site works.
