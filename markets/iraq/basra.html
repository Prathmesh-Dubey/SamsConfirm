Explosion proof phone for Basra and the southern fields

Basra governorate carries most of Iraq’s production, and almost all of it moves through classified areas: degassing stations spread across super-giant fields, the gas plants built to capture what used to be flared, a refinery on the Shatt al-Arab and the export terminals out in the Gulf. The site · Basra

What is classified around Basra

This is not a fenced industrial city. The classified areas are scattered over a very large area of flat ground: fields north and west of the city, gas processing beside them, refining and port industry to the south, and the loading terminals offshore. A device is usually specified once and then carried across several of those, under operators who each run their own technical review.

The conditions this site actually imposes

Three things decide equipment arguments in Basra more often than any specification line: the summer, the sourness of the crude, and how long a replacement takes to arrive.

A summer at the top of the global range

Open plant south of the city holds its heat from morning to night, and a field round offers no shade. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and the temperature class is what an instrumentation engineer compares against the maximum surface temperature the classification allows.

Sour crude as the normal case

Hydrogen sulphide at a degassing station is routine rather than exceptional, so personal detection is already on the belt. Bluetooth pairing keeps a gas detector and the handset working as one set instead of occupying both hands.

Dust events that last for days

Airborne sand settles into every seam and every port. IP68 sealing is what determines whether a unit still works afterwards, and Zone 21 coverage matters where bulk and fertiliser handling sit beside the gas plots.

Replacement is a long loop

Between customs clearance, site access and camp rotation, a failed handset stays failed for weeks. A sealed body, MIL-STD-810H construction and a 4,000 mAh cell are specified for that reality.

Ground with no landmarks

Field routes run across flat terrain where one manifold reads like the last one. Four-constellation GNSS attaches a real position to a photograph, which is the difference between a note and a work order.

Several standards on one crew

International operating organisations write to IEC 60079 or the American code, and the state companies apply their own vendor registration on top. A published certificate and a full marking string travel across those reviews; an undocumented device is argued at every gate.

Questions · Basra

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof phone in Basra today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch, and IECEx, ATEX and the Iraqi conformity route are all still running ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. No unit goes into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Basra?

No figure is published. Until certification completes there is no list price and no committed delivery, and any supplier naming one for an uncertified Ex device is promising more than the paperwork supports. Pre-launch pricing is issued in writing against quantity, configuration and site.

Will the operators at Rumaila or West Qurna accept it?

Each operating organisation decides that through its own engineering and HSE review, and that review reads certificates rather than marketing. The IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the document it is built on, and vendor registration is being prepared with an Iraqi partner.

What Iraqi approvals are needed to import it?

A regulated consignment has to arrive with a Certificate of Conformity issued under the COSQC programme, and the radio needs CMC type approval obtained through a licensed representative inside Iraq. The IECEx certificate is the technical evidence underneath both. None of them is granted yet.

Is it flameproof or intrinsically safe?

Intrinsically safe, marked Ex ib. The two ideas are often confused: flameproof design, Ex d, accepts that ignition may happen and builds an enclosure strong enough to contain it, while intrinsic safety keeps the stored and available energy so low that ignition has nothing to work with. Marking target II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Does it work with the gas detectors our crews already carry?

That is the design intent. Bluetooth pairing with personal gas detectors is part of the specification, and the practical test is done during evaluation with the exact detector model your site issues rather than assumed from a datasheet.

How do repairs and battery replacement work?

Certification covers a sealed assembly, so any repair outside the approved route ends it, and that includes swapping a cell locally. Servicing therefore runs through SAMS and an appointed Iraqi partner, with spares intended to sit in the south so a replacement does not wait on a fresh import.
