Where it works · Netherlands

Where it works in the Netherlands

Dutch classified areas cluster along the water. Rotterdam alone runs refining, petrochemicals and tank storage across the Botlek, Europoort and the Maasvlakte, with Moerdijk and Zeeland on the same corridor and Chemelot inland in Limburg. The gas side has shifted: Groningen production ceased in October 2023 and the field closed for good in 2024, so the hazard sits in import, storage, hydrogen and CO2 rather than extraction.

Operators and contractors such as Shell Nederland, bp Raffinaderij Rotterdam and Zeeland Refinery need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Pernis, Botlek and Europoort. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Regulatory route · Netherlands

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The product route is short and familiar. ATEX 2014/34/EU is implemented in Dutch law through the Warenwetbesluit explosieveilig materieel 2016, so a notified body certificate and the CE marking are the whole requirement and no separate Dutch approval follows. Two details are specific to this market. Instructions have to be supplied in Dutch, which is a supply condition rather than a courtesy. And radio conformity runs under the Radio Equipment Directive, supervised by the Rijksinspectie Digitale Infrastructuur, the RDI, which took over from Agentschap Telecom at the start of 2023.

The obligations that decide what you actually buy sit with the employer. ATEX 153 is transposed into the Arbowet and the Arbobesluit, and the practical output is the explosieveiligheidsdocument: the zoning, done to NPR 7910-1 for gas and NPR 7910-2 for dust, plus the reasoning for every item of equipment selected for those zones, with the NEN-EN-IEC 60079 series behind it. The Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie enforces this and publishes its own inspection instructions. On major hazard sites the wording changed on 1 January 2024, when the Brzo 2015 label disappeared and the Seveso rules moved into the Omgevingswet, but the inspection reality did not: the labour inspectorate, the regional environment service and the safety region still visit together, reading the same equipment file.

Is the EX-04Z1 already ATEX certified?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and certification is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and no unit is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering that use exists.

Does the Netherlands require anything national on top of ATEX?

Not as a second approval. ATEX 2014/34/EU is implemented through the Warenwetbesluit explosieveilig materieel 2016, so a notified body certificate and the CE marking are the product-side requirement. Dutch practice adds a manual, safe-use instructions and a declaration of conformity in Dutch, all planned into the launch, plus radio conformity supervised by the RDI.

What does an ATEX phone cost in the Netherlands?

No list price is published before launch, and a quoted price for an uncertified hazardous area device should make any buyer cautious. Pre-launch pricing is given against a defined quantity, configuration and support scope, and evaluation units are placed under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Is this a flameproof phone or an intrinsically safe one?

Intrinsically safe. Flameproof, the Ex d concept, contains an explosion inside a heavy enclosure; intrinsic safety, Ex ib, limits the energy in the circuit so ignition cannot start. Searches for a flameproof mobile usually mean a phone for a Zone 1 area, and Ex ib is what makes a handheld practical.

Can we write it into our explosieveiligheidsdocument before launch?

You can prepare the entry. The datasheet gives the target marking string, temperature class and equipment protection level, so the selection reasoning can be drafted and reviewed. It should not be signed off as installed equipment until the certificate number exists.

How will repair and battery replacement work here?

Through a controlled route, because work on an intrinsically safe device has to be done by a recognised facility for the certification to remain valid. A Benelux service arrangement is part of the launch programme and can be reviewed under a pre-launch agreement.
