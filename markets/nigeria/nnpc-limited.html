The sites · NNPC Limited

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed across NNPC assets

NNPC Limited became a limited liability company under the Petroleum Industry Act 2021, taking over the commercial role of the former national corporation. It holds the state's interests in joint ventures and production sharing contracts, and owns the state's refineries at Port Harcourt, Warri and Kaduna, a gas transmission business, pipelines, depots and retail stations. It also holds 49 per cent of Nigeria LNG. That range means the hazards differ asset by asset: flow stations and crude terminals in the Delta, gas plants and pipelines, refinery process units and tank farms, and product depots. Some of these assets are operated by NNPC itself, others by joint venture partners who set their own HSE rules. Press reports on the refineries' rehabilitation status have changed repeatedly, so check current status before planning a deployment there.

Wide geography, one specification

From swamp flow stations to a Kaduna refinery in the north, ambient temperature and humidity vary. The EX-04Z1 is being certified to a single marking target of Ex ib IIC T4 with an IP68 enclosure, so one specification can be written for every asset.

Remote pipelines and stations

Gas lines run through remote terrain, with long drives between stations. Four constellation GNSS and a 50 MP camera give a located photograph of a pipeline marker or a leak.

Patchy coverage

Cellular signal varies by kilometre, so Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC support plant networks as a fallback. Messaging and forms should queue when the signal drops.

A fleet that must be controlled

A state company with joint venture partners, contractors and rotating staff needs central management. Android Enterprise and an existing MDM console allow enrolment, policy, remote lock and wipe.

Shift and turnaround work

Refinery turnarounds and terminal maintenance bring large contractor crews for short windows, and the 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift without a safe area stop.

Approvals · NNPC Limited

What an NNPC buyer will ask for

NNPC's supplier registration process is not described here; ask its procurement and HSE teams directly what registration, documentation and equipment review apply to the asset in question. Joint venture assets may follow the partner's rules instead. In every case expect the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, marking, gas group and temperature class, together with the Nigerian import and content steps: SONCAP, NCC type approval and a registered Nigerian supplier. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and SAMS has no supply relationship with NNPC Limited.

Questions · NNPC Limited

Frequently asked questions

Is one handset specification enough for every NNPC site?

Often it can be, because Ex ib IIC T4 covers common gas groups, but each area classification decides. Refinery hydrogen, terminal vapour and gas plants should be checked separately.

Are the state refineries running?

Press reports on the Port Harcourt, Warri and Kaduna rehabilitation have changed over time. Check with NNPC before basing a plan on any one refinery's status.

Do joint venture partners accept NNPC's choice of handset?

Not automatically. Where a partner operates the asset, its HSE standard and equipment review apply, so evaluate with the operator on site.

Is SAMS working with NNPC Limited?

No. There is no order, pilot or agreement. The page describes how the device is designed to fit.
