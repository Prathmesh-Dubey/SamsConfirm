The site · Bonga

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed on Bonga

Bonga, in licence OML 118, is Nigeria's first deepwater development and has produced since 2005 through a floating production, storage and offloading vessel moored roughly 120 kilometres off the Niger Delta coast in about a thousand metres of water. SNEPCo is operator, with Esso and Nigerian Agip as partners alongside NNPC Limited. Two further developments are in play. Bonga North, a subsea tie-back to the existing FPSO, took its final investment decision in late 2024. Bonga South West Aparo is, according to press reports, still moving towards a decision, and would need its own FPSO. Since Shell's 2024 sale of its onshore business to Renaissance Africa Energy, deepwater is where SNEPCo's activity concentrates. A deepwater FPSO is a Zone 1 and Zone 2 environment on the process deck, the turret and the offloading area, with dust a minor concern except in blasting and paint work.

What deepwater does to a handset

On an FPSO the gas group is rarely the problem. Weather, rotation and the people carrying the phone are.

Salt air and constant wind

The deck sees salt spray and sustained heat. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the design response, and the temperature class and ambient range on the certificate are what an instrumentation engineer checks.

A rotation that hands over every few weeks

On a fixed crew rotation, what is not written down is lost at handover. A handset that holds photographs and permit status helps outgoing technicians leave a record for the incoming shift.

Wi-Fi first, cellular second

Offshore the carrier signal is poor, and the vessel network does the work. The device carries Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC alongside 4G LTE, and four constellation GNSS gives a position on the deck or the subsea field vessel.

A full shift away from a charger

Crews on deck are away from a safe charging point for hours. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working shift with camera, torch and location in use.

A fleet that must stay under control

Devices move between crews and contractors on a vessel with limited bandwidth. Android Enterprise management supports enrolment, lock and wipe from an existing console.

Approvals · Bonga

What an offshore operator will ask of a supplier

Offshore operators run their own HSE standards and equipment reviews, and the registration detail for SNEPCo should come from SNEPCo directly. Ask the operator's procurement and HSE teams what they require before a device is allowed on an installation: normally the IECEx certificate, the marking and ambient range, a transfer and handling procedure for helicopters and vessels, and a maintenance route. In Nigeria the SONCAP and NCC steps and Nigerian content documentation apply on top. SAMS has no supply relationship with Shell or its partners.

Questions · Bonga

Frequently asked questions

Is Bonga South West Aparo under construction?

Not according to press reports, which describe a project moving through front end engineering and fiscal approvals towards a decision. This page treats it as a possible future development, not a live site.

Did Shell sell its Nigerian business?

Shell sold its onshore Niger Delta joint venture to Renaissance Africa Energy in 2024. SNEPCo continues to operate deepwater assets, which is the focus here.

Can a handset travel offshore by helicopter?

That is governed by the helicopter operator and the installation's HSE rules, not by the handset. Ask the operator for the carriage policy and check it against the device's battery and radio details.

Does SNEPCo use the EX-04Z1?

No. SAMS has no agreement, pilot or order with SNEPCo or its partners; this page covers what the device is designed for.
