The site · Kundl, Tirol

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is carried at Sandoz in Kundl, Austria

Kundl is a site in the Inn valley that produces antibiotics from fermentation through to finished oral dosage forms. Novartis, before the spin-off of Sandoz, described investment of more than 100 million euros as part of a 150 million euro commitment to the site, including 50 million euros of public funding, with a modern enzymatic process planned for oral amoxicillin and construction to be completed by 2025. The same announcement said that a sterile active ingredient plant would be built in Palafolls in Spain, leaving Kundl to concentrate on oral antibiotics. For a handset, the picture is a mix of large fermenters, downstream recovery with organic solvents, and powder processing, all under strict contamination control. That is a different problem from a refinery.

Two approvals, not one

A device in a classified process area needs an Ex certificate and also has to satisfy contamination control rules. Sandoz's quality teams decide what may enter, and the certificate does not answer their questions about materials and cleaning.

Cleaning and wipe-down

A handset that enters controlled areas has to be wiped down. IP68 describes ingress protection, not chemical resistance, so the housing material and disinfectant compatibility are datasheet questions and no claim is made here.

Photographs under policy

Photography inside a pharmaceutical plant is restricted. Android Enterprise policy can disable or restrict the camera by area, and the 50 MP camera is for use where the site allows it.

Records that must be attributable

Pharmaceutical records need to show who did what and when. A managed handset with user enrolment can support that, though validation of any software remains the plant's job.

Questions · Sandoz Kundl

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Kundl?

No. Certification is in progress for a Q4 2026 launch, and we make no claim to any relationship with Sandoz. Acceptance follows the plant's own engineering and quality review.

Does an Ex certificate satisfy GMP?

No. They address different questions. The Ex certificate covers ignition protection, and GMP covers contamination and data control. A plant needs both answers.

Which solvents are used at Kundl?

We do not state them. The plant's classification documents list the substances and gas groups, and the safety officer checks them against the certificate.
