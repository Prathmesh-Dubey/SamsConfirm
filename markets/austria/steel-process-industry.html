Why it fits steel and process industry

Built for coke-oven gas and bulk material dust, not petrochemical hazards

Integrated steelworks carry hazards distinct from oil, gas or chemicals: coke-oven gas has its own toxicity and flammability profile, and coal, coke and ore handling generate their own combustible dust.

Coke-oven gas as a specific classified-area driver

Coking operations at integrated steel works generate coke-oven gas, a flammable gas mixture with its own hazard characteristics distinct from natural gas or petrochemical process gas, and equipment in these areas needs evaluation against that specific gas composition.

Zone 21/22 dust at coal, coke and ore handling

Bulk material handling for coal, coke and iron ore at Linz and Donawitz generates combustible dust, and the EX-04Z1's Zone 21 / Zone 22 target addresses that classification.

Blast furnace and steelmaking heat exposure

Beyond the classified-area gas and dust hazards, integrated steelworks expose equipment to significant ambient heat near blast furnaces and steelmaking vessels, a durability factor alongside the Ex rating itself.

A broader process-industry category beyond steel alone

AGRANA's sugar, starch and bioethanol plants fall within this same industry grouping, bringing combustible dust and alcohol-vapour hazards that share some classification logic with steelmaking's dust exposure but a different underlying chemistry.

Fleet management across integrated, multi-hazard sites

EMM and MDM enrolment supports an operator managing devices across sites combining gas, dust and heat exposure from one console.

Questions · Steel and process industry

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for steel industry use in Austria yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is coke-oven gas covered by a standard hydrocarbon gas rating?

Coke-oven gas has its own hazard characteristics; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued, and site HSE teams should confirm applicability to this specific gas composition.

Does the device suit high-heat areas near blast furnaces?

Environmental durability testing addresses general heat exposure, though extreme proximity to molten metal processes requires site-specific PPE and equipment placement rules regardless of device rating.

Is AGRANA's process the same hazard class as voestalpine's steelworks?

Both involve combustible dust classification, but AGRANA's sugar, starch and alcohol-vapour hazards are chemically distinct from voestalpine's coke-oven gas and metallurgical dust.
