Why it fits

Built for a European-Gulf joint venture petrochemical producer

With Mubadala as a major shareholder alongside OMV, Borealis's engineering review can be informed by both an Austrian ATEX-native operating culture and a Gulf-linked ownership interest with its own international standards exposure.

ATEX as the operating standard, informed by Gulf ownership interest

Borealis's Austrian plants operate under ATEX as the direct regulatory framework, while Mubadala's ownership stake connects the company into a wider network of Gulf-region industrial and energy investments, a context that can shape how documentation from a device already active in Gulf markets, such as this site's UAE and Saudi Arabia pages, is received.

Zone 1 gas at cracker and polyolefin units

Ethylene cracking at Schwechat and polyolefin production at both Schwechat and Linz are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of that service.

Two plant locations under one Austrian operating company

Borealis runs cracker and polyolefin operations at both Schwechat and Linz, meaning a single device standard needs to work consistently across two distinct Austrian sites.

A joint venture with genuinely global reach beyond Austria

Borealis operates polyolefin plants across multiple countries, meaning documentation prepared for its Austrian operations may have relevance to the company's other international sites.

Fleet management across a two-site Austrian operation

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Borealis managing devices consistently across Schwechat and Linz from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Borealis sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and ATEX certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does Mubadala's ownership affect Borealis's certification requirements?

Borealis's Austrian plants operate under ATEX as the governing framework regardless of ownership structure; Mubadala's stake is a corporate ownership fact rather than a technical certification requirement.

Does the device cover both the Schwechat and Linz plants?

Yes, the Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 specification is designed to cover both sites consistently.

Is Borealis the same company as OMV?

No, Borealis is a separate joint-venture company in which OMV holds a stake alongside Mubadala; it runs its own procurement process distinct from OMV's own refining and upstream operations.
