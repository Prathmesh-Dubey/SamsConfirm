Regulatory route · Kuwait

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in Kuwait runs through the Public Authority for Industry under the Kuwait Conformity Assurance Scheme. A regulated consignment clears customs against a Technical Evaluation Report covering the model and a Technical Inspection Report covering the shipment itself. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence underneath that paperwork is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this is a phone it also needs CITRA type approval for the radio. None of the three is finished.

Getting through the plant gate is a separate exercise. KOC and KNPC each run vendor registration and tender prequalification with their own technical and telecoms review, and a foreign manufacturer is normally expected to bid through a registered Kuwaiti agent. The Kuwait Fire Force licenses fire safety at the facility and the Environment Public Authority holds the environmental permits, but neither of them approves a handset. Plan for two timelines: the national conformity route, and the operator engineering approval that actually decides whether the device is carried into the area.

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Kuwait today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the KUCAS conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Kuwait?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price and no quoted delivery, and a supplier who gives you a firm figure for an uncertified Ex device is telling you something they cannot support. Pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Is an explosion proof phone the same thing as an intrinsically safe phone?

They are different protection concepts, although the search terms are used interchangeably. Flameproof, written Ex d, contains an explosion inside a heavy enclosure. Intrinsic safety, written Ex ib, limits circuit energy so ignition cannot occur, and that is what a Zone 1 handset uses. The marking target here is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Which zones and gas groups is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued alongside, which matters on Kuwaiti projects written by contractors working to the American code rather than to IEC 60079.

Will KOC or KNPC accept it on site?

That decision belongs to the operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review is built on, and the vendor file is being prepared with a Kuwaiti partner.

Will it survive a Kuwaiti summer?

It is designed to, with IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing behind it. The number to evaluate is the temperature class on the certificate, checked against the maximum surface temperature your area classification permits.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both. Your own site applications keep whatever languages they already use.

How do repairs and battery replacement work?

A device holding an Ex certificate cannot be opened on site or repaired by a general phone workshop without invalidating it. The service route runs through SAMS and the appointed Kuwaiti partner, with spares pooled in Ahmadi.
