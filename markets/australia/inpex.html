The sites · Ichthys

Explosion proof mobile phone areas across Ichthys LNG

Ichthys gas and condensate is produced about 220 km off the Western Australian coast. The Ichthys Explorer is a semi-submersible central processing facility, and the Ichthys Venturer is a floating production, storage and offloading vessel that handles the liquids. Gas leaves through the subsea export pipeline to Bladin Point, where a 520 hectare site near Darwin liquefies it for export, with design rates of about 8.9 million tonnes of LNG and 1.6 million tonnes of LPG a year, plus condensate. That gives INPEX two separate worlds to equip: a marine platform and vessel, and a large onshore industrial plant, each with its own permit system and its own logistics.

What the Top End does to a handset

Darwin and the Browse Basin share one trait: the weather is as demanding as the classification. A device has to pass the area drawing first, then survive the wet season.

Battery4,000 mAh

Heat and humidity all year

Darwin's climate is hot and humid for most of the year, with a wet season of heavy rain and storms. IP68 sealing keeps rain and condensation out of the housing, and the marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Lightning in the wet season

Storms interrupt outdoor work on the plant. A handset that is carried to a shelter or a muster point has to be robust, not precious, and rugged build is aimed at drops and shock rather than weather alone.

Offshore and onshore on one device

The same Zone 1 designed handset can go from the Bladin Point plant to the offshore facilities, so a single MDM profile can follow a technician through a rotation.

A long plant to cover

Four-constellation GNSS and the 50 MP camera attach a position and a photograph to an inspection record on a site of 520 hectares, instead of a verbal description.

Policy that follows the site

Android Enterprise management lets INPEX's IT team set camera, application and update policy by group, for example stricter on the offshore facilities than at the offices.

Work pattern and buying

How work is organised, and what buyers ask

INPEX operates Ichthys with a large contractor workforce, and the offshore workforce works on rotation. These are the points to raise with INPEX's own teams. SAMS makes no claim of any relationship with INPEX.

Offshore rotations
Offshore crews travel on fixed rotations, so a device that stays on the facility is handed from crew to crew. Remote lock and wipe from the MDM console cover the handover.
Turnaround and shutdown
Planned shutdowns at the onshore plant bring many contractors for a short time. Ask whether contractors use site-issued devices, since that decides who holds the certificate documents.
Supplier approval
INPEX's procurement and HSE teams set the requirements. SAMS has not been through any INPEX process; ask them for their current equipment documents and prequalification steps.
Evidence for the engineer
The IECEx certificate and its scope, ANZEx from the same test reports and the ACMA RCM radio marking are what an Australian engineer works from. All are in progress ahead of Q4 2026.
Offshore safety case
Facilities in Commonwealth waters are regulated by NOPSEMA, so any new device type is expected to fit the operator's safety case, and that is a matter for INPEX.
Questions · INPEX

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Ichthys yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, with IECEx and ANZEx certification in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. No certificate has been issued.

Does one handset cover the offshore facilities and Bladin Point?

That is the design intent. Both involve gas classified areas, and the Zone 1 target covers them, but each facility's own area drawings and permits still decide where it may go.

What matters for wet-season use?

Sealing and shock resistance matter most: IP68 for rain and wash-down, and rugged build for knocks. Site rules on working outdoors in storms are INPEX's.

Do we need to bring Japanese-format documents?

SAMS cannot say what INPEX will ask for. The IECEx certificate is the international basis, and INPEX's own procurement and HSE teams will say whether anything else is required.
