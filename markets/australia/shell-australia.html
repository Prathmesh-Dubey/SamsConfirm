Why it fits

Built for a standalone floating LNG facility, not a fixed platform

Prelude combines gas production, liquefaction, storage and offloading on a single floating hull, a configuration unlike any fixed offshore platform or onshore LNG plant elsewhere in Australia.

Zone 1 gas across production and liquefaction on one vessel

Gas processing, liquefaction and LNG storage are all contained within Prelude's hull rather than split across separate platform and onshore facilities, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups present across that combined footprint.

Motion and marine exposure unlike a fixed platform

As a floating, moored vessel, Prelude experiences continuous wave motion and marine weathering that a fixed offshore platform or onshore plant doesn't, conditions the device's ruggedness and sealing are specified to handle.

No onshore backup facility to fall back on

Unlike Chevron's or INPEX's projects, which pair offshore production with onshore processing, Prelude's entire liquefaction and storage capability sits on the vessel itself, meaning equipment reliability on board carries more operational weight.

A world-first engineering concept with a small number of comparable facilities globally

As one of very few floating LNG facilities of its kind in operation anywhere, Prelude's equipment and vendor-approval decisions have limited precedent to draw on from other Australian sites, making direct engagement with Shell's own project team especially relevant.

Fleet management for a single, self-contained facility

EMM and MDM enrolment suits managing devices across Prelude's production, liquefaction and offloading areas from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use on Prelude FLNG yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification, including IECEx, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is Prelude's certification different from a fixed offshore platform?

The underlying Zone 1 gas classification is similar, but the vessel's continuous motion and self-contained production-to-storage configuration are engineering factors Shell's own review considers beyond the certificate itself.

Does the device suit continuous marine motion exposure?

Ruggedness and sealing are specified with sustained marine exposure in mind, though Shell's own vessel-specific requirements should always be confirmed.

Is Prelude the same as QCLNG?

No, Prelude is a floating offshore facility in Western Australia; QCLNG is an onshore liquefaction plant on Curtis Island, Queensland, fed by coal seam gas. Both are Shell Australia assets but operate very differently.
