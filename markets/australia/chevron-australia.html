Why it fits

Built for a nature-reserve LNG site with carbon capture at scale

Barrow Island's conservation status and Gorgon's carbon capture and storage operations both add requirements beyond what a standard coastal LNG plant needs to consider.

Quarantine and biosecurity clearance ahead of any Ex evaluation

Every item entering Barrow Island, a Class A nature reserve, undergoes a formal quarantine inspection process to protect the island's conservation status, a clearance step that applies before equipment ever reaches its Zone 1 classified-area use.

The world's largest dedicated carbon capture and storage project

Gorgon injects captured CO2 into a deep reservoir beneath Barrow Island at a scale unmatched by any other single facility, adding CO2-handling equipment and monitoring points to the site's classified-area footprint alongside its LNG processing trains.

Zone 1 gas across LNG trains and CCS infrastructure

Gorgon's LNG liquefaction trains and its CO2 injection wells and compression facilities both include Zone 1 gas-hazard areas, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon and process gas groups relevant to both.

A second major site at Wheatstone with different logistics

Chevron's Wheatstone LNG project at Onslow operates on the mainland rather than an offshore nature reserve, meaning Chevron Australia's device standard needs to work across genuinely different site-access regimes.

Fleet management across two major, differently regulated sites

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Chevron Australia managing devices consistently across Gorgon's nature-reserve site and Wheatstone's mainland facility.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Gorgon or Wheatstone yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification, including IECEx, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does equipment need special approval to enter Barrow Island?

Yes, all equipment undergoes a formal quarantine and biosecurity inspection process specific to the island's Class A nature reserve status, separate from and in addition to Ex certification.

Does the carbon capture system need different certification from the LNG trains?

Both fall under the general Zone 1 gas-hazard classification; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued, and site-specific CO2-handling procedures should be confirmed with Chevron's engineering team.

Does approval at Gorgon carry to Wheatstone?

Chevron's global engineering standards apply across both, but each site's own access and operational requirements, including Barrow Island's unique biosecurity regime, are managed separately.
