Built for Kazakh operating conditions

Kazakhstan is a market of extremes: steppe winters far below freezing, hot summers, wind-driven dust, and sites hundreds of kilometres from a city.

For evaluators in Kazakhstan, the EX-04Z1 explosion proof mobile phone is designed around these points: Steppe winters and summer heat; Sour gas and the right gas group; Coal dust and Zone 21; A long shift in the cold; Remote sites and long distances; Control over a large fleet.

Steppe winters and summer heat

Ambients swing from deep winter cold to summer heat on the same site. The certified ambient range is stated on the certificate and will be published when it is issued; check it against your coldest shift before planning winter use.

Sour gas and the right gas group

Tengiz and Karachaganak process gas with high hydrogen sulphide, a toxic hazard as well as a flammable one. A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers the gas groups involved; personal gas detection remains a separate requirement.

Coal dust and Zone 21

Coal handling, conveying and processing around Karaganda and Ekibastuz create Zone 21 dust areas, which the design covers. Underground mining is equipment Group I and outside this device's scope.

A long shift in the cold

Cold shortens any battery's working time and there is no safe area socket mid shift. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day, and safe area charging instructions are part of the documentation.

Remote sites and long distances

Between plant, camp and wellsite the carrier signal can thin out. The device falls back to plant Wi-Fi, and four constellation GNSS fixes a position on open steppe.

Control over a large fleet

Handsets pass between operator staff and contractors. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock and remote wipe, with Russian and Kazakh language settings.

Regulatory route · Kazakhstan

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Kazakhstan applies the Eurasian Economic Union technical regulation TR CU 012/2011 on equipment for explosive atmospheres. Ex equipment placed on the market needs an EAC Ex certificate of conformity issued by a certification body accredited in the Union, and carries the EAC and Ex markings. In practice the IECEx test report is the technical basis for that work, which is why IECEx comes first in the programme.

Operator specifications at Tengiz, Kashagan and Karachaganak also ask for the IECEx or ATEX certificate, so both sets of documents travel with the device. Radio equipment needs its own approval for use on Kazakh networks, the large operators run local content programmes that favour supply through Kazakh companies, and documentation in Russian and Kazakh is expected.

Frequently asked questions

Ask about Kazakhstan →
Is the EX-04Z1 approved for use in Kazakhstan yet?

No. The device is pre-launch: IECEx certification is in progress and EAC Ex certification follows it. Nothing should be planned into a classified area until the certificates exist.

Do I need EAC Ex certification in Kazakhstan?

Yes. As a member of the Eurasian Economic Union, Kazakhstan requires Ex equipment placed on its market to hold an EAC Ex certificate under TR CU 012/2011. It is in the programme, sequenced after IECEx.

Will it work through a Kazakh winter?

Only within its certified ambient temperature range, which is stated on the certificate and will be published when it is issued. Check it against your coldest outdoor shift before planning winter use.

Is it suitable for underground coal mines?

No. Underground mining is equipment Group I, a separate category from the surface industry Group II that this device targets. Surface coal handling and processing areas classified as Zone 1 or Zone 21 are within scope.

Does it replace a gas detector on sour gas sites?

No. The Ex rating means the phone will not ignite a flammable atmosphere; it does not detect hydrogen sulphide. Carry personal gas monitors as your site rules require.

Is documentation available in Russian and Kazakh?

Yes. The user manual and safe use instructions will be supplied in Russian and Kazakh, as EAC conformity expects, and MDM profiles can be configured in either language.

When will units be available, and who repairs them?

Launch is Q4 2026, with supply in Kazakhstan following EAC Ex. Warranty and service run through the appointed Kazakh partner, and repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a recognised facility.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Kazakhstan?

No list price is published before the certificates exist. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration through the Kazakh partner, and landed cost depends on EAC Ex and import steps, so it is set with the channel rather than published.
