Why it fits

Designed for a single-purpose plant with a variable-supply operating pattern

Methanex's methanol production runs on a different operating rhythm from a steady-state refinery, shaped by how much gas Taranaki's fields can supply in a given period.

Zone 1 gas at synthesis and storage areas

Methanol synthesis, storage and loading areas at Motunui and Waitara Valley are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon and process gas groups involved.

An operating pattern shaped by upstream gas supply, not steady-state throughput

As New Zealand's largest gas consumer, Methanex has periodically idled or curtailed production trains in response to Taranaki basin supply constraints, meaning field communications need to support frequent restart and shutdown sequences rather than assuming continuous operation.

A Canadian multinational operating two adjacent plants

Methanex is headquartered in Canada and operates globally, and its Taranaki operations run two plants close enough together that equipment and contractor pools can move between them, favouring one consistent device standard across both sites.

Export-focused, world-scale production

Motunui and Waitara Valley are sized for global methanol export markets rather than domestic consumption alone, meaning production decisions here respond to international market conditions as much as to local gas supply.

Fleet management across two co-located plants

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Methanex managing devices consistently across the Motunui and Waitara Valley sites from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Methanex plants yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification, including IECEx, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does gas-supply curtailment affect equipment requirements?

It changes the operating rhythm, with more frequent shutdown and restart sequences, rather than the underlying Zone 1 classification requirement.

Does one device work across both Motunui and Waitara Valley?

Yes, the Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 specification is intended to cover both plants given their proximity and shared contractor pools.

Is Methanex the same as Todd Energy or OMV New Zealand?

No, Methanex is a downstream methanol producer and gas consumer, distinct from Todd Energy and OMV New Zealand, which are upstream gas producers supplying the basin Methanex draws from.
