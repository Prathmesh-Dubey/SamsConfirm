The site · Ballance Agri-Nutrients

What is classified at Kapuni

Kapuni makes ammonia from natural gas and converts it to granular urea, and news reports put it at about a third of the urea New Zealand uses each year. The process starts with reforming, which produces a hydrogen-rich synthesis gas, then ammonia synthesis under pressure, then urea formation and granulation. Natural gas is gas group IIA, hydrogen is IIC, and ammonia is IIA but toxic well below any flammable concentration, so the plant's gas-detection and emergency procedures matter as much as its zone drawings. Granulation and handling of the urea product create dust, and the site's classification decides whether any of that counts as a Zone 21 or 22 area; do not assume. The site is also changing: in February 2026 Ballance reported that its Kapuni Project, with Hiringa Energy, reached financial close, bringing renewable energy and green hydrogen production to the site.

What Kapuni asks of an explosion proof mobile phone in New Zealand

Most of the risks at a fertiliser plant are about what a person does after a release, not only before. The handset has to be safe to carry in a gas atmosphere and useful in an emergency, without being mistaken for a gas detector.

Hydrogen needs IIC

Hydrogen has a very low minimum ignition energy and is the most demanding gas group. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Ex ib IIC T4, so one specification covers the reformer, the synthesis loop and any new hydrogen plant.

Ammonia is a people hazard first

An ammonia release is a toxic event that calls for breathing apparatus and muster. The handset is not a gas detector and does not replace one; its role is to carry the call, a position and a photograph back to the control room.

Stop-start operation

A plant running on short gas contracts is repeatedly prepared for shutdown and brought back. Each cycle generates permits, isolation checks and leak checks, and an enrolled handset keeps their photographs and times together.

One site, one console

Kapuni is a single large site, so an MDM console can manage every device from one place. Android 16, Android Enterprise and existing MDM tools mean policy is set once; the 4,000 mAh cell is specified to last a shift.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kapuni still running?

Ballance flagged a possible shutdown of up to four months in August 2025 and later secured short-term gas contracts, the latest reported running to late March 2026. Check its own announcements for the current position.

Does hydrogen change the requirement?

It raises it. Hydrogen is gas group IIC, so a device must be marked for IIC, as the EX-04Z1 is designed to be, rather than IIA or IIB.

Does urea dust need a Zone 21 device?

Only if the site's classification says so. Zone 21 is a design target of the EX-04Z1, but the classification dossier decides which areas apply.

Is Ballance's Awarua plant covered here?

No. Awarua makes phosphate products near Invercargill and has a different hazard profile, so this page deals with Kapuni.
