Sector · chemicals, pharma and biorefining

What makes these Italian plants different

This sector is a mix with one thing in common: batch and campaign operation. Italy’s biorefineries are the conspicuous example. Eni converted the Porto Marghera refinery at Venice into a biorefinery that opened in June 2014, with an Ecofining process turning vegetable oils into green diesel, and the former Gela refinery has a biorefinery of around 750 kt/y processing capacity. A Livorno conversion has been announced, and a new biorefinery at Priolo has been reported, so check current status before planning. See the page on explosion proof phone for Eni for the site detail. Alongside them are specialty chemical works such as those at Ferrara, Mantova and Ravenna, and a pharmaceutical industry strong in Lombardy and Lazio, with many plants making active ingredients and intermediates. These handle solvents, hydrogen and fine powders in small rooms. The consequence is a mixture of Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust inside one building, cleanroom rules on top, and a tighter record-keeping culture than a refinery has.

Hazards by process

Hydrotreating of fats and oils
Hydrogen under pressure, brings gas group IIC.
Methanol and alcohols
Flammable liquid vapour in transesterification and washing, gas group IIA or IIB.
Solvent recovery
Acetone, toluene and tetrahydrofuran stills with vapour at vents and pumps.
Hydrogenation in pharma
Hydrogen with catalyst, often in a small bay.
Milling, drying and blending
Fine powder with low ignition energy, a Zone 21 case.
Waste solvent storage
Drum and tank areas with mixed vapour.

What these plants do to a handset

The classification matters, but so does the quality regime. A handset in a pharmaceutical suite has to meet photography and data rules as well as Ex rules.

Solvents plus powders in one room

A milling or drying area can have both vapour and powder. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 together, so a technician does not carry separate devices for the two.

Photography is controlled

Pharma sites often restrict cameras for product confidentiality and quality reasons. A camera your own console can switch off, per area, is more useful than a device with no camera.

Hydrogen next to everything

Biorefinery and hydrogenation duties put hydrogen into otherwise ordinary process buildings. A device targeted at gas group IIC covers it, rather than needing a separate selection.

Short campaigns, many visitors

A campaign brings auditors, validation staff and contractors in for days. Enrolment, application control and remote wipe allow a handset to be issued, tracked and recovered.

Frequently asked questions

Is a solvent bay Zone 1?

It often is around charging points, vents and open transfers, but the classification is set by the employer’s explosion protection document and not by this page. The handset is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21.

Can the camera be disabled in cleanrooms?

Through Android Enterprise policy from your own console, yes in design. Test the exact policy during an evaluation.

Do biorefineries need IIC equipment?

Where hydrogen is in the process area, yes, and the EX-04Z1 is being certified to IIC. The site’s classification confirms.

When can a plant evaluate it?

It is pre-launch for Q4 2026 and certification is in progress. Evaluation units are offered under a trial agreement; use in a classified area waits for the certificate.
