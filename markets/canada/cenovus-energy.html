Why it fits

Designed for a thermal in situ specialist with an unusual offshore diversification

Cenovus's core business runs on a distributed well-pad model rather than a single mine site, and its offshore Atlantic holdings add a genuinely different operating environment to the same corporate device standard.

A distributed well-pad footprint, not a concentrated mine

SAGD operations at Foster Creek and Christina Lake spread steam-generation facilities and well pads across boreal forest terrain, meaning field crews travel between dispersed pad sites rather than working within one concentrated mine boundary.

Zone 1 gas at steam-generation and processing facilities

Steam generation, produced-fluid processing and associated gas-handling equipment at SAGD sites are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of that service.

Offshore Atlantic interests entirely apart from heavy oil

Cenovus holds offshore Atlantic interests alongside its core thermal in situ heavy oil business, a genuinely different operating environment (marine, offshore platform) that the same corporate device standard needs to accommodate.

Remote boreal forest logistics

Cenovus's SAGD well pads sit in more remote, forested terrain than a Fort McMurray-area mining operation, favouring rugged, self-sufficient equipment for crews working across dispersed sites.

Fleet management across dispersed pads and offshore assets

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Cenovus managing devices consistently across both its dispersed onshore SAGD operations and its offshore Atlantic interests.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Cenovus sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and CSA certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is SAGD the same equipment environment as oil sands mining?

No, thermal in situ operations spread across dispersed well pads rather than one concentrated mine site, though both fall under similar Zone 1 gas-hazard classification at their respective processing facilities.

Does the device need separate certification for Cenovus's offshore assets?

The Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 specification is designed to cover both onshore SAGD facilities and offshore platform equipment, though site-specific classification should be confirmed for each.

Is Cenovus the same as Canadian Natural Resources?

No, both operate thermal in situ assets, but they are separate companies with separate procurement processes; CNRL additionally operates mining, which Cenovus does not.
