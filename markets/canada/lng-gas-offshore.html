Why it fits LNG, gas and offshore

Built for Canada's first LNG export chain and its Atlantic offshore fields

Kitimat's LNG liquefaction, Montney's inland gas processing and the Grand Banks' offshore platforms sit on opposite coasts and in an inland basin, sharing little beyond the underlying Zone 1 gas classification.

Zone 1 gas across liquefaction, gas processing and offshore platforms

LNG liquefaction trains at Kitimat, gas processing plants in the Montney basin, and offshore platform equipment on the Grand Banks are all classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups common across all three.

Canada's first LNG export facility brings a new equipment conversation

LNG Canada's Kitimat terminal, with its first export cargo loaded in 2025, represents Canada's entry into LNG export, meaning device standards for cryogenic-adjacent equipment at this scale are still being established domestically rather than following long-established local precedent.

Indigenous partnership and consultation shape project development

Major Canadian resource projects, including LNG Canada, typically involve extensive Indigenous partnership and consultation processes as part of project development and ongoing operations, a context distinct from resource development in many other countries covered on this site.

Extreme distance between the sector's three operating regions

Kitimat's Pacific coast location, Montney's inland gas basin and the Grand Banks' Atlantic offshore fields are thousands of kilometres apart, meaning a single device standard needs to work across genuinely different climates and logistics contexts.

Fleet management across geographically extreme operations

EMM and MDM enrolment supports an operator managing devices across coastal LNG, inland gas processing and offshore platform operations from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use in this sector in Canada yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including CSA recognition, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit cryogenic LNG terminal equipment at Kitimat?

Environmental testing addresses general cold-exposure durability; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued for cryogenic-adjacent equipment.

Does the device suit offshore platform work on the Grand Banks?

Yes, the Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 specification is designed to cover offshore platform equipment alongside onshore LNG and gas-processing operations.

Does Indigenous partnership affect equipment certification requirements?

It shapes project development and community engagement processes rather than the technical Ex certification itself, though it is an important context for how projects like LNG Canada proceed.
