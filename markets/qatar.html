Who it is for · Qatar

Who needs an ex mobile phone in Qatar

Qatar runs a small number of very large assets, which changes the buying question. It is rarely about one site. It is about whether a device can be standardised across an industrial city where thousands of people move between units on the same permit system.

01

Operations and shift supervisors

A unit in a Ras Laffan train or a Mesaieed plant is walked, not driven. One handset that carries the round, the photograph and the call removes the choice between recording something properly and getting back on time.

02

HSE advisers and permit issuers

You file the equipment protection level and the full marking string, not a brochure page. Qatari operators expect the selection of portable electrical equipment inside a classified area to be documented before it is issued, not afterwards.

03

Instrument and inspection technicians

A 50 MP camera with phase-detect autofocus records a corroded support or a leaking seal at a quality worth attaching to the work order, without arranging a separately permitted camera for the trip.

04

Procurement and contracts teams

Tawteen scoring shapes bid evaluation across the energy sector, so which registered Qatari entity holds the supply agreement is part of the technical conversation rather than a later commercial detail.

How the specification meets Qatari conditions

Coastal heat with humidity behind it, plants measured in square kilometres, and offshore rotations that put a spare a boat ride away. Those three facts shape most of what a Qatari evaluation asks about.

Heat carried in wet air

Coastal Qatar pushes past 45 °C with humidity that dry-heat testing does not represent. MIL-STD-810H covers temperature, humidity and salt fog, and the ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is the line your instrumentation group will read first.

Dust and product powder

Urea and polymer handling at Mesaieed puts combustible dust alongside flammable vapour, so the design covers Zone 21 as well as Zone 1. IP68 sealing is what keeps a handset serviceable through a shamal.

Distance inside one fence

Ras Laffan and Mesaieed are cities rather than plants. A 4,000 mAh battery specified for a full shift matters more here than in a compact facility where stores is five minutes away.

Photography that runs on permit

A camera inside a Qatari energy site is controlled. Android 16 with enterprise management lets a telecoms team disable or restrict the camera by policy, which is how a camera-equipped device becomes acceptable at the gate.

Offshore and marine interface

On Halul, on an Al-Shaheen platform or at an LNG berth, the next replacement arrives with the next boat. A fully sealed body and MIL-STD-810H construction are what hold a unit in service until then.

One radio estate, not two

4G LTE outside the fence, Wi-Fi inside it, Bluetooth for gas detectors and headsets, NFC for asset tags. Push-to-talk over cellular runs as an application rather than as a second device on the belt.

Regulatory route · Qatar

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

On the product side, conformity for goods entering Qatar is administered by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry through the national standards and metrology organisation, which requires regulated imports to arrive with a conformity certificate from an accepted body. Radio equipment is separate again: the Communications Regulatory Authority operates type approval through its e-Spectrum system, and the application is made by a licensed representative in Qatar. Underneath both sits the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, the document a hazardous-area engineer actually reads. None of them is issued yet.

The commercial gate is Tawteen. QatarEnergy’s In-Country Value programme scores suppliers and contractors on their local contribution and feeds that score into bid evaluation across the energy sector, so a supplier without an ICV scorecard is at a disadvantage before the technical evaluation begins. Alongside that, the Civil Defence Department under the Ministry of Interior governs fire and life safety at the facility, and the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change holds the environmental permits. Neither approves a handset, but both shape the site rules it has to live inside.

Questions · Qatar

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 available in Qatar yet?

No. It is pre-launch, with a Q4 2026 launch date, and the certifications behind it including IECEx, ATEX and Qatari product conformity are still running. We will not put a unit into a classified area before the certificate covering that use exists.

How much does an ATEX phone cost in Qatar?

There is no published figure while certification is in progress. Any pricing before a certificate exists is a guess dressed as a quotation. Tell us the quantity, the site and the timing and we will give you pre-launch pricing on request.

Will QatarEnergy accept it inside Ras Laffan or Mesaieed?

That approval is QatarEnergy’s to give and it follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review works from, and registration with a Tawteen ICV score is the commercial half.

Which zones and gas groups does it cover?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust, with an ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued in parallel, which matters on Qatari projects written by contractors using the American code.

Can it be used offshore and on the LNG berths?

It is designed for it, with IP68 sealing, MIL-STD-810H testing and a 4,000 mAh battery sized for a full shift. Even so, we would rather you tested one on your own platform or jetty before committing a fleet to it.

Does it handle Arabic, and can the camera be locked down?

Yes to both. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English, and enterprise management lets your telecoms team disable or restrict the camera by policy, which is usually the condition of taking any camera into a Qatari energy site.

What happens when a unit is damaged?

An Ex certified device cannot be opened by a general repair shop without voiding its certification, so battery replacement and repair run through SAMS and the appointed Qatari partner, with spares pooled within reach of Doha.

Will it work with our MDM and push-to-talk platform?

It runs Android 16 with standard enterprise management, so it enrols into the EMM or MDM platform you already operate, and push-to-talk over cellular runs as an application. Send your build requirements with the evaluation request.
