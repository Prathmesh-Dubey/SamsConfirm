The sector · Oil and gas

Who operates upstream in Kuwait, and where

Kuwait Oil Company produces onshore from the south, where Greater Burgan sits, to the north, where the Jurassic gas programme covers Sabriya, Umm Niqa, Raudhatain, Bahra and Dhabi. Kuwait Gulf Oil Company handles the Kuwaiti side of the Partitioned Zone at Wafra and Khafji, shared with Saudi Arabia. Production flows to the coast, where Kuwait National Petroleum Company and the tanker fleet take over. The hazard picture runs from near-sweet associated gas in the south, through sour crude at newer Burgan gathering centres, to hydrogen-sulphide-bearing gas in the north. A single supplier specification has to survive all three. Work is organised around long rotations at remote sites, drilling and workover campaigns run by contractors, and gathering centre operators on shift rounds. For company-level detail see the pages on the explosion proof phone for Kuwait Oil Company and for Ahmadi, and for the downstream hand-off, the KNPC and refining pages.

Zone 1 where the area calls for it, Zone 2 elsewhere

Not every upstream area is Zone 1. A device designed for Zone 1 is also suitable for the lower-risk zones, and area classification drawings from each operator are the authority.

Sour gas is a detector question first

Hydrogen sulphide is group IIB, so a group IIC design target covers it, but a personal detector remains the safety device. Bluetooth pairing keeps both on the crew's belt without dropping a hand.

Desert logistics

Long distances and unmanned wellpads make a 4,000 mAh battery and GNSS fixes the practical specification. IP68 sealing is what keeps shamal dust out.

Mixed operators, one estate

Many contractors serve several operators. Enrolment into existing MDM consoles lets a contractor carry one managed handset template across clients.

Use cases · Oil and gas

Where the EX-04Z1 goes to work.

Upstream01

Wellpad and flowline call-outs

A fault on a remote flowline sends a crew to a position better given by coordinates than directions. Navigation, photographs and a call to the planner run from one handset, so the crew does not return to the vehicle to use a separate unit.

Gas02

Sour facility operator rounds

Rounds at a Jurassic facility keep detection on and hands free, with the handset recording readings and reporting a leak. A small crew on a remote facility has no second person to run back for a camera, which is the point of carrying one device.

Projects03

Contractor commissioning

New gathering centres mean permit-driven contractor crews. Enrolled handsets are issued per contract and recovered when it ends, with the camera policy set by the owner's console rather than by each crew.

Questions · Oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for Kuwait oil and gas?

No. It is pre-launch and no certificate has been issued; IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 are in progress.

Does it cover sour gas service?

Hydrogen sulphide is group IIB, and the design target is group IIC, which covers it. The handset is never a substitute for a gas detector.

Does it matter that it is Zone 1 if my area is Zone 2?

Zone 1 equipment can normally be used in Zone 2, but check the classification drawings and the operator's own rules.

Do Wafra and the Partitioned Zone follow different rules?

Possibly. Kuwait Gulf Oil Company runs the Kuwaiti side jointly with Saudi Arabia, so ask which party's procurement and HSE rules govern equipment on a given site before assuming Kuwaiti vendor registration is the only route.

Where do I read about specific operators?

See the pages for Kuwait Oil Company and Ahmadi, and for the downstream side KNPC and refining.
