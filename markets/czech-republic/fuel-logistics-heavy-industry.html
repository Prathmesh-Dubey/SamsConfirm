Why it fits fuel logistics and heavy industry

Built for pipeline logistics and coke-oven gas together

This sector combines the fuel-transport logistics that keep a landlocked country's refineries supplied with the coke-oven gas hazards of integrated steelmaking, two very different classified-area drivers.

Zone 1 gas at pipeline and storage infrastructure

Crude and product pipelines, compressor and metering stations, and underground storage caverns are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups present across that logistics chain.

Coke-oven gas as a distinct hazard from pipeline gas

Integrated steelworks at Ostrava and Třinec generate coke-oven gas, a hydrogen-rich, toxic and flammable byproduct of coking with combustion characteristics distinct from the natural gas and refined products moving through the pipeline network.

A country positioned on multiple international pipeline routes

The Czech Republic sits on both the historic Družba crude pipeline route and the IKL route connecting to Western European supply, meaning pipeline operators here manage infrastructure with genuine east-west strategic significance.

Combustible dust at bulk material handling in metallurgy

Coal, coke and ore handling at integrated steelworks generates combustible dust, adding a Zone 21/22 dimension alongside coke-oven gas at Ostrava and Třinec.

Fleet management across pipeline networks and steelworks

EMM and MDM enrolment supports operators managing devices across geographically dispersed pipeline infrastructure and concentrated steelworks sites.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use in this sector in the Czech Republic yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is coke-oven gas covered by a standard pipeline gas rating?

No, coke-oven gas has its own hazard composition distinct from pipeline-transported natural gas or refined products; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued.

Does the device suit both pipeline infrastructure and steelworks?

Yes, the design intent is a device standard usable across both, though each site's specific classification should be confirmed.

What is the strategic significance of the Družba and IKL pipelines?

They represent the Czech Republic's eastern and western crude supply routes respectively, a dual-route logistics structure distinctive to its landlocked, centrally positioned location in Europe.
