Why it fits

Designed for a privately controlled, long-established steelmaker

Třinecké železárny's nearly two-century operating history and its private ownership structure shape both its classified-area equipment landscape and how new vendor relationships develop.

Coke-oven gas as the defining classified-area hazard

Třinecké železárny's coking operations generate coke-oven gas, a hydrogen-rich, toxic and flammable gas mixture with combustion characteristics distinct from natural gas, and equipment used in these areas needs evaluation against that specific gas composition.

Nearly two centuries of incremental site development

Operating continuously since 1839, the site's classified-area documentation spans an unusually long span of construction eras, a mixed-vintage layout a broadly rated device is designed to sit across.

Private family ownership, not state or multinational control

Unlike many large Central European steelworks under state or multinational ownership, Třinecké železárny remains under private Czech family control, meaning vendor relationships can develop more directly with ownership-level decision-makers than at a large public conglomerate.

Zone 21/22 dust at bulk material handling

Coal, coke and ore handling generate combustible dust, and the EX-04Z1's Zone 21 / Zone 22 target addresses these bulk-handling areas alongside the coke-oven gas hazard.

Fleet management for a long-established single integrated works

EMM and MDM enrolment suits managing devices across Třinecké železárny's coking, blast furnace and steelmaking operations from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Třinecké železárny yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and ATEX certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is coke-oven gas covered by a standard hydrocarbon gas rating?

No, coke-oven gas has its own hazard composition; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued, and site HSE teams should confirm applicability.

Does private ownership change how vendor evaluation works?

It can mean a more directly relationship-driven procurement conversation than at a large public conglomerate, though the underlying technical evaluation still centres on the ATEX certificate.

Is Třinecké železárny comparable to voestalpine in Austria?

Both are integrated steelworks with coke-oven gas hazards, but Třinecké železárny is privately held while voestalpine is publicly listed; each runs its own separate procurement process.
