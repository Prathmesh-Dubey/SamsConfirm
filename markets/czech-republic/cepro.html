Why it fits

Designed for a nationwide refined-product distribution network

Unlike MERO ČR's crude-import pipelines, ČEPRO's network moves finished fuel products to distribution points across the whole country, a genuinely nationwide, distributed operating footprint.

Zone 1 gas at pipeline stations and storage terminals

Refined product pipeline pumping stations and fuel storage terminals across the network are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of refined fuel products.

A nationwide network, not one or two routes

ČEPRO's product pipeline and storage terminal network spans the country far more broadly than MERO ČR's two crude import routes, a distributed footprint where a single device standard simplifies procurement compared with terminal-by-terminal evaluation.

The final link before fuel reaches the retail market

As the operator moving refined product from refineries to distribution points, ČEPRO's infrastructure sits at the final industrial stage before fuel reaches retail stations, a logistics role distinct from upstream refining or crude pipeline transport.

Terminal and pipeline maintenance across dispersed sites

Maintenance and inspection crews travel between multiple storage terminals and pipeline sections spread across the country.

Fleet management across a nationwide terminal and pipeline network

EMM and MDM enrolment supports ČEPRO managing devices consistently across its distributed pipeline and terminal network from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at ČEPRO sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and ATEX certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does ČEPRO handle crude oil or only refined products?

Only refined products; crude oil pipeline transport is handled separately by MERO ČR.

Does the device suit ČEPRO's nationwide terminal network?

Yes, the design intent is a single device standard usable consistently across geographically dispersed terminals and pipeline sections.

Is ČEPRO the same as Orlen Unipetrol?

No, Orlen Unipetrol refines fuel; ČEPRO separately distributes it through pipelines and terminals, and each runs its own procurement process.
