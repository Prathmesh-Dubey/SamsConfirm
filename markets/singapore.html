Who it is for · Singapore

Who the EX-04Z1 is for in Singapore

Singapore plants are small in area and dense in inventory, and a classified area here usually sits a few hundred metres from a neighbour’s fence. Four roles decide whether a handset gets past the gate.

01

WSH and safety managers

The Workplace Safety and Health Act puts the duty on the occupier and the employer to show that equipment taken into a classified area is suitable. A blanket no phones rule is easy to write and hard to work under. A certificate on file replaces it with a rule crews will follow.

02

Electrical and instrumentation engineers

You keep the equipment register and match every item to the area classification. Much of Jurong Island is gas group IIC because hydrogen and ethylene oxide are handled there, so the marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is the first line you will read.

03

Turnaround and project managers

A Jurong Island shutdown brings thousands of contractor personnel through the gate inside a few weeks. Every walk back to a safe area to make a call or take a photograph comes out of the critical path.

04

IT, telecoms and procurement

Any telecommunications equipment used here is registered with IMDA, and only a Singapore dealer holding a Telecommunication Dealer’s Licence can file that registration. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support puts the handset in the same console as the rest of the estate.

Heat and humidity with no cool season

Ambient sits near 30 °C with humidity above 80 per cent for most of the year. IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing are the design answer, and the ambient range on the certificate is the compliance number.

Gas group IIC, not only IIB

Hydrogen, ethylene oxide and acetylene are handled on Jurong Island, so a device rated only to IIB is unusable on parts of the island. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Dust beside vapour

Polymer powder, catalyst and additive handling, and bulk cement at the wharves, put Zone 21 inside the same fence line as Zone 1. The handset is designed for both classifications.

Island working and boat access

Bukom, Sebarok and Busing are reached by launch. A flat handset is not a walk to the stores, it is the next boat, so the 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a whole shift with radio, camera and GNSS running.

Crews that read different languages

Jurong Island work is done by a contractor workforce that speaks several languages, and toolbox talks are run accordingly. Android 16 carries the interface languages a crew reads on one hardware build.

Regulatory route · Singapore

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

What your WSH and electrical teams will ask for is the certificate and the marking string, not the brochure. The Workplace Safety and Health Act places the duty on the occupier and the employer, and the risk assessment behind a device entering a classified area has to name the equipment. Singapore works from the international schemes rather than a national Ex mark: an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, read with the Singapore Standard adoptions of IEC 60079, is what a contractor package asks for. On a Major Hazard Installation the handset also has to sit consistently with the safety case assessed by the Major Hazards Department, staffed jointly by MOM, NEA and SCDF officers. None of those documents exists for this device yet.

The second gate is the radio and the import route. Telecommunications equipment sold or used in Singapore is registered with IMDA under the Equipment Registration Framework on the basis of a supplier declaration of conformity, and that registration can only be filed by a local supplier holding a Telecommunication Dealer’s Licence. It shapes how supply into Singapore has to work, and it is why the launch is being built around a Singapore registered partner. Above both sits the plant owner. Jurong Island occupiers each run their own contractor equipment approval and permit systems, so a device can hold every national document and still wait on a site listing.

Questions · Singapore

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 an explosion proof phone that can be used in Singapore today?

No. It is pre-launch and every certification, IECEx included, is still running ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Nothing belongs in a classified area until the Certificate of Conformity covering it is issued.

Does Singapore accept IECEx, or is there a local Ex certificate to obtain?

Singapore works from the international schemes and there is no separate national Ex mark to collect. The IECEx Certificate of Conformity, read with the Singapore Standard adoptions of IEC 60079, is what a plant works from.

How much does an explosion proof mobile phone cost in Singapore?

We do not publish a price and will not quote one for a device whose certification is unfinished. Pre-launch pricing is on request against a stated quantity, and the landed cost of a compliant unit includes the IMDA registration the dealer files.

Which zone and gas group is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and IIC matters here because hydrogen and ethylene oxide are handled on parts of Jurong Island.

Can we take it onto Jurong Island or Bukom once certification completes?

The certificate is the first requirement rather than the last. Each plant owner runs its own equipment approval and permit system for contractor devices, so allow for a site listing as well.

Does an Ex phone need IMDA registration in Singapore?

Yes. Telecommunications equipment used here is registered with IMDA under the Equipment Registration Framework, and only a supplier holding a Telecommunication Dealer’s Licence can file it. That work is part of the launch programme.

When can we get units, and can we test one now?

Launch is Q4 2026. Until then you can request the datasheet, register a requirement for the first allocation, or join the evaluation programme for a unit on your own site.

Who handles repair and battery replacement in Singapore?

An intrinsically safe handset cannot be opened by a general phone workshop without invalidating its certification. Service will run through SAMS and the appointed Singapore partner, and that route is being defined now.
