Why it fits pharmaceutical manufacturing

Built for solvent-classified API production at national manufacturing scale

Solvent charging, reaction and recovery in API production creates classified areas across a large number of manufacturing sites spread through India's pharmaceutical hubs, a distributed pattern rather than a handful of mega-sites.

Zone 1 gas in API block solvent handling

Solvent charging, reaction and recovery areas in active pharmaceutical ingredient blocks are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the solvent vapour groups typical of that production.

Zone 21/22 dust atmospheres in formulation plants

Powder handling and tablet formulation downstream of API synthesis generate combustible dust atmospheres, and Ex tb IIIC T135°C dust protection applies where a purely gas-rated device would not.

PESO as the domestic regulatory authority

PESO governs hazardous-area equipment approval for Indian pharmaceutical manufacturing sites domestically, alongside the ATEX and IECEx bases used for international reference.

A large number of manufacturing sites rather than a few mega-plants

India's pharmaceutical manufacturing base is spread across many mid-sized facilities in established clusters, a distributed pattern that favours equipment that travels well between sites rather than one built for a single very large plant.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for pharmaceutical use in India yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including PESO and ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does one device need to cover both API synthesis and formulation areas?

Yes, pharmaceutical manufacturing commonly moves material between solvent-based and powder-based stages, which is why a dual gas-and-dust rating matters for this sector.

Is PESO approval required in addition to ATEX for pharmaceutical sites?

PESO is the domestic authority for hazardous-area equipment in India; ATEX and IECEx serve as supporting international technical evidence rather than a substitute.

Does formulation-plant approval extend to an API block at the same company?

Typically each facility runs its own site-level procurement even under common ownership, given the different classified-area profile between the two.
