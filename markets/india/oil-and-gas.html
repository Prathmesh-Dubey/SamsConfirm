Why it fits oil and gas

Built for a sector spanning state refiners, private mega-sites and city gas networks

Indian oil and gas ranges from a state-run refinery network to Reliance's single-site scale at Jamnagar, meaning classified-area equipment needs to work as well for a distributed public-sector fleet as for one very large private complex.

Zone 1 gas across refineries, gas processing and LNG terminals

Refining, gas processing and LNG regasification at sites from Panipat to Dahej are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of that combined scope.

PESO as the domestic regulatory authority

India's Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation governs hazardous-area equipment approval domestically, and the EX-04Z1's certification programme is built to address PESO requirements alongside the ATEX and IECEx bases used internationally.

A mix of state-run and private-sector operators

Indian Oil Corporation, Bharat Petroleum and Hindustan Petroleum run as state-owned companies alongside Reliance Industries and Nayara Energy as private operators, meaning equipment procurement follows different processes depending on which sector a site belongs to.

The single largest refining complex anywhere at Jamnagar

Reliance's Jamnagar site is the largest single-site refining complex in the world, a scale that concentrates a large share of India's classified-area equipment need into one location.

Fleet management across a nationwide multi-operator sector

EMM and MDM enrolment supports operators and EPC contractors like L&T, EIL and Tata Projects managing devices consistently across refineries, gas plants and offshore platforms in different states.

Questions · Oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for oil and gas use in India yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including PESO and ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is PESO approval separate from ATEX or IECEx?

Yes, PESO is India's domestic regulatory authority for hazardous-area equipment and operates alongside, rather than instead of, the ATEX and IECEx frameworks used internationally.

Does approval with a state-run refiner extend to a private operator like Reliance?

No, each company runs its own separate procurement process regardless of whether it is state-owned or private.

Does a large complex like Jamnagar have different requirements than a smaller refinery?

The underlying Zone 1 gas classification is similar across Indian refineries; scale affects the number of devices needed rather than the certification basis itself.
