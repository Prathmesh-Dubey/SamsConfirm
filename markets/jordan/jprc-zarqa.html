Why it fits

Built for a national monopoly refinery with a long operating history

Being the country's only refinery means JPRC's equipment decisions have no domestic peer to benchmark against, and its long operating history means classified-area documentation spans several eras of expansion.

Mixed-vintage classified areas from decades of expansion

Zarqa has grown in phases since its original construction, and a broad Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 rating is designed to sit consistently across older and newer sections of the site rather than requiring different equipment per expansion phase.

Inland processing, not a coastal refinery

Zarqa sits well inland from Jordan's coastline, meaning refined product moves onward by road, rail or pipeline toward Amman and Aqaba, a logistics pattern different from the coastal refineries common elsewhere in the region.

Sole-supplier pressure on operational continuity

As the only domestic refinery, unplanned downtime at Zarqa has outsized consequences for national fuel supply, which raises the operational premium on reliable field communications during turnarounds and upsets.

A monopoly position without a direct domestic competitor's benchmark

Without another refinery in-country to compare vendor decisions against, JPRC's own engineering team is the primary reference point for what gets accepted on site, making early, well-documented engagement more consequential than at a market with several refiners.

Fleet management for a single, large integrated site

EMM and MDM enrolment suits a site the scale and operational continuity requirements of Zarqa, particularly during turnaround periods.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at JPRC yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit an older refinery with mixed-vintage classified areas?

It is designed to be usable across mixed-vintage classified areas without requiring separate devices for older and newer sections, though site-specific classification always takes precedence.

Is JPRC the same company as the fuel distribution business, JOPETROL?

No. JPRC operates the Zarqa refinery; JOPETROL separately handles fuel distribution and storage terminals, and each runs its own procurement process.

Does Jordan have any other refineries to compare vendor standards against?

No, Zarqa is the country's only refinery, so JPRC's own engineering review is the primary domestic reference point for equipment approval.
