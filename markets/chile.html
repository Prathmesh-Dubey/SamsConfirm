Fine mineral dust as the dominant hazard

Copper concentrate and lithium processing generate persistent fine dust across the Atacama operations. The device is designed for Zone 21 as well as Zone 1, and IP68 sealing is what keeps it working through a full shift of exposure.

Extreme altitude and temperature swings

Many Atacama sites sit above 3,000 metres with large day-to-night temperature swings and intense UV. MIL-STD-810H testing and a display built for outdoor use are specified with that environment in mind.

A battery sized for high-altitude shifts

Walking distances at an open-pit mine or a salt-flat processing plant are long, and there is no recharging point inside a classified area. The 4,000 mAh cell is specified for a full shift.

Spanish and English on one build

Chilean operations run with local and international technical staff. Android 16 carries both interface languages on the same handset.

Managed centrally across a dispersed footprint

EMM and MDM enrolment lets an operator with sites spread from the far north to Magallanes in the south manage the fleet from one console.

Regulatory route · Chile

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in Chile runs through the Superintendencia de Electricidad y Combustibles (SEC), which regulates electrical and fuel-handling equipment safety nationally. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that conformity is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs Subtel (Subsecretaría de Telecomunicaciones) type approval for the radio. Neither is finished yet.

Getting past the mine or plant gate is a separate exercise. Codelco and the major private miners each run their own vendor-registration and engineering-approval process for equipment entering a classified area, and supply is normally expected to run through a Chilean-registered partner. A device can be fully SEC-cleared and still be unlisted on an operator’s own equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Chile today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the SEC conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

Is this designed mainly for dust rather than gas hazards?

Chile’s classified-area exposure is dominated by combustible mineral dust from copper and lithium processing, and the device is designed for Zone 21 as well as Zone 1 gas atmospheres, so it covers both.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Chile?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price, and pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Will it handle altitude and desert conditions?

It is designed to, with MIL-STD-810H testing and IP68 sealing behind it. The certificate’s temperature class is the number to check against your specific site conditions.

Will Codelco or the major private miners accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review is built on.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Spanish or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both.
