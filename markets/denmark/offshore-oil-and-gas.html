Why it fits offshore oil and gas

Built for a consortium field structure run through one shore base

Danish North Sea production runs through a joint-venture structure rather than fully separate operators, and Esbjerg's role as the single logistics base for the whole sector shapes how equipment gets specified and moved offshore.

Zone 1 gas on production platforms and risers

Wellhead, separation and riser areas on the Danish Underground Consortium platforms are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 matches the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of North Sea production.

ATEX as the governing EU framework

As an EU and Schengen member, Denmark's offshore equipment requirements sit under the ATEX directives, and the EX-04Z1's certification programme treats that as the primary reference for Danish operators and their contractors.

A consortium structure rather than separate rival operators

TotalEnergies EP Danmark operates the fields with BlueNord and the state's Nordsøfonden as partners, so a single specification decision at consortium level can cover offshore procurement across multiple platforms rather than one company's assets alone.

One shore base carrying all offshore logistics

Esbjerg is the single support port for Danish North Sea operations, meaning crew-change and equipment logistics for the whole sector move through one location, unlike countries where several ports split that role.

Fleet management across a joint-venture platform group

EMM and MDM enrolment supports a consortium managing devices consistently across platforms with shared ownership but distinct operating responsibilities.

Questions · Offshore oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for offshore use in Denmark yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does a consortium-wide agreement cover every partner automatically?

No, each partner in a joint venture typically runs its own procurement approval even where the consortium jointly operates the field.

Is Esbjerg the only route for offshore equipment into Danish waters?

It is the established shore base for Danish North Sea logistics, so most offshore-bound equipment and personnel movements pass through it.

Is ATEX the only certification needed for Danish operators?

ATEX is the primary EU regulatory framework; IECEx serves as useful supporting international technical evidence.
