Milk-powder dust is a real Zone 21 hazard

Dried milk powder is a well-documented combustible dust, and drying towers and storage silos are classified accordingly. The device is designed for Zone 21 as well as Zone 1, covering dairy processing on the same specification as Taranaki gas work.

Maritime humidity and temperate conditions

New Zealand’s climate is temperate and humid rather than desert-hot, which is easier on electronics but still demands proper sealing against condensation and coastal salt air. IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H construction are specified regardless of climate extremity.

A single specification for gas and dairy dust

Operators with both energy and food-processing interests, or contractors working across both sectors, do not need two separate certified devices.

A shift-length battery

Both a Taranaki plant round and a dairy-processing shift run a full working day, and the 4,000 mAh cell is specified for that duration without an in-area recharge point.

Managed like the rest of the fleet

EMM and MDM enrolment pushes the corporate profile and camera policy centrally, useful where dairy-processing sites run strict food-safety and hygiene-photography controls alongside the Ex classification.

Regulatory route · New Zealand

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in New Zealand runs under the joint Australia/New Zealand standards framework (AS/NZS), administered nationally with WorkSafe New Zealand responsible for workplace hazardous-area enforcement. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that conformity is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs Radio Spectrum Management (RSM) approval. None of the three is finished yet.

Getting past the plant gate is a separate exercise. Methanex, Fonterra and the Taranaki operators each run their own engineering-approval process for equipment entering a classified area, and a foreign manufacturer is normally expected to supply through a New Zealand-registered partner. A device can be fully AS/NZS-conformant and still be unlisted on an operator’s own equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

Questions · New Zealand

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in New Zealand today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the AS/NZS conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

Is this really needed for dairy processing, not just oil and gas?

Yes. Dried milk powder is a documented combustible dust, and drying towers and storage areas at dairy plants are classified as hazardous areas under the same Zone system used for gas. The device is designed to cover both.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in New Zealand?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price, and pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Does New Zealand use the same standard as Australia?

Yes, largely. New Zealand and Australia share the joint AS/NZS 60079 series for equipment used in explosive atmospheres, so certification work for one market informs the other.

Will Methanex or Fonterra accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review is built on.
