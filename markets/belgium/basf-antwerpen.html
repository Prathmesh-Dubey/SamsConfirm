Why it fits

Designed for BASF's Verbund model, not a standalone plant

The Verbund concept means BASF Antwerpen functions as a tightly interconnected industrial ecosystem rather than a collection of independent plants, a structure that shapes how equipment moves and gets evaluated across the site.

A site built around shared streams, not isolated units

BASF's Verbund model interconnects dozens of production plants at Antwerpen through shared steam, byproduct and raw-material streams, meaning personnel and equipment routinely move across what would be separate classified areas at a less integrated site.

Zone 1 gas across a very large, diverse process portfolio

The scale and diversity of chemical production at Antwerpen means a wide range of Zone 1 gas-hazard areas exist across the site, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups relevant to BASF's broad chemical portfolio there.

German group engineering standards applied on Belgian soil

As BASF's second largest site globally, Antwerpen's engineering review is informed by group-wide standards developed at BASF's German headquarters and largest site in Ludwigshafen, standards the EX-04Z1's documentation is designed to be recognisable against.

One of the largest single chemical-industry workforces in Belgium

Antwerpen's scale means a very large workforce and contractor base moves through the site, a volume where EMM and MDM fleet management delivers particular value.

Fleet management across a large, interconnected Verbund site

EMM and MDM enrolment supports BASF managing a consistent device profile across the site's many interconnected production units.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at BASF Antwerpen yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and ATEX certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

What is the Verbund model and does it affect equipment needs?

It's BASF's approach of interconnecting production plants to share resources; it means personnel and equipment cross between what would be separately classified areas at a less integrated site more routinely.

Does approval at BASF's German sites carry over to Antwerpen?

Not automatically, but documentation prepared to BASF's typical group standard is intended to be a useful starting point for evaluation at Antwerpen.

Is BASF Antwerpen the same as TotalEnergies' or ExxonMobil's Antwerp sites?

No, all three operate separate, adjacent facilities within the broader Antwerp cluster, each with its own procurement process.
