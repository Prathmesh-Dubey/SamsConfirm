The sector · Antwerp cluster

How the cluster is organised

The Port of Antwerp-Bruges was formed in 2022 by the merger of the ports of Antwerp and Zeebrugge. The petrochemical cluster in the Antwerp part is described on the country page as the second largest in the world after Houston. It is split by the river: the right bank has the Scheldelaan chemical belt, with BASF Antwerpen, TotalEnergies and ExxonMobil; the left bank has Kallo and Lillo, where Borealis makes polyolefins and INEOS builds Project ONE.

The processes divide naturally. Refining and cracking are Zone 1 and Zone 2 gas, with hydrogen at hydrotreaters and crackers; polymer plants add powder and pellet dust; storage and loading add jetty and tank-truck bays, covered on the page for bulk liquid storage and port logistics. Pharmaceutical and fine chemical plants, with solvent and GMP rules, are a different sector, covered in pharmaceuticals and specialty chemicals.

What the sector does to a explosion proof mobile phone in Belgium

Shutdowns are planned months ahead

Turnaround campaigns bring thousands of contractor staff for a fixed window. The unit most helped is the one that can be enrolled quickly, carry the permit and work list, and be wiped at the end. Android Enterprise management does that from the console a site already has.

Contractors move between neighbours

Scaffolders, inspectors and instrument crews work for several operators. Each operator decides, so acceptance at one gate does not carry to the next, but one certificate reference helps each team assess the same device.

Seveso inspection is joint

Major hazard sites are visited by federal and regional inspectors together, as the country page explains. A device whose camera and application policy can be demonstrated is easier to defend in that visit.

Hydrogen and hot lines

Group IIC at T4 is the design target, so the same handset suits a hydrotreater, a cracker and a polymer line without a plant-by-plant selection.

Approval and access

How acceptance works across the cluster

Each operator in the cluster runs its own procurement process, and approval by one does not carry to a neighbour. The product-side law is the same for all: ATEX 2014/34/EU, the CE marking and the declaration of conformity. We do not claim any relationship with any operator. Ask each operator's procurement and HSE teams for their current supplier and contractor requirements, which commonly include SCC or VCA safety certification for contractors. Questions · petrochemicals and refining

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for petrochemical use in Belgium yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and ATEX certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Nothing will be supplied for a classified area before the certificate covering it exists.

Does approval at one Antwerp site carry to the next?

No. Each company decides separately, even where sites share a boundary or a road.

Does the device suit both old and new plants?

The design target is the same: Zone 1 gas, group IIC, T4, plus Zone 21 dust. Actual suitability depends on each plant's zoning file.

Is IECEx needed as well as ATEX?

ATEX is the legal route in Belgium. IECEx evidence is in progress as well, and is useful to operators whose parent company works to international standards.
