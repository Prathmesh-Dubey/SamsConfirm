Why it fits

Designed for a pre-commissioning greenfield construction project

Project ONE's construction phase presents a different equipment conversation from an operating plant: today's workforce is building the classified areas that will exist once the cracker is commissioned.

A construction site today, a Zone 1 classified area tomorrow

During construction, hazardous-area classification isn't yet active in the way it will be once the cracker starts up, but early engagement on equipment standards ahead of commissioning positions a device to be evaluated as part of the site's operational equipment planning before startup rather than after.

Imported US shale ethane as feedstock

Project ONE is designed to run on ethane shipped from US shale gas production, a transatlantic feedstock supply chain that reflects INEOS's broader strategy of using competitively priced American ethane in its European crackers.

One of Europe's few genuinely new large-scale crackers in decades

Few new ethane crackers of this scale have been built in Europe in recent decades, meaning Project ONE represents an unusually large greenfield classified-area build-out compared with the mostly brownfield expansion typical elsewhere in the Antwerp cluster.

A new addition to an already dense industrial cluster

Project ONE sits within the broader Antwerp petrochemical cluster, meaning its eventual operations will add to, rather than stand apart from, the area's existing multi-operator contractor and equipment traffic.

Fleet management planning ahead of commissioning

EMM and MDM enrolment can be planned into Project ONE's operational rollout ahead of startup, positioning device fleet management to be in place from day one of commissioned operations.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Project ONE yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and ATEX certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch; Project ONE itself is also still under construction.

Does a construction site need explosion proof devices now?

Classified-area requirements become active once a plant is commissioned; construction-phase work has its own separate safety requirements, though early equipment evaluation can be useful ahead of startup.

Why does Project ONE import ethane from the US instead of using European feedstock?

INEOS's strategy for this project uses competitively priced ethane from US shale gas production, shipped across the Atlantic, rather than relying on European feedstock sources.

Is INEOS the same as the other Antwerp cluster operators covered on this site?

No, INEOS is a separate company from BASF, TotalEnergies and ExxonMobil, each operating independently within the broader Antwerp cluster.
