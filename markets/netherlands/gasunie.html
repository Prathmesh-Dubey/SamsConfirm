The assets · Gasunie

What is classified across Gasunie’s assets

A gas transmission company is different from a refinery. The hazard is natural gas, group IIA, in pipelines, compressor stations, metering and pressure-reduction stations, and the terminals and storage connected to them, spread over a whole country rather than inside one fence. The hydrogen network changes this: hydrogen is group IIC, with a wide flammable range, so equipment that was acceptable for natural gas may not be for hydrogen. Gasunie is converting existing pipelines and building new ones, and the first section, around 30 km from the Second Maasvlakte to Pernis, was announced in 2023 through its subsidiary HyNetwork Services.

Hydrogen sets the bar at IIC

Natural gas is group IIA, but the hydrogen network brings group IIC. The EX-04Z1 marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is aimed at IIC for that reason, with the certificate to confirm it in due course.

Unmanned sites reached by road

Many stations are visited, not staffed. A technician arriving at one needs a phone that works on the way in, records what was done and sends it back, without a safe-area stop.

Position and photograph as the record

Four-constellation GNSS and a 50 MP camera attach a position and a photograph to an inspection entry, which is more useful than a description on a remote site.

A managed device in a regulated company

Android Enterprise lets the operator control applications and camera centrally, and manage the handsets alongside the rest of its estate.

How the work is organised across a national network

Maintenance01

Station inspection and servicing

Technicians visit compressor and metering stations on a schedule, recording readings and defects. A single handset carries the work order, the camera and the call to the control room in the classified area.

Construction02

Hydrogen network build and conversion

A new or converted pipeline puts contractors on a construction site that becomes a classified area as it is commissioned. A managed handset with a fixed application set suits a crew that changes during the project.

Terminals03

LNG and storage sites

Terminal staff walk between ship connections, storage and process areas. The handset covers voice, photographs and position where radios alone do not.

Questions · Gasunie

Frequently asked questions

Why does hydrogen change the handset requirement?

Hydrogen is group IIC, the most demanding gas group, and it ignites with very little energy. Equipment marked only for IIA or IIB is not appropriate for it, so a target of IIC matters.

Is a phone needed on a mostly unmanned site?

Visits to stations are where the phone is used. A technician needs to log what was done and send a photograph, and doing so inside the classified area saves a trip back to the vehicle.

Does the handset need to work with Gasunie’s existing systems?

It runs Android 16 under Android Enterprise, so it can be enrolled in an existing MDM console. Integration with any specific application is a question for the operator’s IT team.
