Explosion proof mobile phone for Shell Nederland in the Netherlands

Shell Nederland runs its largest Dutch industrial footprint at Pernis, on the Nieuwe Maas in Rotterdam, with a chemical site at Moerdijk linked to it by pipeline. The sites · Pernis and Moerdijk

What is classified at Shell’s Pernis and Moerdijk sites

Pernis is the Shell Energy and Chemicals Park Rotterdam, a refinery and chemicals complex in the Pernis area of the Rotterdam port, and Moerdijk is a separate chemical site on the Hollands Diep inland port, with the two connected by pipeline. They are different hazard profiles. Pernis is a refinery in the traditional sense, with hydrocarbon vapour, hydrogen circuits and large tank farms. Moerdijk is a chemicals plant, with olefins and their derivatives handled as gas and liquid at pressure. Wherever solids are handled, Zone 21 dust can appear alongside Zone 1 gas, and the Dutch zoning practice for them runs on NPR 7910-1 and NPR 7910-2.

Who is on the fence

Shell operations staff
Outside operators, panel operators and area supervisors on continuous shifts at both sites.
Maintenance contractors
Dutch firms such as Stork work alongside the in-house teams, and swell in number for a stop.

Authorities
Nederlandse Arbeidsinspectie, the regional environment service and the safety region, which tend to visit major-hazard sites together.

Classified units and areas

Crude and product processing at Pernis
Distillation, conversion and treating units with light hydrocarbon vapour around pumps and exchangers.
Hydrogen circuits
Hydroprocessing units where hydrogen sets the gas group at IIC.
Tank farms and loading
Storage and loading points on the Nieuwe Maas side of the site.
Chemical process units at Moerdijk
Olefin and derivative units with flammable gas and liquid, handled at pressure.
Solids handling
Wherever powders or pellets are conveyed or stored, combustible dust sits close to gas areas.
Control rooms and offices
Safe areas, which is where devices are checked in and charged.

Two sites, two kinds of hazard

A phone used at Pernis for gas may be carried to Moerdijk where dust is present. A Zone 1 and Zone 21 design target means one handset rather than two, and the certificate will show which marking applies.

Weather and wash-down

Pernis and Moerdijk are open industrial sites on Dutch rivers and canals. IP68 sealing and rugged build cover rain, hosing and the knocks of daily use.

A shift-length battery

Contractors on a stop work long shifts away from a charger. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified to cover a full shift with the camera in use.

Management from the existing console

Android Enterprise enrolment means the site’s own team applies camera and application policy centrally, rather than depending on each user to follow it.

Planned stops on a large site

A stop at Pernis or Moerdijk brings in a contractor population that changes weekly. A single managed handset carries the job pack and the sign-off, and leaves the site with the device rather than with the paper.

Operations02

Routes across process units

Operators walk fixed routes between units, taking readings and photographs. The same handset that records the reading can send a position and a voice message without a trip to a safe area.

Movements between the sites

Product moves between Pernis and Moerdijk by pipeline and other routes. Teams checking a transfer need a phone that works at the pipeline interface and at the destination, not one tied to a single building.

Questions · Shell Nederland

Frequently asked questions

Does Shell Nederland publish what phone it allows?

Not that we have verified. Operators generally set equipment rules in their own HSE standards, which are shared with approved suppliers rather than published. Ask the site’s procurement and HSE teams.

Why does the Moerdijk site matter for a phone?

Wherever a chemical site handles solids, dust comes into the picture. A device for Moerdijk has to be considered for Zone 21 as well as the gas zones, which is why the Zone 21 design target is part of the programme.

Can one device serve both sites?

That is the aim. The marking string, once issued, shows which zones and gas group the certificate covers, and the engineers at each site decide whether it fits their zoning.
