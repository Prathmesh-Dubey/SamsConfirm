Explosion proof mobile phone for Grupa Azoty in Poland

Grupa Azoty is Poland’s leading producer of mineral fertilisers, with nitrogen and chemical plants at Tarnów, Puławy, Police and Kędzierzyn-Koźle, and the Polimery Police propylene and polypropylene complex. This page is for the group’s HSE, maintenance and telecoms teams, and the contractors who work in its plants, looking for an explosion proof mobile phone in Poland that copes with gas and dust together. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 / Zone 21; certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026.

The plants · Grupa Azoty

Explosion proof mobile phone for Grupa Azoty: four plants and a polymer complex

The group’s plants are individually large and different from each other. Tarnów is the original nitrogen and plastics site. Puławy makes nitrogen fertilisers and is one of the world’s larger melamine producers. Kędzierzyn-Koźle pairs nitrogen fertilisers with oxo alcohols and plasticisers. Police combines a fertiliser works with Polimery Police, which uses Honeywell UOP Oleflex propane dehydrogenation to make propylene and a Grace Unipol process for polypropylene; the group says its polypropylene production began in 2023. Ammonia and syngas sit at the root of most of it, which means hydrogen and natural gas inside one fence and powdered product in the next building. A sector view of explosion proof phones in Polish fertiliser and chemical plants covers the process families.

Gas and dust in one walk

A shift can run from a reformer to a bagging hall within an hour. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with Zone 21 dust protection in the same programme, so there is no second device for the dusty half.

Ammonia is toxic before it is flammable

Ammonia’s toxic effect works at lower concentrations than its flammability. A handset does not replace a personal gas monitor; what it does is carry the alarm record and the location of the person who raised it.

Season at the loading bays

Fertiliser dispatch peaks around the growing season, and loading, weighing and bagging crews rotate quickly. Enrolment and restriction through MDM suit that turnover.

Shutdowns at ammonia plants

Planned shutdowns bring contractors from several firms onto the same loop. A single enrolled device type shortens the induction of each crew.

Four sites, one fleet

Tarnów, Puławy, Police and Kędzierzyn are far apart. Managing handsets from one console is the practical alternative to four separate procedures.

Frequently asked questions

Does one handset cover both ammonia plants and polypropylene silos?

The design target, Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust, is meant to. Whether it is right for a given area depends on that area’s gas group, temperature class and dust properties, so check each against the certificate once it is issued.

Is melamine dust a Zone 21 concern?

Fine organic powders can form explosive clouds, and the operator’s dust classification decides. The handset’s dust marking should be read against the plant’s zone drawing and the minimum ignition energy of the powder.

Has Grupa Azoty selected this device?

No. SAMS has no agreement, pilot or supply relationship with Grupa Azoty. Contractors and teams should ask the group’s procurement and HSE functions what it requires of portable equipment.
