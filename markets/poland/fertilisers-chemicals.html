The sector · Fertilisers and chemicals

Explosion proof mobile phone for Polish fertiliser and chemical plants

This page is the process view; the operators are covered separately, with explosion proof mobile phone use at Grupa Azoty taking the group’s own plants. Four process families make up most of the classified area. Ammonia synthesis starts from natural gas and ends with a toxic liquid. Finishing plants turn ammonia into granules, prills and melamine, and generate dust. Chlor-alkali electrolysis at Anwil, Włocławek, produces chlorine, caustic soda and hydrogen, and feeds vinyl chloride and PVC. Oxo plants at Kędzierzyn make alcohols and plasticisers from syngas. Polimery Police adds propylene and polypropylene to the Police site. Anwil is a subsidiary of Orlen, and its place in that group is described under the Orlen operator page. What links these processes is that each one is run around the clock by a small operating crew, and that each stops on a planned cycle for maintenance when contractors outnumber the permanent staff.

What a chemical plant does to a handset

In this sector the issue is rarely a single hazard. It is the number of different ones that sit on the same shift.

Dust beside gas

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 with Zone 21 dust protection under the same programme. One handset serves the reformer, the granulator and the bagging hall.

Hydrogen from electrolysis

Chlor-alkali cell rooms produce hydrogen, gas group IIC, and chlorine, which is corrosive and toxic. The first is a Zone 1 matter, the second a PPE and ventilation matter.

Growing-season peaks

Fertiliser dispatch rises with the agricultural calendar. Loading crews and drivers change often, so the handset needs enrolment that takes minutes.

Turnaround clusters

Ammonia, nitric acid and chlorine plants stop on planned cycles, and contractors move from plant to plant.

Records that stand up

Inspectors and insurers ask for dated, located evidence of what was found and when. A photograph carrying a position and a timestamp is stronger than a handwritten line.

Corrosive atmospheres

Chlorine, ammonia and acid mist attack seals and ports over time. The IP68 rating describes ingress protection, and the plant’s own corrosion and maintenance rules still apply.

Questions · Fertilisers

Frequently asked questions

Does ammonium nitrate dust count as combustible?

It is an oxidiser rather than a typical fuel dust, so how it is classified depends on the plant and the operator’s dust study. Ask the site for its classification rather than assuming.

Does PVC powder need Zone 21?

Fine polymer powder can form explosive clouds in dryers and silos; the plant’s own classification decides which areas are Zone 20, 21 or 22. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 21, not Zone 20.

Who decides whether a handset can enter a classified chemical plant?

The plant, through its permit-to-work rules and its explosion protection document. Ask the operator’s procurement and HSE teams what evidence they need for portable electronic equipment before a contractor brings any on site.

Is the device approved for any Polish chemical plant?

No. Certification is in progress and SAMS has no pilot or order at any Polish chemical plant.
