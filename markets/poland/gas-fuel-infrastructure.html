The sector · Gas and fuel

Explosion proof mobile phone for Polish gas and fuel infrastructure

Three kinds of asset sit under this heading and they behave differently. Gas transmission, with the LNG terminal, compressor stations and reduction stations, is a methane business; see explosion proof phone for Gaz-System. Crude and fuel storage, with tank farms and fuel bases, is a liquid-vapour business; see explosion proof phone for PERN. Between them are the interfaces: marine terminals, rail and road loading, and the point where gas becomes feedstock for a refinery. This page compares what each does to a handset, and what the three have in common: unmanned sites, long routes and contractors for short outages. Both operators also feed refineries such as the Orlen sites at Płock and Gdańsk, so a refinery contractor will meet this infrastructure at its fence.

What differs between them

Methane
Gas group IIA, lighter than air, disperses upwards.
Gasoline vapour
Gas group IIA, heavier than air, collects in bunds and pits.
Cold LNG vapour
Cold vapour behaves differently until it warms.
Hydrogen blending
If gas networks ever carry hydrogen, IIC becomes the relevant group.
Dust
Absent in most of these assets, with rail and bulk loading exceptions.
Pressure
High-pressure gas and low-pressure liquid fail differently; a release is a jet in one case and a pool in the other.

Distance and the lone worker

Compressor stations, valve nodes and tank farm outskirts are visited by one person at a time. A device with position, camera and voice lowers the cost of every visit.

Weather on open ground

Tank farms and valve stations have little shelter. IP68 sealing and a rugged build are aimed at that, with the ambient range fixed by the certificate.

Gas group and temperature class

Gas group IIC and T4 are above what methane or gasoline vapour requires, which leaves margin. The operator’s classification drawing is still the controlling document.

Not inside a tank or a pipe

Zone 0 spaces are outside the design target. Tank entry and pipe entry stay on the operator’s procedures.

Managed as part of the fleet

Android Enterprise and existing MDM consoles manage the handset alongside the rest of the operator’s devices.

Short outages, many contractors

Compressor and tank outages are brief, and contractors are on site for days, not months. Enrolment and wiping must therefore be quick.

Questions · Gas and fuel

Frequently asked questions

Is a methane area less demanding than a fuel tank area?

The gas group is less demanding, but the zone is what matters, so a Zone 1 location is a Zone 1 location in either case.

Can the device be used on a marine terminal?

The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 areas, which include many jetty and loading locations. The terminal’s classification decides each location.

Why are gas and fuel assets treated separately from refineries?

Because the working pattern differs: fewer people, longer distances and more lone working, against the crowded units and shutdown surges of a refinery. A handset policy that suits one does not automatically suit the other.

Is it approved for any Polish gas or fuel site?

No. Certification is in progress, SAMS has no pilot or order at any Polish gas or fuel site, and each operator decides for itself.
