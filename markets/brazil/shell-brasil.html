Why it fits

Built for a European IOC operating Brazil's most demanding pre-salt fields

As a foreign operator in Brazil's pre-salt basin, Shell's engineering review can reference both its own global ATEX-influenced standards and the specific technical demands of ultra-deepwater, high-CO2 pre-salt production.

ATEX and IECEx as the dual reference standard

As a European multinational, Shell's engineering teams generally reference both ATEX and IECEx, and the EX-04Z1's certification programme is being run to produce documentation recognisable against both frameworks.

High-CO2 pre-salt gas composition

Bacalhau and other Santos Basin pre-salt fields produce gas with an unusually high carbon dioxide content, requiring CO2 handling equipment that adds to the standard offshore Zone 1 classified-area footprint.

Some of the deepest water production anywhere in Shell's global portfolio

Shell's pre-salt operations in Brazil rank among its most technically demanding globally in terms of water depth, meaning documentation prepared for this site carries particular relevance for how Shell approaches similarly demanding deepwater fields elsewhere.

A partnership context alongside Petrobras

Shell's Santos Basin interests often involve partnership or joint operating arrangements with Petrobras or other companies, meaning equipment decisions can need to satisfy more than one partner's engineering expectations.

Fleet management across offshore pre-salt operations

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Shell managing devices consistently across its Santos Basin pre-salt operations from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Shell Brasil sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification, including both ATEX and IECEx, is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the high CO2 content of Bacalhau's gas change equipment requirements?

It adds dedicated CO2 handling process equipment, but the general Zone 1 hydrocarbon gas classification still applies to the broader FPSO deck; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued.

Does Shell require ATEX certification specifically for its Brazilian operations?

Shell's engineering review is more likely to reference ATEX given its European base, so the certification programme is targeting both ATEX and IECEx rather than assuming one alone is sufficient.

Is Shell Brasil the same as Petrobras?

No, Shell Brasil is a separate operator, though it may hold joint interests or partnerships with Petrobras in specific Santos Basin fields; each runs its own procurement process.
