Why it fits

Designed for a combined bagasse-dust and ethanol-vapour hazard

Sugarcane ethanol production is a genuinely different process from oil, gas or mining, and Raízen's Shell-Cosan ownership brings both an international energy major's engineering culture and deep Brazilian agribusiness experience to how it evaluates equipment.

Bagasse dust and ethanol vapour together, not separately

Sugarcane bagasse handling generates combustible organic dust, while ethanol distillation and storage produce flammable vapour, and Raízen's mills need equipment addressing both hazard types simultaneously rather than a single-hazard-type device.

A joint venture bringing two distinct corporate cultures

Shell's international energy engineering standards and Cosan's deep Brazilian agribusiness and biofuels experience both inform Raízen's equipment evaluation, a combination distinct from either a pure oil major or a pure domestic agribusiness operator.

An intense seasonal harvest window

Brazil's sugarcane harvest (zafra) drives Raízen's mills into concentrated peak production periods each year, meaning equipment use and wear are concentrated into defined seasonal windows rather than spread evenly across the year.

A biofuels business with genuine scale

Raízen operates one of the largest sugarcane ethanol businesses in the world, meaning device fleet decisions touch a significant number of mill sites and a large seasonal workforce.

Fleet management across a seasonal, multi-mill operation

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Raízen managing devices consistently across multiple mill sites, with particular value during peak harvest season staffing surges.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Raízen mills yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device need separate certification for dust and vapour hazards?

The design intent is a combined rating covering both, though the certificate will state its exact scope for each hazard type once issued.

Does the device suit the seasonal intensity of harvest operations?

Yes, the same device specification applies year-round; seasonal harvest intensity affects usage volume rather than the certification requirement itself.

Is Raízen's hazard profile the same as a standard oil and gas site?

No, the combination of organic combustible dust and ethanol vapour is distinct from the hydrocarbon gas hazards covered on this site's oil and gas pages.
