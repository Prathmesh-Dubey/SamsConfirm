Why it fits mining, terminals and biofuels

Built for combustible dust and biofuel vapour hazards side by side

Iron ore, bulk liquid storage and sugarcane ethanol production each bring their own specific hazard chemistry, distinct from Brazil's oil and gas sector, but grouped together as Brazil's other major bulk-materials industries.

Zone 21/22 dust rating at ore handling and export terminals

Iron ore crushing, screening and bulk export handling generate combustible dust, and the EX-04Z1's Zone 21 / Zone 22 target addresses this classification at mining and terminal operations.

Zone 1 gas at bulk liquid tank farms

Independent bulk liquid terminals handle a range of flammable liquids and gases for third-party customers, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of that mixed cargo profile.

A distinctive combined dust-and-vapour hazard at ethanol mills

Sugarcane processing generates bagasse, a combustible organic dust, alongside ethanol production's flammable vapour, meaning mills need equipment addressing both hazard types at the same site, a combination distinct from a pure oil, gas or dust-only operation.

Seasonal harvest-driven operation at sugar and ethanol mills

Brazil's sugarcane harvest (zafra) drives a strongly seasonal production pattern at ethanol mills, concentrating peak equipment use into defined periods of the year rather than flat year-round operation.

Fleet management across dispersed dust, liquid and biofuel sites

EMM and MDM enrolment supports operators managing devices across genuinely different site types within this broad industry grouping.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use in this sector in Brazil yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and the IECEx certification programme is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does one device cover both iron ore dust and ethanol mill hazards?

The design intent is a combined Zone 1 gas and Zone 21/22 dust rating suited to sites where either or both hazard types are present, though each site's specific classification should be confirmed.

Does the device suit seasonal ethanol mill operation?

Yes, the same device specification applies year-round; seasonal harvest intensity affects usage volume rather than the certification requirement itself.

Is Vale's iron ore hazard the same as a Gulf oil and gas site's gas hazard?

No, iron ore mining and handling primarily involves combustible dust classification, distinct from the flammable gas hazards covered on this site's oil and gas pages.
