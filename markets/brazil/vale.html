Explosion proof mobile phone for Vale, Brazil

An explosion proof mobile phone for Vale in Brazil is not mainly about iron ore dust. Iron ore itself is not a combustible dust; the classified areas at Vale's mines, pellet plants and ports come from fuel, coal and other combustible materials, workshops and process plant. This page is for the safety, maintenance and telecoms teams who set what may be carried across those boundaries from the mine face to the ship loader.

The sites · Vale

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed on Vale's Brazilian sites

Vale runs two large iron ore systems in Brazil. The Northern System is built around Carajás in Pará, in the Amazon region, including the S11D complex at Canaã dos Carajás, which ships through the Carajás railway to the Ponta da Madeira terminal at São Luís, Maranhão. The Southeastern System is in Minas Gerais, with mines such as Brucutu, and ships through the Vitória railway to the port complex at Tubarão in Espírito Santo. Most of that estate is open mining and conveyors. Classification applies to the parts of it that handle fuel, coal or flammable materials.

What Carajás and the south do to a handset

Carajás is in a rainforest region and Minas Gerais has a distinct dry and wet season. Neither is a desert, and that changes what fails first.

Rain, humidity and condensation

Heavy seasonal rainfall and sustained humidity attack seals, ports and connectors. IP68 sealing, tested for dust and immersion, is a more useful starting point than a high temperature rating.

Distances measured in kilometres of conveyor

S11D is designed with in-pit crushing and conveyors in place of a truck fleet, and processing plants are large. Four-constellation GNSS puts a position on a defect report instead of a landmark.

Gloves, vibration and drops

Mine and plant maintenance is rough on a phone. The handset is built to the rugged build, and a wrist or lanyard strap is standard practice on a site like this.

Mining rules and plant classification together

Brazilian mining is governed by NR-22, and plants add NR-10 and NR-20 duties. A device that carries the permit and the inspection record helps, but classification of each area is for the site to set.

Contractor crews and shutdowns

Pellet plants and ports are maintained with contractor crews during planned stops. Android Enterprise enrolment makes it possible to issue a managed handset to a crew and recover it afterwards.

Questions · Vale

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Vale sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx, ATEX and the Brazilian INMETRO certification are in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is iron ore dust explosive?

Iron ore itself is an oxide and is not a combustible dust. The relevant hazards at a mining and pelletising site come from fuels, coal or other solid fuels, and charging rooms. Confirm each area's classification with the site's safety team.

Does the humidity of Carajás affect the handset?

It affects seals and connectors more than most sites. IP68 sealing is the design basis, and the usual advice is to keep ports closed and dry the device after heavy rain.

Would approval at one Vale site cover another?

Vale applies company standards, but each site has its own classification and local sign-off. Ask for the site-specific position.
