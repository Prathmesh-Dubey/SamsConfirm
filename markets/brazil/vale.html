Why it fits

Designed for tropical, high-humidity iron ore operations

Unlike the arid desert mining environments covered elsewhere on this site, Vale's major Brazilian operations run in a genuinely different climate, with humidity and rainfall shaping equipment durability requirements as much as the underlying dust hazard does.

Zone 21/22 dust rating at crushing and processing plants

Iron ore crushing, screening and processing plants generate combustible dust, and the EX-04Z1's Zone 21 / Zone 22 target addresses this classification across Vale's Brazilian operations.

Tropical rainforest conditions unlike arid desert mining

Carajás sits in the Amazon region, where sustained high humidity and heavy seasonal rainfall create a different environmental durability challenge from the arid, dry desert conditions typical of comparable iron ore operations elsewhere, testing seals and electronics differently than dust and dry heat alone.

A safety-first operating culture shaped by past incidents

Vale has invested heavily in strengthened safety monitoring and operational rigour across its Brazilian mining operations in recent years, a context that shapes how new equipment is evaluated for reliability and fail-safe behaviour.

Integrated mine-to-port operations

Vale operates its own processing plants and dedicated export terminals in addition to mining, meaning a single device standard needs to work consistently from the mine face through to bulk loading at port.

Fleet management across a tropical, multi-site operation

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Vale managing devices consistently across its Brazilian mining, processing and export operations from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Vale sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and the IECEx dust-rating scope is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit tropical humidity and rainfall better than arid-region devices?

Environmental testing addresses a range of conditions including high humidity and sustained rainfall exposure, relevant to Vale's tropical operating regions.

Does approval at one Vale site carry across the company?

Vale's own engineering standard is generally applied company-wide, though each site's specific environmental and operational conditions should be confirmed.

Is Vale's iron ore dust hazard the same as coal or mineral dust elsewhere?

Iron ore dust falls under a similar general combustible-dust classification, though the certificate will state its exact scope once issued for the specific material.
