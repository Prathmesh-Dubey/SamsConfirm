Why it fits

Designed for Brazil's fully integrated national operator

Petrobras's scale across offshore production, refining and pipeline transmission means its equipment standardisation decisions touch a wider range of operating environments than a single-segment operator.

Zone 1 gas across offshore, refining and pipeline operations

FPSO process decks, refinery process units and pipeline compressor and metering stations are all classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups common across Petrobras's integrated business.

Deep technical expertise in ultra-deepwater pre-salt production

Petrobras has developed some of the world's most advanced deepwater production technology to produce pre-salt reservoirs beneath thousands of metres of water and salt, expertise that shapes how its engineering teams evaluate new equipment for the harshest parts of its portfolio.

A nationwide refining and pipeline network beyond offshore production

Beyond pre-salt fields, Petrobras operates the majority of Brazil's refining capacity and, through Transpetro, its pipeline and coastal tanker logistics, a combined onshore-offshore footprint few other national operators match.

A long operating history with deep local supplier relationships

Decades as Brazil's dominant national oil company have given Petrobras extensive local supplier and community relationships across every region it operates in, relationships a new vendor needs to work within.

Fleet management across a fully integrated national operator

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Petrobras managing devices consistently across offshore, refining and pipeline operations from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Petrobras sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does Petrobras run one unified vendor-approval process?

Petrobras's scale generally means engineering review varies somewhat between its offshore, refining and pipeline business units, even under a common corporate framework.

Does the device suit Petrobras's pre-salt operations specifically?

Yes, the Zone 1 specification is designed to cover FPSO process deck equipment relevant to pre-salt production, alongside Petrobras's refining and pipeline operations.

Is Petrobras the same as Acelen or Braskem?

No, Acelen independently operates the Mataripe refinery and Braskem is a separate petrochemical producer; both run their own procurement processes distinct from Petrobras.
