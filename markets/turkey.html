Who it is for · Turkey

Who it is for

In Turkey the decision usually starts with the İSG team rather than with engineering, because law 6331 puts the duty squarely on the employer and the employer knows it. Around them sit the maintenance side, the supplier registration side and the technical buyer, and a device that satisfies one of them and not the others goes nowhere.

01

İSG uzmanları and workplace safety specialists

You produce and maintain the patlamadan korunma dokümanı, which records the classified areas, the ignition source assessment and the equipment chosen for each zone. That document is inspected, so what you need from a supplier is the certificate reference, the equipment protection level and the complete marking string, in Turkish, not a description of what the device is good at.

02

Bakım and turnaround engineers

A refinery or petrochemical turnaround here runs with large contractor crews and a schedule that will not move. Equipment has to be enrolled quickly, work outdoors through a summer that is genuinely hot on the Aegean and Mediterranean coasts, and be handed back at the end without a dispute.

03

Supplier registration and vendor qualification teams

Large Turkish operators bring suppliers in through their own registration portals and qualification steps before a technical discussion is even scheduled. TÜPRAŞ, for example, takes initial supplier applications through its own supplier portal. Knowing which gate has to be passed first saves a quarter.

04

Procurement and technical buyers

A Turkish tender is written to be defended. You will want the Turkish-language declaration of conformity, the reference to the 2014/34/AB regulation and not only to the European directive, evidence of BTK type approval for the radio, and a named service route inside the country.

Where it works · Turkey

Where it works in Türkiye

Turkish hazardous area work follows the coastline and then turns inland. Aliağa, north of İzmir, holds the largest single concentration, with refining and petrochemical production side by side and an LNG terminal on the same stretch of shore. The Marmara holds the Körfez refinery and the chemical estates of Kocaeli, along with the Marmara Ereğlisi gas terminal. Ceyhan on the Mediterranean is the export end of the crude pipelines and a growing energy hub. Inland, Kırıkkale serves the central Anatolian market and Batman serves the south-east, close to the oilfields. Add fertiliser plants, fuel terminals and the organised industrial zones and you have most of the classified estate in the country.

Coastal summer heat and the temperature class

A tank farm at Aliağa or Ceyhan in August is the hardest thermal test this device will meet in the region, and it is a certification question rather than a comfort one. The T4 target and the certified ambient range are published with the datasheet rather than promised in advance, because a device that derates in the heat gets left in the control room.

Salt air, dust and wash-down together

Most Turkish classified sites are coastal, and the ones that are not sit in dry inland air that moves fine dust everywhere. IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing are aimed at spray, salt, dust ingress and routine cleaning rather than at office handling.

Gas and dust under one programme

The gas marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and Zone 21 dust protection sits inside the same certification programme. Fertiliser plants, grain and bulk terminals and powder handling in the industrial zones put a dust area next to a gas area often enough that one device is worth insisting on.

Turkish on the screen and in the file

Android 16 carries a Turkish interface, and the manual, safe-use instructions and declaration of conformity are produced in Turkish alongside English. In Turkey this is not a courtesy: instructions have to reach the worker in Turkish, and an İSG specialist will not accept an English-only file into the patlamadan korunma dokümanı.

A shift that does not pass a charger

There is no charging point inside a classified area, and at a pipeline station or a jetty the nearest one is a gate away. The 4,000 mAh cell is sized for a full round with the camera and a work order application in use.

Contractor crews through one gate

4G LTE, Wi-Fi and NFC let one handset work on the plant network, on site Wi-Fi and against access control, and Android 16 with EMM and MDM allows enrolment, restriction, remote wipe and reissue as crews rotate through a turnaround.

Regulatory route · Turkey

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Turkey is not an EU member state, and that is the single most important thing to get straight before a specification is written, because it changes what you cite rather than what you buy. ATEX 2014/34/EU was transposed into Turkish law as the Muhtemel Patlayıcı Ortamda Kullanılan Teçhizat ve Koruyucu Sistemler ile İlgili Yönetmelik (2014/34/AB), published in the Resmî Gazete on 30 June 2016, number 29758. It sets the same essential health and safety requirements, the same equipment groups and categories and the same conformity assessment procedures as the European directive, and it uses CE as the conformity marking. In practice a device certified by a notified body to ATEX, marked CE and supported by a declaration of conformity satisfies the Turkish product requirement, provided the declaration addresses the Turkish regulation as well as the directive and the instructions are supplied in Turkish. The harmonised standards behind the certificate are adopted nationally as the TS EN 60079 series, so a Turkish reviewer will recognise the standard numbers on the certificate immediately. Two further steps are Turkish rather than European. Radio equipment requires type approval through BTK, the Information and Communication Technologies Authority, which is a separate process from the explosion protection certificate and is arranged with the in-country partner. Turkey also operates a device registration regime for mobile handsets brought into the country, which is an import and logistics matter rather than a safety one, and it is handled the same way.

The duty side is where a Turkish purchase is actually decided, and it is unusually explicit. Law 6331 on occupational health and safety makes the employer responsible for risk assessment and for the measures that follow from it, and the Çalışanların Patlayıcı Ortamların Tehlikelerinden Korunması Hakkında Yönetmelik, published in the Resmî Gazete on 30 April 2013, number 28633, applies that to explosive atmospheres. Under it the employer classifies the hazardous areas, assesses the ignition sources and prepares the patlamadan korunma dokümanı, the explosion protection document, which has to be kept current and produced when the labour inspectorate asks for it. The workplace İSG specialist and the workplace physician structure that law created are permanent roles, not consultants brought in for an audit, which is why the İSG team rather than engineering usually opens a conversation about handheld equipment. Major hazard establishments carry a further layer under the Seveso-equivalent regulation on major industrial accidents, so at a refinery or a large chemical site the same equipment file can be read for more than one purpose. None of this changes what the device has to be. It changes what has to be written down about it, in Turkish, before it goes through a gate.

Questions · Turkey

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 already certified for use in Turkey?

No. It is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the certification programme is in progress, with launch planned for Q4 2026. Nothing is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering that use exists. We say this plainly because a Turkish İSG file that rests on an assumption is the file that fails at the first inspection.

Turkey is not in the EU. Does ATEX apply here at all?

It applies, under a Turkish number. The Muhtemel Patlayıcı Ortamda Kullanılan Teçhizat ve Koruyucu Sistemler ile İlgili Yönetmelik (2014/34/AB), published in the Resmî Gazete on 30 June 2016, number 29758, carries the same essential requirements, groups, categories and conformity assessment procedures as the European directive and uses CE as the conformity marking. The harmonised standards are adopted nationally as the TS EN 60079 series.

What is the price of an explosion proof mobile phone in Turkey?

There is no list price before launch, and we will not publish one while the certificate is outstanding. Pre-launch pricing is quoted in writing against a stated quantity, configuration and support scope, in the currency the contract uses, so a tender team can cite it. Evaluation units are placed under a trial agreement rather than sold.

Do we need anything beyond the ATEX certificate to bring it in?

Two things, and both are Turkish rather than European. Radio equipment requires type approval through BTK, and mobile handsets brought into the country fall under the device registration regime. Neither is a safety approval and neither changes the explosion protection evidence, but both take time, so they are arranged with the in-country partner rather than discovered at the border.

Will the documentation be in Turkish?

Yes. The manual, the safe-use instructions and the declaration of conformity are produced in Turkish alongside English, and Android 16 carries a Turkish interface. The declaration references the Turkish yönetmelik as well as the European directive, because an İSG specialist quoting a document into the patlamadan korunma dokümanı needs the national instrument named.

What exactly does our patlamadan korunma dokümanı need from a supplier?

The certificate reference, the equipment protection level, the full marking string, the gas group and the temperature class, in a form that can be quoted directly. The regulation published on 30 April 2013 makes that document the employer's responsibility under law 6331, and the labour inspectorate reads it, so the equipment selection reasoning has to rest on cited evidence rather than on a supplier description.

How does it handle Aegean and Mediterranean summer heat?

Ambient temperature is fixed by the certificate rather than by marketing. The device is designed to IP68 and tested to MIL-STD-810H, which includes high temperature exposure, and the certified range is published with the datasheet when the certificate is issued. If your classification drawings set a demanding surface temperature limit, send them and we will answer against the number.

How will repair and battery replacement work in Türkiye?

Through a recognised facility arranged with the Turkish partner, never on site and never in a general phone workshop, because opening an intrinsically safe device destroys the protection concept the certificate rests on. The service and battery route is defined as part of the launch programme and can be reviewed under a pre-launch agreement before it is published.
