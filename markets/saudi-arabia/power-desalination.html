The site · Power and desalination

Explosion proof mobile phone for power and desalination plants

The main plants are Ras Al Khair, which combines multistage flash and reverse osmosis desalination with power generation and supplies the Ma'aden complex; the Jubail and Yanbu utilities run through Marafiq; Shoaiba and Shuqaiq on the Red Sea coast. Most of any plant of this type is safe area: the turbine hall, the evaporators, the membrane rooms and the control buildings. The classified areas are pockets. Fuel gas conditioning and metering skids are the clearest, with natural gas in group IIA. Hydrogen is the one that surprises people. Large generators are often cooled with hydrogen, and electrochlorination plants that make hypochlorite from seawater release hydrogen as a by-product, which is a group IIC hazard. Chlorine itself is a toxic gas rather than a flammable one, so it is handled through gas detection and entry rules rather than the Ex marking.

What a coastal utility asks of a handset

A utility plant is very different from a refinery. Most of it is safe area, but the handset has to cope with seawater, heat and a workforce that moves between classified and unclassified areas many times a day.

Small classified pockets in a large plant

Operators carry a phone everywhere, and the chance of walking into a pocket without noticing is real. A handset that is rated for Zone 1 gas removes the need to remember where the boundary is.

Hydrogen is a group IIC question

The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, which includes hydrogen. The certificate will state the exact scope once issued, and the temperature class matters for hot piping near the fuel skids.

Salt spray at intake and outfall

Seawater intake structures and brine outfalls are wet, salty and exposed. IP68 sealing is specified with that in mind, and rinse-and-dry practice is worth agreeing with the operator.

Operators from several companies

Power and water trains on one site may be run by different companies under different offtake agreements. An enrolled handset can carry a separate profile for each operating team.

Long outages on planned schedules

Turbine overhauls and membrane replacements bring specialist contractors for weeks at a time. A handset that is enrolled before arrival carries the permit application and nothing else.

Frequently asked questions

Does chlorine make an area hazardous in the Ex sense?

Not by itself. Chlorine is toxic, not flammable, so it is managed with gas detection and entry rules. The hydrogen produced in electrochlorination is what drives the classification in those areas.

Is most of a desalination plant classified?

No. Most is safe area. A hazardous-area assessment will mark specific pockets such as fuel gas skids and hydrogen systems, and those are where a certified handset is needed.

Can one handset serve both power and water operators?

That is the intent of enrolment in an MDM console. Each operating team can apply its own profile while using the same hardware.

Has any utility approved the EX-04Z1?

No. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress ahead of Q4 2026, and no approval can exist before the certificate does.
