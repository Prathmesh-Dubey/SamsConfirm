Why it fits

Documentation built for a Saudi-Chinese joint venture

Sinopec's domestic certification system, administered through China's NEPSI scheme, differs in format from IECEx even where the underlying IEC test basis overlaps, and a Sinopec-linked engineering review may expect to see that cross-reference explicitly rather than assume it.

An IECEx basis that maps to NEPSI expectations

China's NEPSI certification and IECEx both derive from IEC standards, and the EX-04Z1's technical file is being prepared so the cross-reference between the two is explicit, rather than leaving a Sinopec-side reviewer to infer it.

Yanbu's Red Sea coastal environment

Unlike the Gulf-facing Eastern Province sites, Yanbu sits on the Red Sea with its own heat, humidity and salt-air exposure profile, and IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing are specified with that coastal duty cycle in mind.

A full-conversion refinery built around residue upgrading

YASREF's refinery configuration is built to convert a high proportion of heavy Arabian crude into higher-value products, meaning a larger footprint of high-severity processing units per barrel of crude than a simpler topping refinery.

A single foreign joint-venture partner rather than a consortium

With Sinopec as sole minority partner, vendor decisions run through one clear counterpart rather than a multi-party consortium, which can simplify the sign-off path once both sides recognise the certification basis.

Fleet management for a single, large integrated site

EMM and MDM enrolment suits a site the scale of YASREF, particularly during turnaround periods with a large temporary contractor population.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at YASREF yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification, including IECEx and SASO, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does Sinopec's involvement mean a different certification is needed?

Sinopec's home-market NEPSI system and IECEx share an IEC basis; the documentation is being prepared to make that cross-reference explicit rather than requiring a wholly separate Chinese certification for a Saudi-based site.

Is YASREF's environment different from a Jubail refinery?

Yes — Yanbu's Red Sea coastal exposure differs from the Eastern Province's Gulf-facing climate, which is reflected in the sealing and environmental testing specified for the device.

Does approval at a Sinopec site elsewhere carry over to YASREF?

No. YASREF is a distinct Saudi joint-venture entity with its own site-level engineering review, separate from Sinopec's operations in other countries.
