Why it fits

Built to satisfy two parent companies, not one

A 50/50 joint venture like SATORP does not simply inherit one shareholder's standards; its own engineering function typically draws on both, which raises the documentation bar above what either parent asks for alone.

Two certification traditions, one certificate

TotalEnergies' European operations lean on ATEX and IECEx, while Aramco's domestic process leans on IECEx alongside SASO/SABER. The EX-04Z1's IECEx Certificate of Conformity is being built as the common technical basis both sides can work from.

A full-conversion refinery plus petrochemicals on one site

SATORP integrates a high-conversion refinery with downstream petrochemical units at Jubail, so the same handset needs to move between crude processing, cracking and utility areas within one site boundary.

Joint-venture procurement can move slower than a single-owner site

Vendor decisions at a JV often need sign-off aligned across both shareholders' engineering functions, which SAMS is accounting for by preparing documentation to both parents' typical evidence standards up front rather than after a first rejection.

Jubail's shared industrial-city context

SATORP sits inside the Jubail industrial city alongside SABIC affiliates and other petrochemical operators, so contractors and inspectors carrying a device onto the SATORP site may also carry it onto a neighbouring plant the same day.

Fleet management for a single large integrated site

EMM and MDM enrolment is suited to a site of SATORP's scale, where turnaround periods bring a large temporary contractor population through one location.

Frequently asked questions

Does SATORP follow Aramco's standards or TotalEnergies'?

Its engineering review typically draws on both shareholders' practices rather than one exclusively, which is why documentation is being prepared to satisfy either evidence standard rather than assuming one is sufficient.

Is the EX-04Z1 approved for use at SATORP yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and no vendor approval exists ahead of the underlying IECEx and SASO certification being completed.

Does a device accepted at SATORP automatically work at other Jubail plants?

No. SATORP is a separate legal entity from neighbouring SABIC affiliates and other operators in the same industrial city, each running its own procurement process even where sites are adjacent.

Does the device cover both refining and petrochemical areas at SATORP?

The Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 target is intended to cover both the refinery's crude-processing units and the downstream petrochemical units on the same integrated site.
