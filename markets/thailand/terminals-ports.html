Built for terminals & ports conditions

Thailand's terminals & ports sector has its own mix of hazard, headcount and site rules.

Terminals & Ports zone conditions

Sites in this sector combine gas and dust risk in the same footprint: Bulk liquid storage, chemical jetties and the deep sea berths at Laem Chabang and Map Ta Phut. The Smart Ex-04 is built to Zone 1 / Zone 21, so one certified handset covers both without a second device.

Working across Thailand's site fences

Contractors and rotating crews move between operators in this sector. A single device with a portable, exportable Ex certificate travels with the person, not just the plant.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Smart Ex-04 certified for terminals & ports sites in Thailand today?

Not yet. The device is pre-launch: ATEX/IECEx-class certification is in progress and the launch is planned for Q4 2026. Teams can review documentation and request an evaluation unit now.

Does one device cover both Zone 1 gas and Zone 21 dust areas?

Yes. The Smart Ex-04 is designed to Zone 1 / Zone 21, the combination most sites in this sector actually need, so staff are not issued two separate handsets for gas and dust areas.
