Thailand · PTT Global Chemical

Explosion proof mobile phone for PTT Global Chemical in Thailand

PTT Global Chemical, usually written GC, runs its main Thai complex at Map Ta Phut in Rayong, and an explosion proof mobile phone for Thailand's petrochemical sector has to cope with ethylene, hydrogen and aromatics in the same plot. This page is for GC's operations, reliability and HSE teams and for the contractors who join them during shutdowns. The SAMS EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, and it is pre-launch with certification in progress.

PTT Global Chemical · the site

Inside the GC complex at Map Ta Phut

GC was formed in 2011 by the amalgamation of PTT Aromatics and Refining with PTT Chemical, and the Map Ta Phut complex is described in industry data as Thailand's largest chemical and petrochemical site, operating since 1991. Reported capacities include roughly 3.7 million tonnes a year of ethylene and propylene across the Olefins 1 and 2 units on naphtha and LPG feed, and about 2.2 million tonnes a year of BTX across two aromatics complexes, plus a propylene oxide plant. Treat these as indicative figures from industry databases, not GC's own statement. What a reader needs from them is the layout: steam crackers, quench and compression with ethylene and hydrogen, aromatics reformers and extraction, and derivative plants all within one fenced estate administered by the Industrial Estate Authority of Thailand.

Ethylene and hydrogen mean group IIC

Cracker gas is ordinary at GC, so a handset assessed only to IIB or IIA is the wrong equipment. The EX-04Z1 target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, which an instrumentation engineer would check first.

Salt, heat and monsoon rain

Rayong stays warm and humid all year and takes the southwest monsoon from May. Salt air off the Gulf works on connectors and speaker meshes, so IP68 sealing and a rugged build are the design floor.

Dust next to vapour

Where GC handles solid product, Zone 21 sits next to Zone 1, often within one route. One design covering both avoids issuing two devices to one operator.

Thai and English on one build

Operators work in Thai; engineering and contractor layers often work in English. Both languages sit on one Android 16 build, so a mixed crew gets one image and one manual.

PTT Global Chemical · working and approvals

How work is organised at GC, and what its teams would ask for

Two gates control access at Map Ta Phut: the operator's own, and the estate's.

Shutdowns overlap on the estate

Cracker and aromatics turnarounds bring large contractor crews who work to a fixed pass window. A permit app, plant directory and camera on one managed unit reduces what is signed out at the gate and reconciled at the end of the shift.

Rounds across a wide plot

Operators and inspectors cover distances between process areas and the safe-area control building. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a full shift with the camera and GNSS running, so a drive back to charge does not clear the gate twice.

Vendor and equipment approval

We have not verified a published GC approval programme for portable devices, so we do not describe one. Ask GC procurement and HSE for the equipment approval steps and the documents expected with an Ex certificate.

National file

Thai practice references TIS standards under TISI, with the Department of Industrial Works on factory safety and NBTC on radio equipment. All are in progress, and GC would still review the device itself.

Frequently asked questions

Can GC buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS today?

No. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch, and IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certification are in progress. The datasheet and certification schedule are available now.

Are the capacities quoted on this page GC figures?

No. They come from industry databases and trade press describing the Map Ta Phut complex, and GC's own published figures should be used for any engineering purpose.

Why group IIC for a petrochemical site?

Hydrogen and acetylene are group IIC, and a cracker complex uses both in places. A device marked to a lower group cannot be taken into those areas, so the target is the strictest group.
