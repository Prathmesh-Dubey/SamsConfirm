Bahrain · Oil & Gas

Explosion proof mobile phone for oil and gas in Bahrain

Bapco's Sitra refinery, Banagas' gas processing plant and Tatweer Petroleum's onshore field development sit within a few kilometres of each other on Bahrain's eastern coast. The EX-04Z1 is designed for the Zone 1 gas atmospheres common to all three, for operators and inspection engineers who move between them in a single shift.

Why it fits oil and gas

Built for Bahrain's refining and gas-processing corridor

Sitra concentrates refining, gas processing and product export in one compact classified-area footprint, unlike the more spread-out fields of larger Gulf states.

Zone 1 gas across a tight footprint

Sitra's refinery units, tank farm, product jetties and the adjoining Banagas gas plant are all Zone 1. Ex ib IIC T4 intrinsic safety is the marking target across the whole complex.

Coastal salt air on reclaimed land

Sitra sits on reclaimed coastal ground, and salt-laden air works on connectors and seams over years of service. MIL-STD-810H construction is specified with that exposure in mind.

A device for a compact but continuous operation

Bahrain's refining and gas-processing sites run short walking distances but long, continuous shifts. IP68 sealing and a 4,000 mAh cell are sized for a full shift with no recharge point inside the classified area.

Fits Tatweer's onshore field work too

Enhanced oil recovery operations at the Bahrain Field carry the same Zone 1 wellhead and gathering-centre classification as Sitra, so one specification covers both upstream and downstream work.

Questions · Oil & Gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for Bahrain oil and gas use yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and IECEx, ATEX and the BSMD/GSO conformity route are all still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does it cover both the Sitra refinery and the onshore field?

Yes. Sitra's refining and gas-processing units and the Bahrain Field's wellheads and gathering centres share the same Zone 1 gas classification, so one Ex ib IIC T4 specification covers both.

Will Bapco accept it as an approved device?

That follows Bapco's own approved-vendor and engineering-evaluation process, built on the IECEx certificate once issued, and normally supplied through a Bahraini-registered partner.
