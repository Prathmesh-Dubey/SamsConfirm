The sector · Oil and gas

Explosion proof mobile phone for oil and gas across Bahrain

Oil was found at the Bahrain Field in 1932. Today crude from the field and, by pipeline, from Saudi Arabia reaches the Sitra refinery, where the modernisation programme has added new units. Associated gas from the field goes to the Banagas plants, and refinery off-gas goes the same way for LPG recovery. Trade press attributes the group structure to Bapco Energies, with the refining, upstream and gas businesses listed as separate companies.

The hazards change as the product moves. Wellheads and gathering lines carry crude and associated gas that can contain hydrogen sulphide; gas plants and LPG storage deal in propane and butane; the refinery adds hydrogen and sulphur; the jetties add marine conditions. A single handset that stays safe and usable along that chain is the point of the Zone 1 design target.

How work runs · Oil and gas

What a chain of this shape asks of one handset

Distances are short but sites are many

Awali and Sitra are on the same island, a short drive apart. A supervisor or inspection engineer can visit several sites in a shift, so the same managed handset should make sense at each.

Different operators, different registrations

Each operating company has its own safety and procurement process. A device accepted at the refinery has to be raised again at a gas plant or a field operator, and the certificate is the common document across all of them.

Gas groups change along the chain

Hydrogen sits at the refinery, propane and butane at the gas plants, and crude hydrocarbons at the wellsites. The target marking, Ex ib IIC T4, is written to cover the demanding end of that range.

Turnarounds and project work set the contractor load

Refinery shutdowns and plant expansions bring large, temporary contractor crews through the gates. Enrolment and removal of devices at that scale needs a console, not a spreadsheet.

Approvals · Oil and gas

How approvals work across several operators

The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch (Q4 2026) and no certificate has been issued; IECEx, ATEX, NEC 500 and BSMD work is in progress. SAMS has no customers, orders or relationships with any operator named on this page.

Bapco Energies runs a Supplier Portal for registration and qualification and says adherence to its Supplier Code of Conduct is mandatory; public tenders are also published on the Bahrain Tender Board. For the other operators, ask their procurement and HSE teams what they require. Expect each to ask for the certificate and its limits, the marking against its own area classification drawings, the gas group and temperature class, accessory rules, camera policy and the repair route. The national route (BSMD, GSO and TRA) is on the Bahrain country page.

Questions · Oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for oil and gas use in Bahrain yet?

No. It is pre-launch, and IECEx, ATEX, NEC 500 and BSMD work is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Will one approval cover every operator?

No. Each operator reviews equipment for its own areas. The certificate is the shared evidence; the approval decisions are separate.

Does a phone replace a gas detector at a wellsite?

No. Associated gas can carry hydrogen sulphide, and gas detection is a separate certified safety system. The handset must not be an ignition source and should carry the call when an alarm sounds.

Does the same handset work in the control room and the plant?

Yes. It is an ordinary Android Enterprise device in the safe area and is designed for Zone 1 when carried out, so the console and apps are the same either side of the boundary.
