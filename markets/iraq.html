Who it is for · Iraq

Who an ex proof phone is for in Iraq

Iraqi operations are run by a mix of state companies, international operating organisations and their contractors, and each buys differently. What they share is a hard constraint: replacing a failed device is slow, so equipment is chosen for endurance first.

01

Field operators and shift supervisors

A degassing station round covers ground, and radio coverage is not uniform across it. One device that records the reading, takes the photograph and places the call is worth more here than in a compact plant.

02

HSE and technical safety leads

International operating organisations write their standards to IEC 60079 or the American equivalent, and expect the marking string and equipment protection level on file before portable equipment enters a classified area.

03

Maintenance, inspection and turnaround crews

Ageing surface facilities mean heavy inspection workload. A 50 MP camera with phase-detect autofocus records corrosion evidence a reliability engineer can assess remotely.

04

Logistics, IT and procurement

Import clearance and site access both take planning. Central enrolment, a locked app set and remote wipe are how a fleet of loaned devices stays accounted for across camps and rotations.

Extreme summer heat in the south

Basra sits at the top of the global temperature range through the summer and open plant holds it. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and the temperature class is the first figure a safety engineer checks.

Sour service as the normal case

Southern Iraqi crude is sour, and hydrogen sulphide is a routine hazard at degassing stations rather than an exception. Bluetooth pairing lets a personal gas detector and the handset work together instead of filling both hands.

Dust storms and fine airborne sand

Dust events cover the south and the centre for days at a time. IP68 sealing keeps a handset serviceable afterwards, and Zone 21 coverage matters where fertiliser and bulk handling sit next to gas.

A replacement is not a short trip

Between import clearance, site access and camp logistics, a failed device stays failed for a while. A 4,000 mAh battery, a sealed body and MIL-STD-810H construction are chosen for exactly that reason.

Fewer items across the gate

Radio, camera, torch and paper folded into one enrolled handset reduces what a crew signs out at the start of a rotation and hands back at the end.

One managed build for a mixed workforce

Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English and enrols into your EMM or MDM platform, so a national crew and an international contractor team can be issued the same image under the same policy.

Regulatory route · Iraq

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

On the import side, Iraq operates a conformity assessment programme run by the Central Organization for Standardization and Quality Control under the Ministry of Planning. Regulated consignments must arrive with a Certificate of Conformity issued by a body COSQC has appointed, and the regulated product list is revised periodically. Radio equipment is a separate approval: the Communications and Media Commission issues type approval through a licensed representative inside Iraq. Beneath both sits the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, the one your Ex authority will actually read. None has been granted.

Site access is decided by whoever runs the asset, and in Iraq that is rarely a single body. The Ministry of Oil and its operating companies each maintain vendor registration and tender prequalification, while the international operating organisations at Rumaila, West Qurna and the gas ventures apply their own contractor HSE standards on top. The Kurdistan Region is a separate exercise again, with its own authorities, operators and import route. Expect to register more than once, and treat certification and registration as two different timetables.

Questions · Iraq

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Iraq today?

No. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch date, and IECEx, ATEX and the Iraqi conformity and type approval steps are all still in progress. We will not put a unit into a classified area before the certificate covering that use has been issued.

What does an explosion proof phone cost in Iraq?

There is no published figure and we will not invent one while certification is running. Iraqi landed cost also depends on the conformity certificate, clearance and who holds the supply. Pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Which zones and gas groups is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust, with an ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued in parallel, which matters because a good deal of Iraqi surface facility work is written to the American code.

Is it suitable for sour gas and hydrogen sulphide areas?

An Ex certificate defines where a device may be carried by zone, gas group and temperature class, not by which gas is present. What matters in practice for sour service is that the handset pairs with a personal gas detector over Bluetooth.

Will it be usable in the Kurdistan Region as well as the south?

That is the intention, but the two run on separate registrations, import routes and operator approvals. If your work spans both, tell us at the enquiry stage so the partner arrangements are set up for both rather than retrofitted to one.

How does it get through Iraqi customs?

Regulated goods enter against a Certificate of Conformity issued by a body COSQC has appointed, and the radio needs CMC type approval held by a licensed local representative. Both are part of the launch programme and the appointed Iraqi partner will hold them.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset and the manual will be issued in both, which is what a mixed national and international crew needs from one build.

How do repairs and battery replacement work in Iraq?

An Ex certified device cannot be opened on site or repaired by a general phone workshop without invalidating its certification. Repair runs through SAMS and the appointed Iraqi partner, with pooled spares held in Basra.
