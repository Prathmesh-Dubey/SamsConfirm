Explosion proof mobile phone in Portugal: refining, polymers and fuels areas

Sines is the only operating refinery in the country. Galp's plant there, rated at around 220,000 barrels a day, is described on explosion proof phone for Galp, and Repsol's polymer complex next door on explosion proof phone for Repsol Polímeros. The older Matosinhos refinery stopped fuel production in October 2020 according to press reports, and the site now serves storage and logistics. Behind both sit fuel depots and port jetties where gasoline, diesel and jet fuel are received, stored and loaded. Hydrocarbon vapour, group IIA and IIB, is the main hazard, with hydrogen in the conversion units and polymer powder in the plastics plants.

What downstream sites do to a handset

Downstream work is spread across a complex and a network. The handset has to cope with large process units as well as small, lightly staffed depots.

Large units, small depots

At a refinery, a round covers kilometres. At a fuel depot, one operator may cover the gantry, tank farm and pump house alone. The same managed handset can carry a lone-worker check-in at both.

Static and loading discipline

Loading gantries are where phones are most tightly controlled. A certified device with a locked-down application set is easier to approve than a general phone with an open app store.

Shutdown calendars

A refinery shutdown lasts weeks, such as the 25-day stop at Sines in October 2022 reported in the trade press. Contractor devices need enrolling quickly, and the console should allow bulk enrolment and clean wiping.

Weather and wash-down

Atlantic coastal weather, jetty spray and routine cleaning are the working conditions, and IP68 sealing is aimed at them. The 50 MP camera produces clear defect photographs for inspection records.

Approvals and access

What refiners and terminal operators will ask for

Refineries and fuel depots are usually Seveso sites, and the operators set their own supplier and contractor approvals. We have verified no named programme, so ask each operator's procurement and HSE teams what documents and site induction they need. The standard product evidence is the ATEX certificate, the marking, gas group, temperature class and a service statement.

The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch. No certificate has been issued, SAMS has no customer relationship with the operators mentioned, and nothing is supplied for use in a classified area until the certificate exists. Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is a fuel depot different from a refinery for phone policy?

A depot is small and often staffed by one or two people, so lone-worker safety and loading rules dominate. A refinery has long routes and many contractors, so enrolment and range dominate.

Is jet fuel a Zone 1 hazard?

Jet fuel has a higher flash point than gasoline, so classified areas around it are often smaller, but loading and tank vapour spaces can still be classified. The operator's area classification governs each case.

Does the Matosinhos site still need Ex equipment?

A storage and distribution site still has classified areas at tanks and loading points. The scale is smaller than at a process plant, but the equipment rule is the same.
