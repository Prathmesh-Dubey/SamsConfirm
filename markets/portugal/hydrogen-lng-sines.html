What a new energy hub does to a handset

New plants mean new classified areas, small commissioning teams and very little local history of which devices work. Group choice matters more than usual.

IIC is the safe choice

A device certified to group IIB would be excluded from a hydrogen area. The EX-04Z1 marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, so that choice does not need revisiting if a methane site becomes a hydrogen site.

Commissioning with small crews

A new electrolyser or terminal extension is commissioned by a handful of specialists from several vendors. A managed handset, enrolled once, gives them the permit, the P and ID drawings and a call list on a single device.

Exposed ground and wind

Sines is an open coastal site. IP68 sealing and four-constellation GNSS are suited to wind, rain and large distances, and the 4,000 mAh battery covers a shift.

Evidence for the file

New plants write their explosion protection documents from scratch. Receiving the marking string, gas group and temperature class early lets engineers record the handset in the first issue.

Ask each site's procurement and HSE teams what documents, induction and testing they require. For the refinery and polymer sites see explosion proof phone for refining, polymers and fuels; the main product documents are the ATEX certificate, the marking, the temperature class and a servicing statement.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why choose group IIC rather than IIB for a hydrogen site?

Hydrogen has a very low minimum ignition energy and is placed in group IIC. Equipment certified for IIB cannot be used in a hydrogen atmosphere, so IIC is the only workable choice.

Does a data centre need an explosion proof mobile phone?

Mostly no. Data halls are not classified in the usual way. The classified spots are fuel stores, some battery rooms and any hydrogen systems, so only those staff would need an Ex device.

Are the Sines hydrogen projects confirmed to start in 2026?

We cannot confirm dates. The statement that the Galp electrolyser should enter operation in the second half of 2026 comes from press reports, so check it with the project owner.
