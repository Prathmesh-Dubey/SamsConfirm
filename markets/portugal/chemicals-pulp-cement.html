Explosion proof mobile phone in Portugal: chemicals, pulp and cement areas

Three industries, three different hazards. At Estarreja, chlor-alkali and organic chemistry bring hydrogen and flammable liquids; see explosion proof phone for Bondalti. Navigator runs pulp and paper mills at Figueira da Foz, Setúbal and Aveiro, with recovery boilers, lime kilns, biomass handling and wood dust. Secil and Cimpor operate cement plants where coal, petcoke and alternative fuels are ground and fed to kilns. Gas areas are the main concern in the chemicals, while dust areas decide the outcome in the mills and cement works.

Built for local conditions

What these plants do to a handset

A device for this sector has to survive airborne fibre, cement dust and wet cleaning while remaining legal in both gas and dust areas.

Dust gets into everything

In a mill or cement plant, fine powder settles on every surface and works into seams. IP68 sealing keeps it out of the housing, and a dust-rated device also means a layer on the surface does not become the ignition problem.

Mixed zones on one site

A pulp mill has a fuel farm, a solvent store and a wood yard within the same fence. One Zone 1 and Zone 21 handset covers the full walk, instead of two devices with two sets of rules.

Long routes and noisy plant

Kiln lines and recovery boilers are loud, tall and spread out. Four-constellation GNSS gives a position, and the 4,000 mAh battery is specified to last a shift without any recharge in the classified area.

Contractors between sites

Shutdown crews move between pulp mills, cement plants and chemical sites. A device whose certificate follows the person lowers the paperwork each time, though each operator still decides separately.

Approvals and access

What buyers in this sector will ask for

Each operator keeps its own contractor and equipment approval process, and we name none of them. The usual documents are the ATEX certificate for the device, the marking with its gas and dust categories, the temperature class, the maximum surface temperature for dust and a statement on servicing. Ask the procurement and HSE team at each site what they need before a new device type is allowed in. Sites handling hydrogen or methane should also look at explosion proof phone for hydrogen and LNG at Sines.

The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and no certificate has been issued, so no unit is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering that use exists.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which matters more at a pulp mill, gas or dust?

Both appear, but the wood yard, bark and biomass fuel and lime handling are classically dust. The gas risk comes from fuel gas, solvents and black liquor vapours, so a Zone 1 and Zone 21 device is the right basis.

Is cement dust itself explosive?

Cement and raw meal are largely non-combustible. The risk comes from the fuel side, such as coal and petcoke, plus waste-derived fuels. The classification is made area by area, and the operator's explosion protection document decides it.

Can one device cover several operators?

The certificate belongs to the device, so it can be listed in more than one operator's file. Each operator still has to accept it, so contact each site separately.
