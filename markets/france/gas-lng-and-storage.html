Why it fits gas, LNG and storage

Built for a three-tier gas infrastructure system

Import, storage and transmission are run as distinct businesses in France, each with its own classified-area equipment needs shaped by a different part of the gas handling process.

Zone 1 gas at LNG terminals and compressor stations

Regasification equipment at Elengy's Montoir-de-Bretagne and Fos terminals and compressor stations on the GRTgaz network are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the natural gas atmospheres typical of this equipment.

ATEX as the governing EU framework

As an EU member state, France's hazardous-area equipment requirements are governed by the ATEX directives across the gas infrastructure sector.

Three separate businesses covering import, storage and transmission

Elengy's terminals, Storengy's underground storage and GRTgaz's transmission network are run as distinct companies, meaning a contractor moving between them needs equipment approved across more than one operator's site rules.

Fleet management across a nationwide gas infrastructure network

EMM and MDM enrolment supports an operator or contractor managing devices consistently across geographically dispersed terminals, storage sites and compressor stations.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use on French gas infrastructure yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Do LNG terminals and underground storage sites carry the same classified-area rating?

Both are gas-rated Zone 1/2 atmospheres from natural gas handling, so the same Ex ib IIC T4 rating applies across the two, even though the equipment layout differs.

Is ATEX the only certification needed for French gas operators?

ATEX is the primary EU regulatory framework; IECEx serves as useful supporting international technical evidence.

Does approval with one of the three gas businesses cover the others?

No, Elengy, Storengy and GRTgaz are separate companies and each runs its own procurement process.
