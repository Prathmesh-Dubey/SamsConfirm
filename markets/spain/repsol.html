Frequently asked questions

Does Repsol require its own equipment approval beyond national certification?

Most large operators in Spain, Repsol included, run a vendor/equipment approval step on top of the national ATEX/IECEx certificate. That process is separate from this page and is handled directly with the site's procurement or HSE team.

Is this page an endorsement by the operator?

No. This page describes where the Smart Ex-04 would fit at a Repsol site based on its published operating environment; it is not an endorsement, partnership or supply agreement.
