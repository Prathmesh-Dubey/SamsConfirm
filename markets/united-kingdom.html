Where it works in the UK

UK classified areas fall into two patterns. Offshore and downstream, the work sits on a few large installations with formal permit systems. Inland, Zone 1 and Zone 2 work is spread thinly across gas networks, water and waste sites staffed by one or two people at a time.

Operators and contractors such as Harbour Energy, Adura and Ithaca Energy need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Aberdeen, St Fergus and Sullom Voe. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Regulatory route · United Kingdom

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Two routes are open in Great Britain and your file can rest on either. UKEX certification is issued by a UK Approved Body and supports the UKCA mark, while recognition of CE marking for equipment for potentially explosive atmospheres was extended indefinitely by the Product Safety and Metrology etc. (Amendment) Regulations 2024, so an ATEX certificate from an EU notified body remains valid for GB supply. Northern Ireland still follows the EU ATEX regime directly.

The duty that shapes what you actually buy sits on the operator, not the manufacturer. DSEAR requires a documented hazardous area classification and a justification for every item of equipment selected for a classified area, and offshore that evidence goes into the safety case the HSE reviews. So a UK enquiry opens with the certificate schedule, the protection level and the marking string, and the radio side is checked against the Radio Equipment Regulations too.

Ask about United Kingdom →
Is the EX-04Z1 certified for hazardous areas in the UK yet?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and the certification programme is in progress, with UKEX to follow. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and no unit is supplied for classified-area use before its certificate is issued.

Do we need UKCA, or is a CE marked ATEX device still acceptable in Great Britain?

Both are acceptable. Recognition of CE marking for equipment for potentially explosive atmospheres was extended indefinitely in 2024, so an ATEX certificate and a UKEX certificate are each a valid basis for GB supply. Northern Ireland follows the EU regime.

Can it be used offshore in the North Sea?

That is one of the design cases. Zone 1 classification, IP68 sealing and rugged build are aimed at exposed decks and terminal jetties, and the certificate evidence is written to go into an offshore safety case. Offshore trials are part of the pilot programme.

What does the T4 temperature class mean for our site?

T4 limits the maximum surface temperature of the equipment to 135 °C, which covers most substances handled at UK sites. The ambient range on the certificate and the device operating range are both stated on the datasheet.

How will repair and battery replacement work here?

A UK repair and battery replacement route is part of the launch programme, because an intrinsically safe device must be repaired through a controlled route to keep its Ex certification valid. Terms are published at launch and can be reviewed earlier under a pre-launch agreement.

Can we get a price now?

Not a published one before launch. Pre-launch pricing is quoted against a stated quantity and configuration, and evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement rather than sold. Register interest and the datasheet comes first.
