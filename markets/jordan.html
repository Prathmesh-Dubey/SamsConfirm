Phosphate and potash dust as the primary hazard

Unlike the Gulf, Jordan’s largest classified-area exposure is combustible dust from mining and processing rather than hydrocarbon gas. The device is designed for Zone 21 as well as Zone 1, and IP68 sealing is what matters after a dusty shift at Eshidiya.

Desert heat and altitude swings

Southern desert sites run extreme daytime heat while Amman sits at altitude with cooler nights. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, and the temperature class is the first line an instrumentation engineer checks.

Remote site logistics

Eshidiya and the southern phosphate mines are a genuine distance from Amman, and there is no recharging point inside a classified processing area. The 4,000 mAh cell is specified to last a full shift.

Arabic and English on one build

Jordanian operations run with a mixed national and expatriate technical workforce. Android 16 carries both interface languages on the same handset.

Regulatory route · Jordan

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in Jordan runs through the Jordan Standards and Metrology Organization (JSMO), which sets and enforces national technical regulations including those adopted from Gulf and international standards. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that conformity is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRC) type approval for the radio. Neither is finished yet.

Getting past the plant gate is a separate exercise. Arab Potash Company and Jordan Phosphate Mines Company each run their own vendor-registration and engineering-approval process for equipment entering a classified processing area, and supply is normally expected to run through a Jordanian-registered partner. A device can be fully JSMO-cleared for import and still be unlisted on an operator’s own equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

Questions · Jordan

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Jordan today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the JSMO conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

Is this mainly for dust hazards rather than gas?

Jordan’s largest classified-area exposure is combustible dust from potash and phosphate processing, and the device is designed for Zone 21 as well as Zone 1 gas atmospheres, so it covers both.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Jordan?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price, and pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Which zones and gas groups is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued alongside for projects run to the American code.

Will Arab Potash or JPMC accept it on site?

That decision belongs to the operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review is built on.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both.
