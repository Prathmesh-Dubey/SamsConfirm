Why it fits pulp, board and bioproducts

Built for wood dust risk across a nationwide mill network

Kraft pulping, wood handling and recovery boiler operation generate combustible wood dust across mill sites spread from southern Finland up to Kemi in the north, a distributed hazard pattern rather than a single concentrated site.

Zone 21/22 dust atmospheres in wood handling and pulping areas

Debarking, chip handling and pulp drying at kraft and bioproduct mills generate combustible wood dust atmospheres, and Ex tb IIIC T135°C dust protection applies across these process areas.

ATEX as the governing EU framework

As an EU member state, Finland's hazardous-area equipment requirements are governed by the ATEX directives across the forest industry.

Bioproduct mills adding chemical process scope to traditional pulping

Metsä Group's bioproduct mills at Äänekoski and Kemi convert wood into textile fibres, biochemicals and biofuels alongside pulp, extending the classified-area scope beyond conventional pulp and paper production.

Fleet management across a multi-company national mill network

EMM and MDM enrolment supports operators managing devices consistently across mills belonging to UPM, Stora Enso and Metsä Group in different regions of the country.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use in Finnish pulp mills yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including ATEX, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Do bioproduct mills carry different classified-area requirements than conventional pulp mills?

Bioproduct mills add biochemical process areas alongside the wood-dust risk common to all kraft mills, so the same dust rating applies but the site footprint of classified areas is typically larger.

Is ATEX the only certification needed for Finnish forest industry operators?

ATEX is the primary EU regulatory framework; IECEx serves as useful supporting international technical evidence.

Does one company's mill approval extend to a competitor's mill elsewhere in the country?

No, UPM, Stora Enso and Metsä Group each run separate procurement processes across their own mill networks.
