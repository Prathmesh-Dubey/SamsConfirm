The sector · Upstream oil and gas

Onshore and offshore, mature and new

Two large producers dominate the picture. OMV Petrom reports domestic production through assets that include Moesia, Crișana, Oltenia, Muntenia and Petromar, the Black Sea asset, while Romgaz produces gas mainly in the Transylvanian Basin around Mediaș and Târgu Mureș. Together they are developing Neptun Deep, a deep-water gas project whose first gas is targeted for 2027, with a subsea pipeline of about 160 km to the shore at Tuzla. The country page also names a Black Sea gas development at Midia with an onshore gas treatment plant near Vadu. What distinguishes Romanian upstream work from the Gulf or the North Sea is its pattern: very many small, old and dispersed facilities. A mature field has hundreds of wellheads, tank batteries and flowlines, serviced by small crews in vans, with the occasional large drilling or workover campaign passing through.

What an explosion proof mobile phone has to cope with in Romanian upstream

Upstream conditions test a handset on distance, weather and the lack of infrastructure rather than on process complexity.

Hundreds of sites, one round

A field operator’s route can cover many wellsites in a day. Four-constellation GNSS and 4G LTE let a route be tracked and each inspection photograph carry a position.

No charger inside the boundary

There is no power point inside a classified wellsite. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized to cover a complete shift with the camera and a work-order application running.

Mud, ice and rain

Romanian winters and thaws leave wellsites icy or flooded. IP68 sealing and rugged construction are meant for that, and trial use at a mature field is the right test.

Offshore reach

Platform crews are flown or shipped out and stay for a rotation. The device must work with the platform’s own network; any offshore use is subject to the operator’s assurance process.

Drilling contractor turnover

Drilling and workover crews belong to contractors. Android Enterprise policy lets the operator issue devices, restrict applications and wipe them when the rig leaves.

Frequently asked questions

Does a gas field need a IIC phone?

Dry gas is mostly methane, gas group IIA, so IIA would meet the classification. A IIC target lets the same handset be used across other sites such as refineries, so one model can be issued across an operator’s estate.

Where can I read about the operators?

See our pages for OMV Petrom and Romgaz, which cover the two principal producers in more detail.

Can the device be used on the platform?

It is designed for Zone 1 duty with IP68 sealing and a rugged build, but offshore acceptance rests with the operator’s own assurance process and the final certificate. Nothing is supplied before certification.
