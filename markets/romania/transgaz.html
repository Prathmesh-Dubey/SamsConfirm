What is classified along a gas transmission system

A transmission operator has fewer people and more kilometres than a producer or a refinery. The classified places are scattered and mostly small: compressor stations, pressure reduction and metering stations, pig launcher and receiver traps, valve nodes and the delivery points where gas is odorised before it leaves the system. The BRUA corridor built across the country added a 479 km pipeline and three compressor stations at Podișor, Bibești and Jupa, the last with two Solar Turbines compressor units of 4.6 MW each. A further project to bring Black Sea gas ashore and into the system at Podișor is linked to the Neptun Deep development. The gas is natural gas, normally gas group IIA with a T1 class, so the question for a handset is less the gas than the number of sites, the pressure and the volume that could be released. Work in a classified area is controlled by permit, and the nearest colleague is often a long drive away.

Classified situations

Compressor halls
Gas-turbine or engine halls with ventilation and gas detection as the primary control.
Pressure reduction
Regulator runs and relief outlets that vent small volumes of gas during operation.
Pig traps
Short Zone 1 or Zone 2 situations during launch and receipt, with the trap opened.
Odorant storage
Sulphur compound odorants stored and dosed at delivery points; strong smell, not an ignition source.
Vent and blowdown stacks
Release outlets whose zone depends on stack height and the gas released.
Gas measurement
Analyser cabinets and sampling lines classified as part of the station.

Why a Zone 1 mobile phone fits a gas transmission corridor in Romania

Distance as the main hazard

A compressor station may be a long way from the nearest office, so the handset is also the operator’s link to the dispatcher. 4G LTE and Wi-Fi give that link, and four-constellation GNSS gives the dispatcher a position that does not depend on a description.

Pig runs and their paperwork

A pig launch is a sequence of valve operations, permits and confirmations. A handset can carry the checklist and photograph the trap closure, so the record is made at the trap rather than reconstructed afterwards.

Heat from turbine halls

Compressor halls are warm and noisy, and the handset has to be usable with gloves and ear defenders. The certified ambient range will be published once the certificate fixes it; the T4 temperature class target is what a station engineer checks against the area classification.

Evidence for the explosion protection document

HG 1058/2006 puts the employer in charge of the explosion protection document. The gas group, marking string and declaration of conformity supplied with the device can be quoted in that document without paraphrase.

Contractor crews on shutdowns

Annual station shutdowns bring turbine specialists and instrument contractors. Android Enterprise enrolment lets the operator issue devices for the campaign and withdraw them afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Does a gas transmission operator need a IIC handset?

Natural gas is gas group IIA, so IIA would meet the area classification on its own. A IIC marking target means one device can be issued across a transmission estate and into other sites, such as hydrogen-bearing industry, without being re-purchased.

Does Transgaz have a supplier approval process?

We have not verified any published supplier programme and do not claim one. Ask the Transgaz procurement and HSE teams what documentation an equipment supplier must provide.

Can the handset be used as a lone-worker device?

It provides position, a network connection and applications that can be configured for check-ins. Whether that meets a company’s lone worker policy is for the company’s HSE team to decide.

When can we test one?

Evaluation units are planned around the Q4 2026 launch, after certification. Request the datasheet and certification schedule and we will confirm what can be shared at each stage.
