The sector · Cork, Dublin and inland sites

Where pharma and biopharma sites are classified

Cork Harbour has pharmaceutical plants at Ringaskiddy and Little Island, including Janssen's biologics site at Ringaskiddy, operating since 2005, and a Janssen plant at Little Island that makes bulk active ingredients and dates from 1981. Eli Lilly has made active ingredients at Dunderrow near Kinsale since the late 1970s. MSD has Brinny in Cork and Ballydine in Tipperary, while Pfizer and Takeda have plants at Grange Castle and MSD has a biologics site in Dublin. The hazard sits mostly in the chemical side: solvents in synthesis and recovery, powder in drying and milling, and utilities. Biologics plants are largely aqueous, so their classified areas tend to be solvent stores, alcohol points and the boiler house. The practical point is that you cannot assume a pharmaceutical site is hazardous, or that it is not.

Solvents across the gas groups

Ethanol, acetone, isopropanol and toluene fall in lower groups than IIC, and hydrogen falls in IIC. The EX-04Z1 marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is designed to cover the whole list with one device.

Cleaning and gowning

Handsets are wiped with alcohol and disinfectants, and gowning rules stop people leaving a suite to charge. IP68, MIL-STD-810H and a 4,000 mAh battery specified for a full shift are all aimed at that routine.

Controlled configuration

GMP change control asks for a defined build. Android 16 with Android Enterprise and MDM allows camera, location and applications to be fixed and recorded.

Contractor-heavy shutdowns

Maintenance windows at API plants bring many contractors. Restricted profiles keep them to the permit and reporting tools.

Evidence for the explosion protection document

Under Part 8 of the 2007 General Application Regulations the employer names the equipment chosen for each zone. For a pharmaceutical site that document sits beside the GMP files, so the datasheet, marking and instructions need to be supplied in a form both teams can file once certification is complete.

Campuses and satellite sites

Cork Harbour and Grange Castle are large campuses. Four-constellation GNSS and a 50 MP camera help report defects in the right place.

Questions · Pharma and biopharma

Explosion proof mobile phone questions for Ireland

Are all pharmaceutical plants hazardous areas?

No. Cleanrooms are controlled for contamination, not explosion. Solvent and powder areas are classified, and the site's classification drawing decides which.

Does hydrogen need a different handset?

Hydrogen is in group IIC. The EX-04Z1 is designed to that group, so it is covered, though the site decides which areas are classified.

Where are the company-specific pages?

Site-level detail for two of the largest operators is on the pages for an explosion proof phone for Pfizer and an explosion proof phone for MSD in Ireland.

Is the EX-04Z1 available for trial?

Not in a classified area before certification exists. It is pre-launch and certification is in progress. A desktop review of the datasheet, the proposed configuration and the cleaning guidance can start now, which is usually the longest part of a pharmaceutical qualification anyway.
