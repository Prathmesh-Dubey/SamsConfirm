Ireland · Food, Drink and Distilling

Explosion proof mobile phone for Food, Drink and Distilling in Ireland

Irish whiskey and dairy are both exports built on flammable atmospheres: ethanol vapour in the still house and bonded warehouse, and combustible milk powder in the drier. This page is for the EHS and engineering teams who need an explosion proof mobile phone for food, drink and distilling in Ireland. The SAMS EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and is being certified ahead of its Q4 2026 launch.

The sector · Midleton, Mitchelstown, Ballyragget

Where distilling and dairy plants are classified

Irish Distillers, a Pernod Ricard company, makes Jameson and other whiskeys at the New Midleton Distillery in east Cork, a plant whose published capacity runs to tens of millions of litres of spirit a year from pot stills and column stills, with maturation warehouses at Midleton and Dungourney. Ethanol vapour is a gas hazard, and where a warehouse holds many casks the vapour question becomes one of ventilation and classification rather than a single release point. On the dairy side, Dairygold has built new milk drying facilities at Castlefarm in Mitchelstown, and Tirlán has a large dairy site at Ballyragget in Kilkenny. Milk powder is a combustible dust, and Irish industry commentary has long noted that dryers are the usual setting for dust explosions here. Grain intake and malt milling add a second dust problem at distilleries.

Ethanol vapour and the gas group

Ethanol falls in a lower gas group than the one targeted by the EX-04Z1, whose marking aim is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, so it is covered by design. The temperature class and zone drawn on the distillery drawings are still checked by the site.

Dust zones in the drier hall

Milk powder and grain dust are classified as Zone 21 or 22 depending on how often a cloud forms. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 21, so a technician can move between a still house and a drier without changing handset.

Wet, steamy and washed-down areas

Dairies are washed down often and stills run hot and damp. IP68 sealing is the basis for surviving that, and wash chemicals should be checked with the manufacturer.

Seasonal peaks in the dairy year

Milk supply peaks in spring and early summer, so drier plants run flat out while maintenance waits for winter. A managed fleet of handsets can be extended with seasonal staff and stripped back afterwards.

A food-safety rulebook of its own

Food plants control glass, loose items and photography. Camera and application policy can be enforced centrally with Android Enterprise and the MDM console the site already uses.

Questions · Food, drink and distilling

Explosion proof mobile phone questions for Ireland

Is a bonded warehouse a hazardous area?

It can be, depending on the cask count, ventilation and the area classification the distiller has carried out. Only the site's own assessment can say, so ask the EHS team for the explosion protection document.

Does milk powder need Zone 21 equipment?

Where a dust cloud can form in normal operation, Zone 21 is the likely classification. Frequency of cloud and layer determines zone, and dairies decide this for each plant.

Can a phone with a camera enter a food plant?

Many plants control cameras. Because the EX-04Z1 is managed by MDM, a policy can disable the camera in set areas or at set times.

Do you supply Irish Distillers, Dairygold or Tirlán?

No. SAMS has no customer relationship with them and the EX-04Z1 is pre-launch, with certification in progress.
