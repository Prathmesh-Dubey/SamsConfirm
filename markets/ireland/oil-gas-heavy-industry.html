The sector · Cork, Mayo and the Shannon

Where oil, gas and heavy industry are classified in Ireland

The Health and Safety Authority's upper tier COMAH list is a reasonable map of this sector. It includes Irving Oil's Whitegate refinery, Calor's LPG site at Whitegate, the Atlantic Fuel Supply Company terminal at Foynes Harbour and the ESB's Moneypoint generating station at Killimer in Clare. Corrib is separate, a gas field in the Atlantic with its processing terminal at Bellanaboy, and Aughinish Alumina on the Shannon is Europe's largest alumina refinery. These are very different sites with very different hazards: hydrocarbon vapour at Whitegate, natural gas at Bellanaboy, fuel storage and loading at Foynes, and coal handling and fuel oil at Moneypoint. A phone policy cannot be written once for all of them; the area classification at each site is what governs.

Estuary and Atlantic weather

Foynes, Moneypoint and Whitegate all sit on tidal water, and Bellanaboy is on the Mayo coast. Rain and salt air are normal, and IP68 sealing is the baseline. The target marking of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 is the figure that matters inside the plant boundary.

Turnarounds bring the crowd

Refinery and power station outages arrive in blocks, with many contractors on site for weeks. A managed Android 16 handset can be issued, locked to the approved applications and wiped on exit, which a personal phone cannot.

Gas groups that vary by site

Methane at Bellanaboy, mixed hydrocarbons at Whitegate, LPG at the gas site, and hydrogen where it is present all fall in groups the EX-04Z1 is designed to cover through its group IIC target. The classification drawing for each area still decides.

Long distances and few charge points

A refinery or power station spreads over a big footprint, and a round from the control room to a far tank can be a long walk. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift, and four-constellation GNSS locates a defect on a large site.

Documented evidence of each fault

A 50 MP camera and a recorded location make a defect report concrete. MDM policy on the camera can be applied where site photography rules demand it.

Questions · Oil, gas and heavy industry

Explosion proof mobile phone questions for Ireland

Which Irish sites are classed as upper tier COMAH?

The Health and Safety Authority publishes the list and updates it. It includes Whitegate refinery, Foynes, Moneypoint and the Whitegate LPG site, among others. Check the current list rather than relying on this page.

Is Aughinish a hazardous area site?

Parts of it can be. A large alumina plant has combustion plant and fuel storage, but most of its process is not flammable-atmosphere work. The site's area classification identifies exactly where a handset must be Ex rated.

Does coal dust need Zone 21 equipment?

Where coal is handled, dust zones around conveyors, mills and storage are common, and Zone 21 is the design target for the EX-04Z1. Whether Moneypoint's current fuel handling has such areas is for ESB to say.

Are these operators customers of SAMS?

No. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch with certification in progress, and SAMS has no supply relationship with the operators named here.
