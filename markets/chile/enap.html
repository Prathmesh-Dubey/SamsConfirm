Why it fits

Designed for one company spanning two climate extremes

Few national oil companies run operations spanning as wide a climate range as ENAP does between its central refineries and its far-southern gas fields.

Zone 1 gas across refining and upstream gas alike

Concón and Biobío refinery process units and Magallanes wellhead and gathering facilities are all classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups common to both.

Sub-Antarctic cold at Magallanes

Gas production near the tip of South America operates in sustained cold and severe wind chill, conditions that test battery performance and material flexibility beyond what the temperate-zone refineries experience.

A single national operator's standard, applied across the range

ENAP's own engineering function sets standards intended to apply across its full asset base, meaning a device accepted for refinery use is likely to be evaluated for Magallanes gas-field use under the same broad review rather than a wholly separate process.

Remote southern operations far from central-Chile supply chains

Magallanes is geographically isolated from ENAP's central refining operations and from most of Chile's population and logistics network, raising the value of a rugged, self-sufficient device for field crews there.

Fleet management across a geographically split operation

EMM and MDM enrolment supports ENAP managing devices consistently across refining sites in central Chile and gas operations in the far south.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at ENAP sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit Magallanes' sub-Antarctic climate?

Environmental durability testing is specified with sustained cold and wind-chill exposure in mind, and the certificate will state its exact test conditions once issued.

Does approval at ENAP's refineries carry to its Magallanes gas fields?

ENAP's engineering review is intended to apply company-wide, but each asset's specific classified-area documentation should still be confirmed locally.

Is ENAP the only operator in Magallanes?

ENAP is the dominant operator of gas production in the Magallanes region as part of Chile's state hydrocarbons operations.
