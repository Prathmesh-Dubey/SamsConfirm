Why it fits

Designed for brine evaporation, not hard-rock mining

SQM's lithium and potassium process moves brine through evaporation ponds before any crushing or drying happens, an operational sequence with its own environmental demands distinct from a copper mine's dust profile.

Zone 21/22 dust rating at the processing plant

Lithium carbonate and potassium salt drying and packaging generate combustible dust, and the EX-04Z1's Zone 21 / Zone 22 target addresses this stage of SQM's operation.

Hypersaline, high-altitude desert exposure ahead of the dust hazard

The Atacama salt flat combines extreme aridity, intense UV and airborne saline particulate from the evaporation ponds at elevations above 2,300 metres, a combination that stresses equipment differently from a standard industrial dust environment.

Rapid capacity growth under global lithium demand

SQM has expanded production significantly to meet global battery-material demand, meaning workforce and equipment fleet growth at a pace that puts a premium on standardised, easily deployed devices rather than site-by-site procurement.

A process shared with, but distinct from, Jordan's Dead Sea potash operation

The solar-evaporation brine process resembles potash extraction elsewhere in the world, but SQM's high-altitude Atacama setting brings a different combination of altitude, dryness and UV exposure than a sea-level brine operation.

Fleet management across pond and plant operations

EMM and MDM enrolment supports SQM managing a consistent device profile from the evaporation ponds through to the drying and packaging plant.

About the operator

SQM (Sociedad Química y Minera)

A major global lithium and potassium producer, extracting from brine on the Atacama salt flats.

Core process
Solar evaporation of Atacama salt-flat brine, followed by drying and packaging of lithium carbonate and potassium salts.
Location
Atacama salt flat, above 2,300 metres elevation.
Primary hazard
Combustible lithium and potassium salt dust at the processing plant, alongside extreme aridity, UV and high-altitude exposure across the site.
Evaluation basis
Site engineering review, built on the IECEx Certificate of Conformity's dust-rating scope once issued.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at SQM yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and the IECEx dust-rating scope is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is this a gas-hazard or dust-hazard certification?

Dust. SQM's process does not involve a significant flammable-gas hazard; combustible salt dust at the drying and packaging plant is the relevant classification.

Does the high-altitude, high-UV environment require special testing?

Environmental durability testing is specified with that combination in mind, though material compatibility for SQM's specific brine and dust conditions should be confirmed with SQM's own engineering team.

Does SQM operate the same way as Albemarle's lithium operations?

Both extract lithium from Atacama brine using a broadly similar evaporation process, but they are separate companies with separate sites and separate procurement processes.
