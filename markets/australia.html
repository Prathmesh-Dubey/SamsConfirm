Heat and temperature class

Pilbara summers push site ambients past 45 degrees, where a handset that throttles at midday is no use. The marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with the ambient range on the certificate.

Dust as well as gas

Iron ore fines, coal dust and alumina dust make Zone 21 a live classification here. The device is designed for Zone 21 dust as well as Zone 1 gas, sealed to IP68.

Remote sites and roster length

Where the nearest replacement is a flight away, a twelve hour shift is the normal case. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a full roster day with radio, camera and GNSS running.

Gloves, drops and vibration

Designed for glove operation and built rugged for drop, shock and vibration, which is what a device meets in a light vehicle on a haul road.

Connectivity across the site

4G LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and NFC cover the plant and carrier networks, and four constellation GNSS holds a position down in a pit. Push to talk runs on the same device.

Fleet management for rostered crews

Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock, remote wipe and controlled updates, which matters when handsets rotate between crews and contractors.

Regulatory route · Australia

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

What your HSE and electrical teams will ask for is the certificate, not the brochure. Australia participates directly in the IECEx system and AS/NZS 60079 is harmonised with IEC 60079, so an IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the practical basis for equipment in a classified area. The ANZEx scheme issues a national certificate from the same test reports, and TestSafe Australia is the best known certification body. Because the handset carries radios, the program also covers ACMA requirements and the RCM mark.

Underground coal is the exception. Coal mine safety is regulated at state level through Resources Safety and Health Queensland and the NSW Resources Regulator, and underground coal equipment is Group I, a different regime from the Group II surface certification this device targets. A device accepted in a Zone 1 surface area is not automatically accepted underground, so confirm the group. Offshore facilities in Commonwealth waters sit under NOPSEMA, so the handset must also appear in the safety case.

Ask about Australia →
Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use in Australia?

Not yet. The device is pre-launch and every certification, IECEx included, is in progress. Nothing should be planned into a classified area until the Certificate of Conformity is issued.

Does an IECEx certificate satisfy Australian requirements?

For surface and process industry work, in practice yes. Australia is in the IECEx system and AS/NZS 60079 is harmonised with IEC 60079. An ANZEx certificate is issued where a specification names it.

Can it be used in an underground coal mine?

No. Underground coal is Group I equipment, regulated by the states through Resources Safety and Health Queensland and the NSW Resources Regulator. This device targets Group II for Zone 1 and Zone 21.

What zones and marking is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, with NEC 500 Class I Division 1 also in the program.

Will it hold up at Pilbara summer temperatures?

It is designed for that, with a rugged build and IP68 sealing. The figure that matters for compliance is the ambient temperature range printed on the certificate.

When can we get units in Australia?

Launch is Q4 2026. Until then you can request the datasheet, register a requirement for the first allocation, or join the pilot program for an evaluation unit.

Who handles warranty and repair in Australia?

Warranty and service run through the appointed Australian partner. Repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a facility recognised for that work.

What does an explosion proof phone cost in Australia?

There is no list price while certification is in progress. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration, and for Australian sites the questions that usually decide a tender are service related: who holds stock in country, the turnaround on a replacement, and the repair route for certified equipment.
