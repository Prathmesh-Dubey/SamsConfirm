Why it fits ports and industry

Built for growing industrial free zones

Duqm and Sohar combine port logistics with fuel storage and emerging heavy industry in purpose-built economic zones.

Zone 1 gas at fuel terminals

Bulk liquid and fuel storage terminals within these zones are classified Zone 1, matched directly by the device's intrinsic-safety rating.

Built for a diversifying industrial base

As Duqm and Sohar add new refining, petrochemical and manufacturing tenants, a single certified device specification serves multiple new operators without separate procurement each time.

Coastal port conditions

Salt air and humidity at both port zones are specified for in the same IP68 and MIL-STD-810H build used across Oman's coastal sites.
