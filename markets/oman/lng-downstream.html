Why it fits LNG and downstream

Built for coastal liquefaction and refining

Oman's downstream sector sits on the coast, combining humid marine conditions with conventional Zone 1 refinery and LNG risk.

Zone 1 gas across refining and LNG

Crude distillation at Sohar and liquefaction trains at Qalhat are both Zone 1 gas environments the device is designed to enter.

Coastal humidity and salt air

Sohar and Qalhat sit directly on the coast, adding humidity and salt exposure to the conventional heat of a Gulf refinery. IP68 sealing is specified for that combination.

Built for turnaround-driven surges

Refinery and LNG-train turnarounds multiply contractor traffic into short windows; one enrolled handset carrying the permit app cuts what every crew signs out.
