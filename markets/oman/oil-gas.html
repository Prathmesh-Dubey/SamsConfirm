The sector · Upstream Oman

Who an explosion proof mobile phone serves in Omani oil and gas

Omani upstream is made of a few very large operations rather than many small ones. PDO is the dominant producer and its Block 6 estate is covered on its own page: explosion proof phone for PDO. Around it are concessions that behave differently. Occidental Oman runs heavy oil at Mukhaizna with large-scale steam injection. Daleel Petroleum works Block 5 near Fahud. CC Energy Development holds Blocks 3 and 4 in central Oman. BP Oman operates the Block 61 tight gas development in central Oman, where Khazzan and Ghazeer feed a central processing facility with gas and condensate export lines connected to the national gas network at Fahud. Each of those is a different hazardous-area problem, and a handset specification that only fits one will disappoint on the others.

Heat on open plant

Interior summers run well above 45 °C with no cover. MIL-STD-810H construction and the II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 temperature class target are set with that in mind, and the certificate figure is what your instrument engineer checks.

Steam, heat and dust together

Thermal recovery puts hot surfaces, hydrocarbon vapour and powder or sand in the same area. Zone 1 and Zone 21 are both design targets for the EX-04Z1, so one specification covers a field and its processing plant.

Remote logistics

A central processing facility can be a long way from stores. A 4,000 mAh battery specified for a full shift and a sealed body that is not opened on site keep a unit working between supply runs.

Position and evidence

Four-constellation GNSS and a 50 MP camera give a coordinate and a clear photograph for a fault on a flowline in open desert, where a description alone gets lost.

One policy across operators and contractors

Android 16 with Android Enterprise enrols into the MDM console each operator already uses, and a contractor's lent units can be locked, restricted and wiped centrally.

Use cases · Upstream

Where the EX-04Z1 goes to work.

Enhanced recovery01

Steam and polymer plant rounds

Operators and technicians at a steamflood or polymer site walk between generators, injection manifolds and the wells they feed. A handset that logs a reading, photographs a leaking flange and calls the control room replaces three items and keeps the round inside one shift.

Gas processing02

Central processing facility inspection

Inspection and maintenance crews at a gas processing facility work from permits and isolation records. An enrolled device carries both and connects by Bluetooth to the personal gas detector the job already requires.

Drilling03

Rig moves and well interventions

A drilling or workover crew moves its classified area through the field. The handset carries the daily report, the site directory and the permit, restricted to the applications the operator and contractor have agreed.

Questions · Oil & Gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for Oman oil and gas use yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 are all running, alongside Omani conformity and radio approval.

Will each operator accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows its own engineering review, which is built on the IECEx certificate once it is issued. PDO is covered on its own page, and other concessions run separate reviews.

Does it work with an operator's existing MDM?

It is designed to enrol into existing Android Enterprise and MDM consoles, so fleets can be managed alongside the rest of the estate rather than through a separate tool.

Is there an Arabic interface?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English, so Omani and expatriate staff can be issued the same build under the same policy.
