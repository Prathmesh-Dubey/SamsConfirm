Why it fits oil and gas

Built for Oman's interior desert fields

Omani upstream operations run across remote interior desert, far from the coastal population centres.

Zone 1 gas across wellhead and gathering

PDO's wellheads, gathering stations and gas-oil separation plants are classified Zone 1. Ex ib IIC T4 is designed to meet that classification directly.

Extreme interior desert heat

Oman's interior fields run some of the highest sustained temperatures in the Gulf. MIL-STD-810H construction and the Ex ib IIC T4 temperature class are specified with that in mind.

A battery for remote field logistics

Interior fields are a genuine distance from Muscat, and there is no recharging point inside a classified area. The 4,000 mAh cell is specified for a full shift.

Enhanced oil recovery projects included

Steam-flood and polymer enhanced-recovery projects run by international operators share the same Zone 1 classification as conventional production.
