The sector · Fertilisers and chemicals

An explosion proof mobile phone for fertiliser plants and estates

Toros Tarım, which says it holds about 38% of Türkiye’s installed chemical fertiliser capacity, has plants at Ceyhan, Mersin and Samsun, making ammonium nitrate and CAN, DAP and NPK compounds. İGSAŞ’s Kocaeli plant makes ammonia, urea and compound fertilisers, and its Kütahya site makes ammonium nitrate and nitric acid. Gübretaş is listed with plants at Yarımca, İskenderun, İzmir and Samsun. The chemistry follows the gas and dust split: ammonia and nitric acid on the process side, fertiliser dust in granulation, bagging and bulk stores. In Dilovası, GEBKİM is a chemistry-specialised organised industrial zone with resin and paint makers. On the Mersin coast, Şişecam’s Kazanlı soda plant runs about 1.5 million tonnes a year.

Dust and gas within a short walk

A granulation hall and an ammonia process area are close together. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 21 dust and Zone 1 gas under one certification programme, so a worker does not leave a device at the boundary.

Toxic gas is a separate job

Ammonia is toxic as well as flammable, so personal gas detectors stay mandatory. The handset does not replace one; it carries the work order, camera and call to the control room.

Estates with many tenants

An estate such as GEBKİM has many separate operators. Each runs its own console, and the EX-04Z1 can be managed from an existing MDM console, so one device policy does not have to span tenants.

Port and plant on one route

A fertiliser movement from a plant to a quay passes from process areas to a loading yard. IP68 sealing is meant for spray and dust, and the 4,000 mAh battery for a full round.

Questions · Fertilisers and chemicals

Frequently asked questions

Does the handset replace a toxic gas detector?

No. A phone is not a gas detector. Ammonia and similar gases need the personal and fixed detectors the site already specifies. The handset adds a camera, a position fix and a record, not gas detection.

Which dust risks fall under Zone 21?

Zone 21 is an area where combustible dust is likely to be present in normal operation. Granulation, drying and bagging can qualify, but each plant classifies its own areas. Ask the İSG team for the drawing.

How would an estate such as GEBKİM treat visiting equipment?

Estate rules and tenant rules both apply. Ask the estate administration and the tenant’s HSE team what they need to see. This page does not state any specific estate programme.
