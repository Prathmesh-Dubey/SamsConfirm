Turkish refining and petrochemical capacity sits in a few clusters: Aliağa on the Aegean, İzmit and Körfez on the Marmara, and single plants at Kırıkkale and Batman. This page is for the HSE managers and engineers who need an explosion proof mobile phone for refining and petrochemicals in Türkiye that works across clusters and operators. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and is still being certified.

The sector · Refining and petrochemicals

An explosion proof mobile phone across the refining clusters

Aliağa is the densest cluster. Tüpraş’s İzmir refinery, SOCAR Türkiye’s STAR refinery and the Petkim petrochemical complex sit within the same coastal strip, which means contractors, shared utilities and shared jetties. On the Marmara, Tüpraş’s İzmit refinery at Körfez has an 11 million tonne capacity and a residue upgrading complex, and Petkim’s original plant is at Yarımca in Kocaeli. Dilovası, also in Kocaeli, houses the chemistry-specialised industrial zone known as GEBKİM. Inland, Kırıkkale takes crude through a 457 km pipeline from Ceyhan and Batman runs local crude. Each cluster has its own labour market and its own rhythm of stops.

Stops that draw on the same labour pool

Planned shutdowns at neighbouring plants compete for the same contractor workforce. A handset managed through each operator’s own console can be issued, restricted and wiped as a crew moves from one gate to the next.

Hydrogen, ethylene and dust together

A refinery brings hydrogen (IIC), a cracker brings ethylene (IIB), and a polymer plant brings powder. The EX-04Z1’s II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 gas target and Zone 21 dust design are meant to cover the sector with one device.

The legal file is in Turkish

Under law 6331, the employer’s explosion protection document is written and kept in Turkish. A Turkish interface and documentation set are meant to make citing the marking string simple.

Coastal and inland conditions

Aliağa and Körfez face salt air and summer heat, Kırıkkale and Batman face dust and inland winters. IP68 sealing and a published temperature range are the evidence a reviewer will ask for.

Frequently asked questions

What does Seveso-based regulation change for equipment choice?

Sites that hold large inventories come under a regulation on the prevention of major industrial accidents, which an update aligned with the EU’s Seveso III brought into Turkish legislation in 2019. It adds safety reporting for the site, so the equipment file can be read for more than one purpose.

Why do contractors at different refineries need one policy?

Contractor firms move between sites on a shutdown calendar. A handset the operator can enrol, restrict and wipe means the same device and policy follow the crew.
