Regulatory route · Egypt

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in Egypt runs through the Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality (EOS) under the Ministry of Trade and Industry, working alongside the General Organization for Export and Import Control (GOEIC) for customs clearance. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that conformity is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs National Telecom Regulatory Authority (NTRA) type approval for the radio. None of the three is finished yet.

Getting past the site gate is a separate exercise from national conformity. EGPC and its operating affiliates, along with international operators like Eni, each run their own vendor-registration and engineering-approval process for equipment entering a classified area, and supply is normally expected to run through an Egyptian-registered partner. A device can be fully EOS-cleared for import and still be unlisted on an operator’s own equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

Questions · Egypt

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Egypt today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the EOS conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Egypt?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price and no quoted delivery, and a supplier who gives you a firm figure for an uncertified Ex device is telling you something they cannot support. Pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Is an explosion proof phone the same thing as an intrinsically safe phone?

They are different protection concepts, although the search terms are used interchangeably. Flameproof, written Ex d, contains an explosion inside a heavy enclosure. Intrinsic safety, written Ex ib, limits circuit energy so ignition cannot occur, and that is what a Zone 1 handset uses. The marking target here is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4.

Which zones and gas groups is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued alongside, which matters on Egyptian projects run by international contractors working to the American code.

Will EGPC or Eni accept it on site?

That decision belongs to the operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx certificate is the evidence their engineering review is built on, and the vendor file is being prepared with an Egyptian partner.

Will it cope with Gulf of Suez and Western Desert conditions?

It is designed to, with IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing behind it. The number to evaluate is the temperature class on the certificate, checked against the maximum surface temperature your area classification permits.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both.

How do repairs and battery replacement work?

A device holding an Ex certificate cannot be opened on site or repaired by a general phone workshop without invalidating it. The service route runs through SAMS and the appointed Egyptian partner, with spares pooled near Suez.
