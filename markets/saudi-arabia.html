Regulatory route · Saudi Arabia

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Procurement will be asked for a Product Certificate of Conformity on the SABER platform, then a Shipment Certificate for every consignment before customs releases it. For hazardous-area equipment the evidence underneath that certificate is an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs CST registration for the radio. None of the three is finished yet.

The second gate decides whether the handset gets through the plant gate. Saudi Aramco runs an approved-vendor process with material specifications above the national standard, and SABIC, Ma’aden and the Saudi Electricity Company each run their own engineering approval. A device can be fully SABER-cleared and still be unusable on site until the operator’s instrumentation or telecoms group has listed it, so plan for two timelines.

Questions · Saudi Arabia

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use in Saudi Arabia yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and every certification, including IECEx, ATEX and the SASO conformity route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. We will not supply a unit into a classified area before the certificate covering it exists.

Will it be accepted on Saudi Aramco sites?

Aramco approval is separate from national conformity and is granted by Aramco, not by us. The IECEx certificate is the technical evidence that process is built on, and the vendor documentation is being prepared with an in-Kingdom partner.

Which zones and gas groups is it designed for?

Zone 1 and Zone 21, gas and dust. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. NEC 500 Class I Division 1 is being pursued in parallel for Saudi projects written to the American code.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Arabic or English on the same handset, and the user manual will be supplied in both. Your own site apps keep whatever languages they already use.

Will it cope with an Eastern Province summer?

It is designed to, with IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing. The number to evaluate is the temperature class on the certificate, checked against the maximum surface temperature your area classification allows.

How do repairs and battery replacement work?

An intrinsically safe device cannot be opened on site or repaired by a general phone workshop without invalidating its certification. The service route runs through SAMS and the appointed Saudi partner, and is being defined as part of the launch.

Can we get a unit to test now?

Yes, for evaluation. Register interest with your site details and we will tell you what is available and when. Pre-launch pricing is on request; we do not publish prices or quote lead times while certification is running.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Saudi Arabia?

There is no list price yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and we do not publish pricing for a device whose certification is still running. Pre-launch pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration, and supply into the Kingdom will run through a partner holding the relevant vendor registrations.
