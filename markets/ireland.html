Where it works in Ireland

Ireland has one oil refinery, Irving Oil's plant at Whitegate in Cork Harbour, and one indigenous gas field, Corrib, landed at Bellanaboy in Mayo. Distilleries and dairy powder plants complete the picture and bring their own zones with them.

Operators and contractors such as Irving Oil, Aughinish Alumina and Pfizer need an explosion proof mobile phone they can carry into classified areas at Ringaskiddy, Whitegate and Little Island and Carrigaline. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 duty there.

Solvent atmospheres and the gas group

The target marking is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4. Group IIC sits above the groups that cover the ethanol, acetone, isopropanol and toluene handled in pharmaceutical production, and it also covers the hydrogen used in hydrogenation steps, so one device answers the whole solvent list rather than most of it.

Wipe-down between batches

A handset in a GMP area is cleaned far more often than a handset on a refinery. IP68 sealing and rugged build are the basis for a device that tolerates a routine of alcohol wipes and gowning without the seals or the screen coating giving up first.

Powder rooms as well as solvent rooms

Dust protection for Zone 21 belongs to the same certification programme as the gas marking. Dispensing, milling and drying areas, and the milk powder driers on the food side, are Zone 21 work, and they usually sit in the same building as the Zone 1 areas.

A configuration you can put in a change record

Android 16 supports EMM and MDM, so the camera, location services and the installed application list can be fixed centrally and evidenced. That is what turns a phone into a controlled item that quality can approve rather than an unmanaged device someone carried in.

Remote terminals and lone working

At Bellanaboy, on the Shannon Estuary or on a jetty at night the connection matters more than the specification. 4G LTE, Wi-Fi and four-constellation GNSS keep a position and a line to the control room across sites where the next person may be a field away.

A full shift on one charge

The 4,000 mAh cell is sized for a whole shift with the camera and a work order application open. There is no charging point inside a classified area, and in a gowned suite going out to find one costs a change of clothing as well as time.

Regulatory route · Ireland

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The product route is short and familiar. ATEX 2014/34/EU is transposed by S.I. No. 230 of 2017, so a notified body certificate, the CE marking and the EU declaration of conformity are the complete product-side requirement, and there is no separate Irish approval to obtain. The Health and Safety Authority is the market surveillance authority for this equipment as well as the occupational safety regulator, which means the same body reads the certificate and inspects the workplace. Radio conformity runs under the Radio Equipment Directive, with ComReg responsible for radio spectrum and equipment supervision. Because Ireland works in English, none of the translation questions that slow a purchase in continental Europe apply here, and the evaluation moves straight to the technical and quality questions.

The duties that decide what you actually buy belong to the employer, under the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work Act 2005 and Part 8 of the Safety, Health and Welfare at Work (General Application) Regulations 2007, S.I. No. 299 of 2007, which transposes the ATEX workplace directive. Part 8 requires the hazardous area classification, the assessment of ignition sources and an explosion protection document naming the equipment selected for each zone. The Health and Safety Authority publishes its own guidance on Part 8 and inspects against it. On upper tier sites the Chemicals Act (COMAH) Regulations 2015 add the major accident regime, with the Authority as central competent authority. On pharmaceutical sites there is a second, parallel system that a supplier should expect: anything introduced into a manufacturing area also passes through GMP change control, and the paperwork that satisfies an inspector there is about specification, configuration and cleaning, not about zones.

Is the EX-04Z1 already ATEX certified?

No. It is designed to ATEX 2014/34/EU for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and certification is in progress. Launch is planned for Q4 2026, and no unit is supplied for use in a classified area before the certificate covering that use exists.

What does an ATEX phone cost in Ireland?

There is no list price before launch. Pre-launch pricing is given against a defined quantity, configuration and support scope, and it follows the certificate rather than an order date. Evaluation units are issued under a trial agreement rather than sold, so a trial does not depend on agreeing a price first.

Does Ireland need a national approval on top of ATEX?

No. S.I. No. 230 of 2017 transposes ATEX 2014/34/EU, so a notified body certificate and the CE marking are the whole product-side requirement. The duties that follow are the employer's, under Part 8 of the General Application Regulations 2007, and the Health and Safety Authority is the body that reads both sides.

Can it be brought into a GMP manufacturing area?

That is a site decision made through change control, and the honest answer is that we supply the inputs rather than the conclusion. What you get is the specification, the certified configuration, the enclosure and screen materials, and cleaning guidance, which is what a quality team needs to write and approve the change. The trial is the right place to prove the cleaning routine.

Does the target cover the solvents we actually use?

Group IIC is the target, and it sits above the groups covering the common pharmaceutical solvents, as well as covering hydrogen. Temperature class T4 is the other half of the answer, and both will be on the certificate. During evaluation the useful exercise is to check your own solvent list against the marking rather than against a category name.

Is this a flameproof phone or an intrinsically safe one?

Intrinsically safe. Flameproof, the Ex d concept, contains an explosion inside a heavy enclosure. Intrinsic safety, Ex ib, limits the energy in the circuit so ignition cannot start. For something carried through a gowned suite all shift, the Ex ib route is the one that produces a device anyone will actually use.

How will repair and battery replacement work on an island?

Through a named facility, because work on an intrinsically safe device has to be done by a recognised repairer or the certification lapses. A service arrangement covering Ireland and the UK is part of the launch programme, and the turnaround terms can be reviewed under a pre-launch agreement before they are published.

Can we start now if we cannot buy yet?

Yes, and that is the point of the pre-launch stage. You can take the datasheet and certificate schedule, draft the equipment selection entry, open a change control record and run a trial unit through a real week. What you cannot do is put an uncertified device into a classified area, and we will not supply one for that.
