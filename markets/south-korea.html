Regulatory route · South Korea

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in South Korea runs through the Korea Occupational Safety and Health Agency (KOSHA), which certifies equipment for use in hazardous areas under national industrial safety law. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that certification is typically aligned with an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs KC (Korea Certification) type approval from the National Radio Research Agency. None of the three is finished yet.

Getting past the plant gate is a separate exercise. SK Energy, GS Caltex, S-Oil and Hyundai Oilbank each run their own equipment-approval process for a device entering a classified area, and supply is normally expected to run through a Korea-registered partner. A device can be fully KOSHA-certified and still be unlisted on an operator’s own approved-equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

Questions · South Korea

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in South Korea today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the KOSHA route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in South Korea?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price, and pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Will SK Energy or GS Caltex accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. KOSHA certification and the underlying IECEx evidence are what their engineering review is built on.

Does it apply to shipbuilding as well as refining?

Yes. The same Zone 1 gas rating covers confined-space and tank-entry work during LNG carrier and offshore-platform construction at Korean shipyards, alongside conventional refinery and petrochemical use.

Does it work in Korean?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Korean or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both.
