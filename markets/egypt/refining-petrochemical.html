Why it fits refining and petrochemical

Built for a mature refining base near a growing industrial corridor

Suez and Alexandria host some of Egypt's oldest refining infrastructure alongside newer petrochemical capacity, a mix that puts brownfield retrofit considerations alongside standard Zone 1 process-unit hazards.

Zone 1 gas at the process units

Crude distillation, catalytic units and tank farms at Suez and Alexandria refineries are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 intrinsic safety is specified for the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of refining service.

Older assets with mixed-vintage classification

Several Egyptian refineries date to earlier decades of expansion, meaning classified-area documentation can be inconsistent across a single site's older and newer sections, a retrofit reality the device's broad Zone 1 rating is built to sit alongside without requiring area-by-area exceptions.

Canal-corridor logistics adjacency

Suez-area refining sits close to Suez Canal shipping and the Suez Canal Economic Zone's marine terminals, adding canal-side jetty and tanker-loading exposure to some sites' classified-area footprint.

Petrochemical units alongside refining

Sidpec's petrochemical production at Alexandria/Amreya sits adjacent to refining operations, so a device carried between the two needs one consistent rating rather than a change in specification mid-shift.

Centrally managed across two refining hubs

EMM and MDM enrolment supports an operator managing devices consistently across the Suez and Alexandria refining clusters from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for refining use in Egypt yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including IECEx, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit older, brownfield refining sites?

It is designed to be usable across mixed-vintage classified areas without requiring separate devices for older and newer sections of a site, though site-specific area classification always takes precedence.

Does it cover both refining and petrochemical operations?

Yes — the Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 specification is intended to serve both process types where they sit on adjacent or shared sites, as at Alexandria/Amreya.

Does canal-corridor jetty work need separate certification?

Marine-loading and jetty areas fall under the same general gas-atmosphere classification; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued, and site HSE teams should confirm applicability to specific jetty equipment.
