Why it fits

Built for a distributed network, not a single facility

A transmission and processing network operator's procurement problem is different from a single refinery's: consistency across many geographically separated sites matters more than depth at any one of them.

One specification across many compressor stations

Standardising on a single Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 device across a nationwide network of processing plants and compressor stations avoids a patchwork of site-specific equipment approvals that would be difficult to track centrally.

Feed points to both export terminals and domestic supply

GASCO's network feeds gas to Damietta and Idku for export as well as to the domestic distribution grid, meaning device standardisation carries consequences for both the export and domestic sides of the business.

Remote compressor-station duty

Compressor stations along transmission routes are often in less accessible locations than a coastal terminal, and a rugged, long-battery-life device suits maintenance crews who travel between stations rather than working from one fixed site.

A network-wide safety and engineering standard

Because GASCO's own engineering function sets standards applied across its whole network rather than one plant's local team, a single successful evaluation is more likely to translate into network-wide adoption than a one-off site approval would.

Fleet management built for scale

EMM and MDM enrolment is suited to a network operator managing a device fleet spread across many geographically dispersed sites from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use on the GASCO network yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and IECEx certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does approval need to happen separately at each compressor station?

The intent of GASCO's network-wide engineering standard is that a single evaluation and approval can apply across the network, though local site rules may still add specific requirements.

Does GASCO's network serve export terminals or only domestic supply?

Both — the network feeds the domestic distribution grid as well as the Damietta and Idku LNG export terminals.

Is GASCO the same company as Egyptian LNG or Union Fenosa Gas?

No. GASCO processes and transmits gas; Egyptian LNG and Union Fenosa Gas separately operate the Idku and Damietta liquefaction terminals that receive some of that gas.
