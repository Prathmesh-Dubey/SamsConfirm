Built for Azerbaijani operating conditions

Azerbaijan combines Caspian offshore conditions with a continental climate: strong winds and salt spray on the platforms, hot dry summers and cold winters onshore.

Wind, cold winters and hot summers

Baku's wind is a fact of life, winters bring cold mornings and summers are hot and dry. IP68 sealing keeps spray and dust out, and the certificate will state the ambient range the device is approved for.

Gas group for Caspian atmospheres

Hydrocarbon service is typically Group IIA and IIB. A marking target of Ex ib IIC covers those, so one specification runs from an offshore platform to the terminal and the refinery.

Platforms, ladders and transfers

Kit gets carried up stairs and ladders, across wet decks and through crew transfers. The handset is tested against MIL-STD-810H, and IP68 covers immersion rather than splash.

A full shift away from a charger

Process modules and terminal areas offer no safe area socket mid shift. The 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a working day with camera, torch and GNSS in use.

Coverage offshore and onshore

Offshore the device falls back to platform Wi-Fi, and four constellation GNSS fixes a position between structures at the terminal and the plants.

Control over a mixed fleet

Handsets move between operator staff and contractors. Android 16 with EMM and MDM support gives enrolment, remote lock and remote wipe, with Azerbaijani, Russian and English language settings.

Regulatory route · Azerbaijan

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

The certificate HSE and engineering teams in Azerbaijan ask for is the IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and the ATEX certificate where a specification names the European directive. The large production sharing agreements are operated to international standards, and the requirement that reaches site is the operator equipment specification.

Azerbaijani steps sit alongside it. Imported equipment can need national conformity certification, and radio equipment needs approval before it is used on local networks; the partner confirms which apply to each shipment. SOCAR and the international operators both favour suppliers working through a locally registered company, and documentation in Azerbaijani, Russian and English is expected.

Ask about Azerbaijan →
Is the EX-04Z1 approved for use in Azerbaijan yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and the IECEx certification programme is in progress, as is every other scheme. Nothing should be planned into a classified area until the Certificate of Conformity exists.

Which certificate will a Caspian operator ask to see?

The IECEx Certificate of Conformity in almost every case, because the large projects here are specified to international standards. An ATEX certificate is issued in parallel where a specification names the European directive.

Does it need national approval in Azerbaijan?

Imported equipment can need national conformity certification, and the radios need approval for use on local networks. The partner confirms which apply to each shipment and handles them as part of delivery.

Can it be used offshore?

It is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, which covers the classified areas on a Caspian production platform, with a marking target of Ex ib IIC T4. Operator management of change still applies.

Is documentation available in Russian or Azerbaijani?

The user manual and safe use instructions will be supplied in Russian and English, and MDM profiles can be configured in Azerbaijani, Russian or English.

When will units be available, and who repairs them?

Launch is Q4 2026. Warranty and service run through the appointed Azerbaijani partner, and repair of certified Ex equipment must be done by a recognised facility.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Azerbaijan?

No list price is published before the certificates exist. Pricing is quoted in writing against quantity and configuration through the partner, and landed cost depends on import and approval steps, so it is set with the channel rather than published.
