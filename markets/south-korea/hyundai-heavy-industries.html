Why it fits

Designed for the world's largest shipbuilder's hazardous-area work

Hyundai Heavy Industries' scale and its LNG-carrier specialisation create a volume and type of hazardous-area work that a standard refinery's device evaluation doesn't need to account for.

Confined-space work at unmatched shipbuilding volume

As the world's largest shipbuilder, Hyundai Heavy Industries runs confined-space tank construction and hot-work operations across more vessels simultaneously than almost any other yard globally, a volume that puts a premium on standardised, easily deployed communications equipment for its workforce.

LNG carrier specialisation brings cryogenic commissioning expertise

Building and commissioning LNG carriers' membrane containment systems is a core specialism at Ulsan, work that combines cryogenic exposure with flammable-gas testing protocols distinct from a refinery's steady-state process hazards.

A project-based hazard map that shifts continuously

Unlike a fixed refinery classified-area layout, Hyundai Heavy Industries' yard has different vessels at different construction stages simultaneously, meaning hazardous-area work locations shift week to week across the yard rather than remaining fixed.

Part of the HD Hyundai group alongside Hyundai Oilbank

Shared group ownership with Hyundai Oilbank means HD Hyundai's broader procurement relationships can sometimes span both businesses, even though shipbuilding and refining present entirely different hazard profiles.

Fleet management across a large, multi-vessel shipyard workforce

EMM and MDM enrolment supports Hyundai Heavy Industries managing devices across a large workforce moving between multiple vessels under construction at different stages.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Hyundai Heavy Industries yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is shipyard hazardous-area work certified the same way as a refinery?

The underlying Ex certification basis is similar, but shipyard hazards are typically project-based and tied to a vessel's construction stage rather than a fixed, permanent classified-area layout.

Does the device suit LNG carrier cryogenic commissioning work?

Environmental testing addresses general cold-exposure durability; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued, and site HSE teams should confirm applicability to specific commissioning procedures.

Is Hyundai Heavy Industries the same company as Hyundai Oilbank?

No, they are separate operating companies within the HD Hyundai group, each with its own procurement process and entirely different hazard profiles.
