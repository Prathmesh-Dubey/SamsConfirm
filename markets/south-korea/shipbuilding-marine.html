Why it fits shipbuilding and marine

Built for shipyard hazardous-area work, not continuous-process refining

LNG carrier and tanker construction puts flammable-atmosphere and confined-space hazards into a project-based, ship-by-ship work pattern rather than a fixed process plant's stable classified-area layout.

Confined-space gas hazards during tank construction

Building and testing LNG containment tanks and cargo hold structures involves confined-space work where flammable-gas or oxygen-deficient atmospheres can develop during welding, coating and hot-work operations, a hazard profile distinct from an open industrial process unit.

Cryogenic membrane tank commissioning

LNG carrier construction includes commissioning cryogenic membrane containment systems, work that combines low-temperature exposure with the flammable-gas testing protocols used before a tank is declared gas-free or ready for cargo.

A project-based, not fixed-site, hazard pattern

Unlike a refinery's permanent classified-area map, a shipyard's hazardous-area work follows each vessel through its construction sequence, meaning the same yard has different hazard zones active on different ships at different times.

Marine fuel-system commissioning and sea trials

Testing and commissioning a vessel's own fuel systems, particularly on LNG-fuelled ships, brings a further layer of gas-hazard work during the final stages before delivery.

Fleet management across a large, multi-vessel shipyard workforce

EMM and MDM enrolment supports a shipbuilder managing devices across a large workforce moving between multiple vessels under construction at different stages.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for shipyard use in South Korea yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including IECEx, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is shipyard hazardous-area work the same as a refinery's classified areas?

No, shipyard hazards are typically project-based and tied to a specific vessel's construction stage, such as confined-space tank work, rather than a fixed, permanent classified-area layout.

Does the device suit cryogenic tank commissioning work?

Environmental testing addresses general cold-exposure durability; the certificate will state its exact scope once issued, and site HSE teams should confirm applicability to specific cryogenic commissioning procedures.

Is Hyundai Heavy Industries the only shipbuilder in Korea?

It is the world's largest and Korea's dominant shipbuilder; other Korean shipbuilding groups also operate in the sector.
