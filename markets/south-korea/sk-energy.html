Why it fits

Designed for a chaebol-owned refinery with a six-decade history

SK Energy's position within the SK Group conglomerate and its refinery's long operating history both shape how new equipment gets evaluated for use on site.

A chaebol group's own engineering ecosystem

As part of SK Group, one of Korea's largest business conglomerates, SK Energy's procurement decisions can be informed by relationships and standards shared across the wider group's other industrial businesses, not solely by the refining unit's own standalone history.

Six decades of incremental refinery expansion

Ulsan has expanded in phases since the 1960s, meaning classified-area documentation spans a wide range of construction eras, a mixed-vintage layout the EX-04Z1's broad Zone 1 rating is designed to sit across without requiring area-by-area exceptions.

One of Asia's largest single-site refining complexes

Ulsan's scale means SK Energy's device fleet decisions touch a workforce and contractor base larger than many other Asian refineries manage at one location, a scale EMM and MDM fleet management is built to support.

Zone 1 gas across a long-established process footprint

Crude distillation, catalytic units and tank farms at Ulsan are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 covers the hydrocarbon gas groups typical of refining service.

Fleet management for a large, long-operating single site

EMM and MDM enrolment suits managing devices across Ulsan's extensive, multi-era refinery infrastructure from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at SK Energy sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does SK Group's wider conglomerate structure affect vendor evaluation?

It can mean procurement relationships developed elsewhere in the SK Group ecosystem are relevant context, though SK Energy runs its own site-level engineering review.

Does the device suit Ulsan's mixed-vintage refinery infrastructure?

Yes, it is designed to be usable across classified areas built at different points across the refinery's six decades of expansion.

Is SK Energy the same company as S-Oil?

No, both operate refineries in the Ulsan area but are separate companies with entirely different ownership and procurement processes.
