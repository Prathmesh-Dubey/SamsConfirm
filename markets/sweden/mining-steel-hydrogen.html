The sector · Norrbotten to the Baltic

Mining, steel and hydrogen: where Sweden classifies areas

This sector is hard to classify as a whole because the risk is not spread evenly. A mine is mostly a mining-safety problem with a few classified spots. A steelworks is a large gas network with dust. A hydrogen project is a small, new area with the most demanding gas group. The common thread is that each of them is changing: LKAB is moving from pellets towards hydrogen-reduced sponge iron, SSAB is replacing blast furnaces with electric arc furnaces, and new projects at Boden and Luleå are bringing hydrogen to sites that never had it. A handset bought for today's classification drawings should still be correct when those drawings are redrawn. See the operator pages for an explosion proof phone for LKAB and an explosion proof phone for SSAB.

Gas and dust in one footprint

Zone 1 sits beside Zone 21 in a steelworks and a pellet plant. One handset designed for both avoids issuing a second device for the dust area.

Group IIC for the transition

Hydrogen is group IIC. Specifying IIC at the outset avoids replacement when an electrolyser or hydrogen line is added to a site classified for lower groups.

Cold, remote and spread out

Norrbotten sites have long winters and long distances. rugged build covers low temperature, and the 4,000 mAh battery is sized for a shift away from a charger.

Portable credentials, managed centrally

Android Enterprise and the existing MDM console let a contractor pool be enrolled and wiped by operator, so a handset can travel between sites without carrying one operator's data into another.

Work and approval

Contractors, project sites and the approval step

Work in this sector comes in waves: construction of new capacity, then commissioning, then long periods of operation with periodic outages. Contractors move between operators during each wave. For a safety manager the useful question is whether the same device and the same enrolment route work at every site, not whether each site buys its own.

Operators set their own requirements for equipment suppliers and this page does not claim any relationship with any of them. Ask each operator's procurement and HSE teams what a supplier file needs. The EX-04Z1 certificates have not been issued yet.

Questions · Mining, steel and hydrogen

Frequently asked questions

Which Swedish rules govern equipment in mines and steelworks?

ATEX 2014/34/EU covers the equipment through ELSÄK-FS 2016:2 for electrical products. Underground work adds the mining inspectorate. See the Sweden page for the regulatory route.

Can one handset cover both a mine surface plant and a hydrogen plant?

That is the design aim. The target of Zone 1 and Zone 21 with group IIC covers both, but the classification drawing for each location decides, so send us the zone, group and temperature class.

How is a contractor pool managed across operators?

Through the MDM console. The handset is enrolled, restricted to approved applications and wiped at the end of the contract, so one pool of devices serves several sites.
