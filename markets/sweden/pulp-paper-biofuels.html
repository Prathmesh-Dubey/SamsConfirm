The sector · Mills and biorefineries

Wood dust and process gas in Swedish pulp mills

A kraft mill is a chemical plant built around a wood yard. Chips and bark generate combustible dust from the yard to the boilers. The cooking and recovery side generates sulphur-bearing gases, methanol and turpentine, and a lime kiln needs fuel gas or oil. Södra's Värö mill south of Gothenburg is one of the large softwood pulp mills in Sweden and delivers tall oil, bark and turpentine as by-products. SCA's mills at Östrand, Obbola and Munksund produce tall oil too, and the Sunpine biorefinery at Piteå upgrades crude tall oil into a fuel feedstock. That second product line brings oil-handling hazards into a plant that started with wood. For the west-coast refining side see an explosion proof phone for refining and petrochemicals.

Dust as the main hazard

In a mill, Zone 21 is as important as Zone 1. The EX-04Z1 is designed for both, so a technician moves between chip handling and the recovery area without changing devices.

Steam, wet floors and chemical splash

A mill contains liquor, bleach chemicals and steam. IP68 sealing keeps the handset working through wash-down, and the temperature class on the certificate is what matters near hot process lines.

Annual shutdowns with large crews

A mill stops once a year for a maintenance outage with hundreds of contractors. Android Enterprise enrolment lets the site issue handsets by team and wipe them afterwards.

Remote inland sites

Many mills are in forest regions with limited public coverage. Managed through the existing MDM console, the handset joins the site network rather than relying on public cover.

Work and approval

Shutdowns, contractors and the approval step

A mill's year is organised around its shutdown, with a large temporary workforce on top of the permanent staff. For the rest of the year, shift crews do rounds across a wide site, often with one operator covering a long route. A handset that works in both dust and gas areas is therefore more valuable than a second dedicated device.

Each operator sets its own requirements for equipment suppliers and this page does not claim any relationship with any company named. Ask the mill's procurement and HSE teams what a supplier file needs. The EX-04Z1 certificates have not been issued yet.

Questions · Pulp, paper and biofuels

Frequently asked questions

Is wood dust really a Zone 21 hazard?

Yes, where it can form a cloud or settle in layers, as in chip handling, bark fuel and sander dust. The classification drawing sets the zone and the EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 21.

Does the sulphur-bearing gas in a kraft mill change the specification?

It is toxic as well as flammable. The phone is designed not to ignite it but does not detect it, so personal gas monitors remain necessary.

Can the same handset be used at a biorefinery?

The target is Zone 1 and Zone 21, which covers flammable liquid areas and dust, subject to the classification drawing and temperature class at the site.
