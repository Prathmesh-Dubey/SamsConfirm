Why it fits

Designed for an upstream-and-pipeline business at national scale

PetroChina's combination of upstream field operations and a nationwide pipeline network creates two very different day-to-day use patterns for the same corporate device standard.

Zone 1 gas at both wellfields and pipeline infrastructure

Wellhead, gathering and processing facilities alongside pipeline compressor and metering stations are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and the device's intrinsic-safety design targets the hydrocarbon gas groups common to both.

Remote field operations far from major cities

Many of PetroChina's producing fields sit in remote parts of the country, far enough from urban centres that a damaged handset is not a same-day replacement, favouring IP68 sealing, MIL-STD-810H construction and long battery life.

A vast pipeline network with distributed maintenance crews

The pipeline transmission network requires maintenance and inspection crews to travel between stations spread across the country, a distributed workforce for whom standardised, rugged equipment reduces logistics complexity.

Continental climate extremes

PetroChina's fields and pipeline routes span regions with harsh winters and significant temperature swings, environmental conditions the device's testing regime is specified to withstand.

Fleet management at national scale

EMM and MDM enrolment supports PetroChina managing a device fleet across upstream fields and pipeline infrastructure spanning the country from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at PetroChina sites yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and NEPSI certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit PetroChina's remote field operations?

Ruggedness and battery life are specified with that isolation in mind, though resupply logistics should always be planned for independently of device durability.

Does one device cover both wellfield and pipeline operations?

Yes, the Zone 1 specification is designed to cover both, so crews moving between field production and pipeline maintenance work do not need separate devices.

Is PetroChina the same company as CNPC?

PetroChina is the publicly listed operating subsidiary of China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC), the parent state company.
