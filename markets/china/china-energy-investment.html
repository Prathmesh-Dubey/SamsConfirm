The sites · China Energy

Where an explosion proof mobile phone fits across China Energy's coal and power sites in China

The chain starts at the mine. Shenhua's large coal operations include the Shendong coalfield on the Shaanxi and Inner Mongolia border, the open-pit mines of the Zhunge'er area such as Heidaigou and Haerwusu in Inner Mongolia, and Baorixile in Hulunbuir. Coal then moves by rail, including the Shuohuang line to Huanghua port in Hebei, to power stations and ports. The coal chemical side includes direct and indirect liquefaction and olefins plants in Ordos, Baotou and Ningdong. The Guodian side adds a large fleet of coal-fired, gas and renewable power stations. Each stage has a different hazard. Surface coal handling, crushing, conveyors, transfer towers and bunkers are combustible dust areas, Zone 21 or Zone 22 in the engineer's terms. Pulverised coal mills and boiler fuel systems add explosion risk. Coal chemical plants bring hydrogen, synthesis gas and methanol, which are gas matters, and generators are cooled with hydrogen. Underground coal faces are a separate regime: they are mining equipment, Group I, with their own safety mark, and the EX-04Z1 design target is Zone 1 and Zone 21, Group II.

Coal dust that settles on everything

Coal dust layers on surfaces insulate heat and can ignite or be thrown into a cloud. Surface temperature class matters as much as protection concept, and the T-class on the certificate is what the dust engineer checks against the site classification.

Open pits that are kilometres across

Heidaigou-scale pits and the haul roads around them mean long distances between points. Four-constellation GNSS records where an inspection or fault was found, and the 50 MP camera records what it looked like.

Cold, windy continental sites

Inner Mongolia and Shaanxi have severe winters and strong wind. Cold shortens battery life, and the certificate will state the temperature range, so check it against your coldest shift.

Two engineering cultures in one group

Mining and power engineering teams may still apply different specification habits. A single design target that covers both Zone 21 dust and Zone 1 gas lets one handset serve either, once certified.

Contractors in power and chemical shutdowns

A power station outage or a coal chemical turnaround brings contractors in numbers. Enrolling handsets in an existing MDM console lets the plant restrict applications and the camera by crew.

How work is organised

Operating pattern and approvals at China Energy

Mines run continuously on shift patterns, with maintenance planned into scheduled stoppages. Power stations plan outages around grid demand, and coal chemical plants run long campaigns between turnarounds. Because the group combines mining and power, equipment questions can come from different engineering teams, and the right first question is which business unit owns the classified area. We have not verified a published supplier-registration programme at China Energy and do not name one. Ask the business unit's procurement and HSE teams which Ex certificates they accept, whether mining-specific marks apply to the area in question, and whether supply must run through a China-registered partner. NEPSI is among the certification routes in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Questions · China Energy

Frequently asked questions

Can the EX-04Z1 be used underground in a China Energy mine?

Not on the current design target. Underground coal equipment falls under mining rules, Group I, with its own safety mark. The EX-04Z1 is being certified for Zone 1 and Zone 21, so underground use would need separate certification.

Does the 2017 combination mean one procurement process?

Not necessarily. Mining, chemical and power businesses may still run their own evaluations, so confirm which unit owns the site before assuming a group-wide route.

Is China Energy the same as Yankuang?

No. Yankuang is a separate coal group in Shandong with its own procurement, and it is covered on the coal mining page.

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for China Energy sites yet?

No. It is pre-launch, certification is in progress and no approval exists at any China Energy business. The launch follows the certificates.
