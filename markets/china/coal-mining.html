Why it fits coal mining

Built for the dual methane-and-dust hazard of underground coal

Underground coal mining is historically one of the highest-consequence hazardous environments for equipment certification, combining flammable methane gas with combustible coal dust in confined underground workings.

Methane gas and coal dust together, not one or the other

Underground coal mines generate both flammable methane and combustible coal dust, and China's mining-specific certification requirements (administered through NEPSI and China's coal-mine safety regulator) address this combined hazard distinctly from a surface industrial site's single-hazard classification.

Underground versus surface operations

Shanxi and Shaanxi host extensive underground mining while Inner Mongolia includes significant surface (open-pit) coal production, two different working environments where a device needs to perform reliably in confined underground workings as well as open-air dust conditions.

A safety record that has driven some of the world's strictest mine equipment rules

China's coal-mining safety regulations, developed in response to a long history of underground gas and dust incidents, are among the most demanding equipment certification regimes globally, and devices reaching this sector need documentation that speaks directly to those mining-specific standards.

Remote mining regions with harsh continental climate

Shanxi, Shaanxi and Inner Mongolia experience cold winters and significant temperature swings, conditions the device's environmental testing is specified to withstand alongside its underground and dust-hazard ratings.

Fleet management across a large, multi-region mining sector

EMM and MDM enrolment supports an operator managing devices across multiple mining regions and both underground and surface operations from one console.

Questions · Coal Mining

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for coal mining use in China yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including NEPSI and mining-specific approval, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Is coal mine certification different from general Ex certification?

Yes, underground coal mining in China has its own dedicated safety certification regime addressing the combined methane and coal-dust hazard, distinct from a general industrial Zone 1 or Zone 21 rating.

Does the device suit both underground and surface coal mining?

The design intent is to cover both, though underground coal-mine certification carries additional mining-specific requirements beyond a general dust or gas rating.

Does one device work across Shanxi, Shaanxi and Inner Mongolia?

Yes, in principle, since the certification and environmental testing target conditions common across China's major coal-mining regions.
