Why it fits

Designed for an iron-ore operator running its own port and rail

Shougang Hierro Peru's operation spans open-pit mining, its own railway and a dedicated port terminal, a wider footprint than a single mine site and different hazard profile than a copper concentrator.

Iron ore, not copper, changes the dust and process profile

Marcona's magnetite ore and associated processing generate a different particulate and handling environment than the copper concentrators covered elsewhere on this page, relevant to how zone 21 dust classification applies across the site.

A self-contained mine-rail-port operation

Few Peruvian operators run their own railway and marine export terminal alongside the mine itself, which means field communications need to work consistently across pit, rail corridor and port-side hazardous areas, not just a single classified zone.

A second Chinese state-owned parent in this market, different metal

Like Minera Chinalco Peru, Shougang Hierro Peru answers to a Chinese state-owned parent (Shougang Group), but the commodity, processing route and port logistics are entirely different, so the two should not be treated as a single buying decision.

Coastal desert environment at San Juan de Marcona

The same salt-air and fine desert sand conditions that shape Mina Justa's requirements apply here too, since Marcona's port and pit sit in the same coastal Ica desert.

One of Peru's longest-running mining operations

Marcona has operated for decades under different owners before Shougang's acquisition, meaning the site has entrenched procurement habits a new vendor needs to work within.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Marcona yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and the IECEx certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device cover port-side hazardous areas as well as the mine?

The zone 1/21 specification is designed for classified areas generally; port-specific area classification should be confirmed against Shougang Hierro Peru's own site documentation.

Is this the same company as Minera Chinalco Peru?

No, both have Chinese state-owned parents but they are separate operators mining different commodities, iron ore versus copper, with independent procurement processes.

Does the coastal desert environment affect the device?

IP68 sealing is specified to address salt-laden coastal air and fine desert sand common to Peru's southern coastal mining sites.
