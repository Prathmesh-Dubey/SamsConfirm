The site · Talara

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed at Petroperú

The new Talara refinery was designed to process 95,000 barrels a day, against about 65,000 for the plant it replaced, and was inaugurated in December 2023. Its start-up has not been smooth: reports in 2024 said the flexicoking unit had stopped working correctly in March of that year, and in 2025 the prime minister acknowledged the refinery was not yet at full capacity. So Talara is best described as a new plant being brought up to rate, not a refinery still under construction. For a handset, a ramp-up means start-ups, shutdowns and repairs on process units, with larger and more frequent contractor presence than a steady-state plant. Hydroprocessing units that handle hydrogen, crude and product tanks, a marine terminal and the loading of LPG all bring flammable atmospheres, and hydrogen belongs to gas group IIC, the group the EX-04Z1 is designed to address.

What Talara does to a handset

Talara is a coastal desert site with strong sun, wind-blown sand and salt air, and it is a big refinery with long distances between units. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1, with Ex ib IIC T4 as its marking target; certification is in progress and no certificate has yet been issued.

A refinery being brought up to rate

Start-up problems mean frequent shutdowns, restarts and repairs. Contractors and permit-to-work paperwork multiply, and a managed handset carrying permits and isolation lists helps.

Hydrogen and the IIC gas group

Hydroprocessing units use hydrogen, which sits in the most demanding gas group. The device's IIC design target is relevant where those units are classified, and the certificate will list the groups it covers.

Sun, sand and salt

The north coast is dry, sunny and salty. IP68 sealing helps against sand and spray, and screen readability in glare should be part of any trial.

Long walks inside the fence

A refinery of this size means long rounds between units and no charging inside the process area. The 4,000 mAh cell is specified for a full shift.

A state company's public accountability

As a state-owned company handling a high-profile project, Petroperú's decisions face outside scrutiny. Well-documented, internationally certified equipment is easier to defend.

Questions · Petroperú

Frequently asked questions

Is the Talara refinery still being modernised?

The modernisation project has been completed in the sense that the new refinery was inaugurated in December 2023, but public reports say it has not yet reached full capacity. Check the current status with Petroperú before assuming any unit is old or new.

Does Petroperú run refineries other than Talara?

Its operations have included smaller refineries elsewhere in Peru and the Northern Peruvian Pipeline. Which of them are in service changes over time, so confirm with the company.

Does hydrogen change the device requirement?

Hydrogen is in gas group IIC, so a device used where hydrogen can be present needs to be certified for group IIC. The EX-04Z1's marking target is Ex ib IIC T4, subject to the certificate.

Can a supplier register with Petroperú now?

Registration processes are for Petroperú to describe, so ask its procurement and HSE teams. No SAMS certificate has been issued yet, so a trial in classified areas has to follow certification.
