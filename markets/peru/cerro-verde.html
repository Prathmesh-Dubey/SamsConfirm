The site · Cerro Verde

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed at Cerro Verde

Cerro Verde is an unusually large operation by Peruvian standards. Shareholder filings describe a processing capacity above half a million tonnes of ore a day, of which more than 400,000 tonnes go through the concentrator and the rest through leaching and solvent-extraction circuits. That gives the site two distinct families of hazardous area. The concentrator has dry-ore and concentrate handling where combustible dust can build up, plus reagent stores for flotation. The leach and electrowinning side has solvent extraction, where the organic phase is a flammable liquid and the plant layout is built around keeping vapour away from ignition sources. Add fuel for a large haul fleet and the molybdenum-bearing streams that come from a porphyry orebody, and the result is a site where a person can cross several classified boundaries in a single round, without leaving the grounds or going anywhere near a remote camp.

Power and substations
Mostly outside classification, but with fuel-fired standby plant and battery rooms that need review.

What Cerro Verde does to a handset

Cerro Verde is closer to a city than the other big copper mines in the country, which changes the problems. The altitude is far lower than the Apurímac and Cusco sites, so battery and cold are less of a concern. The problems here are scale, dust, and the number of different classified areas a technician crosses in one shift. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21, and certification is in progress.

Dust and spray in an arid valley

The pit and the concentrator sit in a dry desert valley where wind lifts fine dust and the process adds mist and spray. IP68 sealing protects connectors and speakers in both conditions, so a quick wash between areas is practical.

A large site walked on foot

With a concentrator of this size, a round between the grinding lines and the thickeners is a long walk and charging inside the building is not an option. The 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a full shift without a recharge.

Two hazard families in one pocket

A technician can start the shift in a dust area and finish at a solvent-extraction plant. A Zone 1 and Zone 21 design target avoids carrying two different devices, though the operator's classification study decides what is allowed where.

A parent company that standardises

Freeport-McMoRan operates mines across several continents and standardises where it can. Android Enterprise and existing MDM consoles let the Peruvian IT team apply the same policies it applies elsewhere, with the camera and location switched according to area.

Contractor turnover during shutdowns

A site of this size brings in hundreds of contractors for a mill shutdown. Enrolling, issuing and wiping handsets centrally keeps the register accurate when crews change every few weeks.

Questions · Cerro Verde

Frequently asked questions

Does Freeport-McMoRan's approval of a device elsewhere apply at Cerro Verde?

No. Each operating company runs its own review and holds its own records. Documentation prepared to a standard the parent company recognises can make an evaluation easier, but the Peruvian site decides, against Peruvian regulation and its own area classification.

Is solvent extraction a Zone 1 area?

Classification depends on the plant design, the diluent used and the ventilation. That is for the operator's classification study to say. Where flammable liquid is handled, a device with an Ex certificate for the relevant gas group is generally what is required.

Cerro Verde is near Arequipa: does that change how a supplier supports it?

Yes. A city of that size gives easier access to logistics, couriers and engineers than the Apurímac or Cusco sites, which helps with spares, repairs and training. It does not change the certification requirement.

Can we trial a unit at Cerro Verde now?

No unit is certified yet; IECEx, ATEX and any local route are in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. An evaluation can start with datasheets and documents, and hands-on use in classified areas follows the certificates.
