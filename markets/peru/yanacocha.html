Why it fits

Designed for a US-listed major under sustained public and regulatory scrutiny

Yanacocha's history of community and environmental scrutiny means Newmont's procurement process places unusual weight on documentation completeness and public defensibility, not just technical fit.

A US-listed major's disclosure standard

As a New York Stock Exchange-listed company, Newmont's procurement and safety documentation follow disclosure norms that favour a vendor able to produce a complete, verifiable certification trail rather than partial or informal assurance.

Heightened scrutiny from a contested expansion history

Yanacocha's Conga expansion project drew sustained public and environmental opposition, which means Newmont's Peru operations run under more visible outside attention than most mines in the region, and vendor choices are made with that visibility in mind.

Northern Andes altitude and dust, a different corridor from the south

Cajamarca sits in Peru's northern highlands, a separate logistics and altitude corridor from the southern copper belt (Cuajone, Constancia, Toromocho), affecting which distributor can realistically support the site.

One of the longest-operating mines in Peru

Yanacocha has run since 1993, giving it one of the most established and conservative vendor-approval cultures among Peru's major mines, where a new device is more likely to enter through a formal trial than a fast adoption.

Gold rather than copper or iron processing

Yanacocha's cyanide heap-leach gold processing creates a different chemical hazard profile than the copper concentrators and iron ore handling covered elsewhere on this page, relevant to the specific zone classification a device needs to satisfy on site.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for use at Yanacocha yet?

No. The device is pre-launch, and the IECEx certification is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does Newmont's scrutiny mean a longer approval process?

Vendor evaluation at Yanacocha tends to be documentation-heavy given the operator's public profile, so a complete certification trail matters more here than at a lower-profile site.

Does the device suit Cajamarca's northern Andes conditions?

Environmental testing addresses high-altitude conditions typical of the Peruvian Andes generally; Cajamarca-specific confirmation is always advisable given its distinct northern climate from the southern copper belt.

Is Yanacocha the same company as Antamina or Southern Copper?

No, Yanacocha is a gold mine operated by Newmont, a separate company from the copper operators covered elsewhere on this site, with its own procurement process.
