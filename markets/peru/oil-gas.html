Sector · Oil and gas

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed in Peruvian oil and gas

Camisea is the centre of the sector. A consortium operated by Pluspetrol holds Blocks 88 and 56, gas is carried from the jungle to the Malvinas processing plant near the fields, and liquids go to Pluspetrol's fractionation plant and marine terminal at Pisco, where products such as naphtha, propane, butane and diesel are extracted. Gas also goes by pipeline towards the coast and to the Peru LNG liquefaction plant at Pampa Melchorita, which has been operated by Hunt Oil with partners. The result is a chain with very different working conditions at each end: jungle wellpads and a processing plant reached by river and air, a pipeline corridor, a coastal fractionation site and an LNG terminal. Downstream, refining is dominated by Petroperú, and the Talara basin on the north coast has its own oil operators. Everything here is Zone 1 or Zone 2 gas, which suits a handset marked Ex ib IIC T4.

What the sector does to a handset

The two ends of the Camisea chain could hardly be more different: rainforest humidity at the fields, desert coast at Pisco and Melchorita. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and has IP68 sealing; certification is in progress and no certificate has yet been issued.

Rainforest humidity and heat

Camisea's fields are in the Amazon foothills, hot and wet for most of the year. IP68 sealing is relevant to humidity, rain and spray, and a battery sized for a shift matters when recharging is far away.

Remote logistics by river and air

Equipment reaches the fields by river or by air. A failed handset cannot be swapped in an hour, so durability, spares and distributor support are part of the evaluation.

Gas groups across the chain

Natural gas, propane, butane and naphtha belong to different gas groups, and LNG adds low-temperature handling. The certificate and the site's own classification define where the device is allowed.

International operators with their own standards

Consortium members and contractors bring international practice. Documentation that is complete, in Spanish and English, makes cross-company evaluation easier.

Coastal conditions at Pisco and Melchorita

Desert coast sites add salt and sand to the list. The same handset is intended for both ends, though trials should include a coastal site.

Questions · Oil and gas

Frequently asked questions

Does one device cover both Camisea and the coast?

The same Zone 1 design target is intended to serve upstream, processing and terminals. The certificate's gas groups and temperature class decide what is allowed at each site.

Is the Peru LNG plant a Zone 1 area?

Parts of an LNG plant are classified for gas, with the exact zones set by its own classification study. A supplier should ask for the area drawings before proposing any equipment.

Who operates Camisea?

Pluspetrol is operator of the consortium that holds Blocks 88 and 56, according to published industry sources. Ownership among consortium members has changed over time, so verify with the operator.

Can we evaluate a unit before launch?

Not in classified areas: no certificate has been issued and certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. Evaluation starts with the datasheet and certification schedule.
