Why it fits oil and gas

Built for jungle gas fields and coastal refining alike

Camisea's Amazon-foothill gas production and Petroperú's coastal refineries present very different working environments under one Zone 1 gas-hazard umbrella.

Zone 1 gas at both ends of the value chain

Camisea's gathering and processing facilities and Petroperú's refinery process units and tank farms are classified Zone 1 gas atmospheres, and Ex ib IIC T4 intrinsic safety covers the hydrocarbon gas groups present at both.

Amazon foothill logistics unlike anywhere else in the region

Camisea's gas fields sit in dense jungle terrain accessible mainly by river and air rather than road, an isolation that raises the operational cost of equipment failure and makes ruggedness and battery life more consequential than at a road-accessible site.

High humidity and heat at Camisea versus arid coastal refining

Camisea's tropical rainforest environment brings sustained high humidity and heat, a very different environmental exposure from the dry coastal desert climate around Talara, and the device's sealing and environmental testing are specified to handle both.

A refinery modernisation programme underway at Talara

Petroperú has been investing heavily in modernising the Talara refinery, meaning classified-area layouts there include both older infrastructure and newly commissioned units side by side during the transition.

Fleet management across a geographically split sector

EMM and MDM enrolment supports operators managing devices consistently across jungle gas-field and coastal refining operations from one console.

Questions · Oil & Gas

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for oil and gas use in Peru yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification, including IECEx, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does the device suit Camisea's jungle environment?

Environmental testing addresses sustained high humidity and heat, though site-specific logistics for a remote jungle location should always be planned for independently of device durability.

Does one device cover both Camisea and Talara?

Yes, the same Zone 1, Ex ib IIC T4 specification is intended to serve both upstream gas facilities and downstream refinery operations.

Is Petroperú the only refiner in Peru?

Petroperú operates Talara and other state refining assets; other smaller private refining operations also exist in the country.
