The site · Mina Justa

Where an explosion proof mobile phone is needed at Mina Justa

Mina Justa has two processing routes, which is what makes its hazardous areas unusual. Project descriptions give a 6 million tonne a year copper sulphide concentrator and a 12 million tonne a year vat-leach oxide plant with solvent extraction and electrowinning, working through two open pits and designed for about 16 years of mine life. Production was scheduled to start in 2021. Water comes from seawater desalination, which is notable in a desert. The concentrator handles dry ore and concentrate, so combustible dust is the question there. The oxide plant uses solvent extraction, where an organic diluent is a flammable liquid and the plant is laid out around that fact. Marcobre is a newer operation, so the equipment fleet and approved supplier list are still being built, and a supplier with complete documentation arrives at a better moment than at a site with decades of incumbent contracts.

What Mina Justa does to a handset

Mina Justa is at sea level, so the battery questions that dominate the Andean mines mostly disappear. What replaces them is salt, sand and heat. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and has IP68 sealing; certification is in progress and no certificate has yet been issued.

Corrosive coastal air

Salt carried inland corrodes connectors, speaker grilles and housings over months, not weeks. IP68 sealing and the usual practice of rinsing a handset after exposure are the sensible starting points for an evaluation.

Fine desert sand

The Ica desert produces wind-blown sand finer than most mine dust. A fully sealed handset keeps it out of ports, and the Zone 21 design target applies where combustible dust accumulates.

Two plants, two hazard families

A technician may move from a dust-producing concentrator to a solvent-extraction plant in one shift. A device designed for both Zone 1 and Zone 21 reduces the number of items to carry, but the operator's drawings decide which area permits what.

A newer fleet and an open supplier list

With operations only recently established, Marcobre is still settling its preferred equipment. A supplier that can show a complete certificate and support route has a better chance than one proposing a late change.

Peru and Chile as reference points

With Peruvian and Chilean owners, the benchmark for what good looks like can come from both countries' mining practice. Documentation in Spanish and English helps both.

Questions · Marcobre

Frequently asked questions

Is Mina Justa's SX plant a Zone 1 area?

It depends on the design, the diluent flashpoint and the ventilation, and the operator's classification study decides. Where a flammable organic phase is handled, an Ex-certified device is generally required in the area.

Does the device need altitude derating at Mina Justa?

No altitude issue arises at sea level, so the high-Andes battery and cold questions are mostly absent. The environmental questions here are salt, sand and heat.

Is Marcobre related to Shougang Hierro Peru nearby?

They are separate companies in the same district, with different owners, commodities and procurement. A relationship with one does not carry over to the other.

Can a supplier join Marcobre's list before certification?

Registration and approval are for Marcobre to describe. Ask its procurement and HSE teams what they need and when. No SAMS certificate has been issued yet, so a trial in classified areas has to wait for it.
