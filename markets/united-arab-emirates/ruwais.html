The site · Ruwais

What is actually at Ruwais

Ruwais sits about 240 km west of Abu Dhabi city, on the coast near the Jebel Dhanna crude terminal, and it is a purpose built industrial city rather than a single plant. A refinery complex, polyolefin production, fertiliser plant, gas and NGL processing, a chemicals zone under construction and the export berths share one perimeter, one road network and one permit culture. Crews cross between them, contractors mobilise into them for turnarounds, and the accommodation city sits alongside. That is why equipment selection here is a site wide question rather than a plant by plant one.

What Ruwais does to a handset

Two things dominate here: a site big enough that walking it is a shift in itself, and a hazard picture that changes from gas to dust and from group IIA to group IIC within a few hundred metres. The EX-04Z1 is designed against both, and against the coastal climate that wears equipment out faster than the inland fields do.

Group IIC, because hydrogen is on site

A device rated only for the propane group would be fine at the crude units and wrong at the hydrogen plants. The ATEX marking target is II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4, which is the group and temperature class an instrumentation engineer at Ruwais will check first.

Gas and dust on the same walk

A technician can leave a Zone 1 hydrocarbon area and enter a Zone 21 polymer or urea dust area on the same permit. The design covers Zone 1 and Zone 21 together, so one specification and one issued device follow the person rather than the plant.

Coastal heat, not dry heat

Ruwais runs above 45 °C in summer with Gulf humidity and salt behind it. MIL-STD-810H covers temperature, humidity and salt fog, and IP68 sealing is what survives the washdowns a refinery and a jetty both apply.

A complex you cross on foot

Stores is not five minutes away from a far corner of the complex. A 4,000 mAh battery sized for a full shift and a sealed body that never needs opening in the field are what keep a unit working from gate to gate.

Contract crews that change every turnaround

Ruwais draws in large contractor populations for shutdowns. Android 16 with enterprise management lets a telecoms team enrol a loaned fleet, restrict the camera by policy, and wipe and recover units when the crew demobilises.

Questions · Ruwais

Frequently asked questions

Can we buy the EX-04Z1 for Ruwais today?

No. It is pre-launch, with a Q4 2026 launch date, and the IECEx, ATEX and NEC 500 certifications are all still in progress, as is UAE conformity registration. What we can put in front of you now is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

How much does an explosion proof phone cost in the UAE?

We do not publish a list price before certification completes, because a price quoted against an unfinished certificate is not a price you can hold us to. Send the quantity, the areas and your project date and we will put pre-launch pricing in writing against that configuration. Supply into ADNOC assets runs through a registered UAE partner, and ICV standing affects how a bid is scored, so pricing is discussed alongside the channel.

Is it approved for ADNOC sites at Ruwais?

Operator approval is ADNOC’s to give and it follows the certificates rather than preceding them. The IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the document their engineering review works from, and supplier registration with a current In-Country Value certificate is the commercial half of the same question.

Does it cover the hydrogen areas as well as the crude units?

The design target is gas group IIC, which is the group hydrogen sits in, with a T4 temperature class and Zone 1 and Zone 21 equipment protection. The certificate, once issued, is what defines the permitted areas, and that is the document to put in your equipment selection case rather than this page.

Will it survive a Ruwais summer and the washdowns?

That is what IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing for temperature, humidity and salt fog are for. Even so, we would rather place an evaluation unit with a crew at Ruwais and let them prove it over a rotation than argue the point from a specification sheet.

Can the camera be locked down for site rules?

Yes. Android 16 with enterprise management lets your telecoms team disable or restrict the camera by policy, which is usually the condition attached to bringing any camera equipped device through a Ruwais gate. The same enrolment covers remote wipe when a contractor fleet demobilises.
