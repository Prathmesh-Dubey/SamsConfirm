Why it fits marine and terminals

Built for jetty and tank-farm conditions

Marine terminal work combines classified onshore tank farms with vessel-side loading operations in a humid, salt-laden environment.

Salt air and constant humidity

Coastal terminals at Jebel Ali and Fujairah run high humidity and salt exposure year-round. IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H construction are specified for continuous marine exposure, not occasional splash.

Zone 1 gas at loading arms and jetties

Loading arms, manifolds and tank farms are classified Zone 1 gas areas. Ex ib IIC T4 intrinsic safety is designed to meet that classification directly.

A device for both terminal and vessel-side work

Terminal operators and marine surveyors both need a handset that survives spray and humidity while remaining usable in gloves during cargo operations.

Full-shift battery for round-the-clock loading

Terminal operations run continuously, and the 4,000 mAh cell is specified to last a full shift without a recharge point on the jetty.

Where it works

UAE marine terminals

The UAE's major crude, product and LNG terminals sit along the Abu Dhabi and Fujairah coastlines.

Ruwais
ADNOC's refining and export terminal complex on the western Abu Dhabi coast.
Jebel Ali
One of the region's largest ports, handling bulk liquid and general cargo.
Fujairah
Major bunkering and crude storage hub on the UAE's east coast, outside the Strait of Hormuz.
Typical hazards
Loading arm and manifold gas release, tank farm vapour, and confined-space work during tank inspection.
