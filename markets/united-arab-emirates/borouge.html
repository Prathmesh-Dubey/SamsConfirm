Borouge's polyolefins complex at Ruwais combines Zone 1 feedstock gas handling with Zone 21 polymer dust in pelletising and bagging areas. The EX-04Z1 is designed to cover both without requiring two separate certified devices.

Why it fits

Designed for polymer production end to end

A polyolefins plant runs gas-handling crackers alongside dust-generating pelletising and bagging lines in one integrated site.

One device across gas and dust zones

Cracking and feedstock handling are Zone 1 gas areas; pelletising and bagging generate Zone 21 polymer dust. A single specification covers both without a second device.

Built for a world-scale single site

Borouge is among the largest single-site polyolefins producers anywhere, and a rugged, long-battery device suits its scale and contractor traffic.

IECEx as the documented basis

Borouge's engineering review is built on the IECEx Certificate of Conformity once issued.

The approval path

Two gates for Borouge

National conformity and Borouge's own vendor approval run on separate timelines.

National conformity
ECAS conformity marking, with an IECEx Certificate of Conformity as the technical basis for both Zone 1 and Zone 21 areas.
Vendor approval
A separate engineering review run by Borouge's own approved-vendor process.
Typical environments
Cracker units, polymerisation reactors, pelletising, bagging and bulk polymer storage.
Questions · Borouge

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 approved for Borouge sites?

No, not yet. National certification is a prerequisite for Borouge's own vendor review, and neither is complete.

Does it cover the plastic dust in pelletising areas?

Yes, the Zone 21 rating is designed specifically to cover combustible polymer dust alongside the plant's Zone 1 gas areas.
