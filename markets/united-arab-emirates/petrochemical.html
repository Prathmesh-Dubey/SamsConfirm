Why it fits petrochemicals

Built for polymer dust and gas together

Petrochemical production combines Zone 1 feedstock gas handling with Zone 21 polymer dust in pelletising and packaging areas.

Polymer dust alongside feedstock gas

Polyolefin pelletising and bagging generate combustible plastic dust, while upstream cracking and feedstock handling remain Zone 1 gas. One Zone 1 / Zone 21 specification covers both halves of the plant.

Built for a mega-site the scale of Ruwais

TA'ZIZ and Borouge together form one of the largest integrated chemical sites anywhere. A rugged, long-battery device suits the long rounds and heavy contractor traffic of a site at that scale.

Managed like the rest of a large estate

EMM and MDM enrolment lets an operator with thousands of units across a mega-site manage the fleet from one console.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for UAE petrochemical use yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch.

Does it cover polymer dust as well as gas?

Yes, the Zone 1 / Zone 21 specification is designed to cover both the gas-handling and polymer-dust areas of a petrochemical site.
