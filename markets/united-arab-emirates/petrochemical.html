The sector · Petrochemicals

UAE petrochemicals at Ruwais: Borouge, FERTIL and TA'ZIZ

Al Ruwais Industrial City holds a fertiliser plant run by Ruwais Fertilizer Industries and Borouge's petrochemical complex, alongside the refinery and fractionation plant. TA'ZIZ, a joint venture of ADNOC and ADQ, is building a new chemicals zone beside them. Its first phase is planned to reach 4.7 million tonnes a year by 2028, covering methanol, low-carbon ammonia, PVC, ethylene dichloride, vinyl chloride monomer and caustic soda, and several of those will be produced in the UAE for the first time. The methanol plant, a Proman partnership, has been awarded to Samsung E&A as EPC contractor. A site this size has three working patterns at once: live operations, construction, and commissioning, each with different crews and different classified boundaries.

Gas and dust on one site

Borouge combines Zone 1 gas with Zone 21 polymer dust, and a new zone adds more gases. One device designed for both, with the group and temperature class checked per area, simplifies issue to a mixed workforce.

Toxic gases need their own detectors

Ammonia, chlorine and vinyl chloride are toxic as well as flammable. Intrinsic safety addresses ignition, not toxicity, so personal gas monitors stay mandatory. The handset can pair with a detector over Bluetooth; it does not replace one.

Construction crews change classified areas

During commissioning, areas change status as systems are energised. Central enrolment lets the owner update device policy, restrict the camera and wipe units when a contractor demobilises.

Long rounds on a very large site

The 4,000 mAh battery is specified for a shift and four-constellation GNSS records position on a large, repetitive site where descriptions of location are unreliable.

The approval path

Two gates for petrochemicals

National conformity comes through MoIAT's ECAS-Ex scheme, built on IECEx certification, with TDRA approval for the radio. All of these are in progress and none is issued. Operator approvals then apply separately. Borouge's own supplier approval, ADNOC's registration and In-Country Value process and TA'ZIZ's procedures are not ours to describe, and we have not verified them; ask procurement. Questions · Petrochemical

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for UAE petrochemical use yet?

No. The device is pre-launch and certification is in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. SAMS has no approval, order or relationship with Borouge, TA'ZIZ or any other operator.

Does it cover polymer dust as well as gas?

It is designed to: the Zone 1 and Zone 21 target addresses gas and combustible dust. The dust group and temperature limit will be fixed on the certificate.

Does Ex certification make it safe in ammonia or chlorine areas?

No. Intrinsic safety deals with ignition. Toxic exposure is controlled by personal monitors, procedures and site rules.
