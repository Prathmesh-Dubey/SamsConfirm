Japan’s coastal industrial complexes combine humid summers, typhoon exposure and some of the most rigorous site engineering standards anywhere. These are the points a Japanese evaluator presses on, and how the design answers them.

Humidity, typhoons and salt air

Japan’s coastal petrochemical sites face heavy summer humidity, typhoon-season storms and salt-laden air. IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H construction are specified with that combined exposure in mind.

High site-engineering standards

Japanese operators are known for rigorous internal engineering review that often exceeds the certificate minimum. Build quality and documentation are prepared with that scrutiny in mind, not just the IECEx threshold.

A battery for large integrated-complex rounds

A round at a site the scale of Kashima or Yokkaichi covers real distance with no recharging point inside a classified area. The 4,000 mAh cell is specified for a full shift.

Japanese and English on one build

Operations run predominantly Japanese-language, with English needed for international contractors and technical documentation. Android 16 carries both interface languages on the same handset.

Managed like the rest of the fleet

EMM and MDM enrolment pushes the corporate profile and camera policy centrally, matching how large Japanese operators already manage their device estates.

Regulatory route · Japan

What your HSE and procurement teams will ask for.

Conformity in Japan runs through the Technology Institution of Industrial Safety (TIIS), the country’s Ex certification body, operating under the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) framework for industrial safety. For hazardous-area equipment the technical evidence behind that certification is typically aligned with an IECEx Certificate of Conformity, and because this product is a phone it also needs Giteki (Technical Regulation Conformity Certification) approval from the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications. None of the three is finished yet.

Getting past the plant gate is a separate exercise. ENEOS, Idemitsu and the major petrochemical operators each run their own detailed engineering-approval process for equipment entering a classified area, and supply is normally expected to run through a Japan-registered partner. A device can be fully TIIS-certified and still be unlisted on an operator’s own approved-equipment register, so plan for two timelines rather than one.

How to evaluate and buy in Japan

The EX-04Z1 launches in Q4 2026. None of these steps needs a purchase order, and they are ordered so that your approvals and our certification run in parallel.

  1. 01

    Send us the area classification

    Give us the zone, gas group and temperature class for the areas a device would enter, plus the operator whose approval you work under. That is enough for us to say plainly where certification stands against your requirement.

  2. 02

    Take the datasheet and the certificate schedule

    You receive the full specification and a written list of the certifications in progress with target dates. An HSE officer can start an equipment selection justification with it, and procurement can open a vendor file.

  3. 03

    Join the evaluation programme

    Evaluation units are going to Japanese operators and contractors for handling and MDM trials in safe areas, moving to classified-area pilots once the certificate exists.

  4. 04

    Supply through a Japan-registered partner

    Procurement processes at ENEOS, Idemitsu and other major operators favour a locally registered supplier who can hold the TIIS documentation. Evaluation quantities can be supplied directly while that channel is appointed.

Can we buy an explosion proof mobile phone from SAMS in Japan today?

Not yet. The EX-04Z1 is pre-launch and every certification behind it, including IECEx, ATEX and the TIIS route, is still in progress ahead of the Q4 2026 launch. What exists today is the datasheet, the certification schedule and a place in the evaluation programme.

What is the price of an explosion proof phone in Japan?

We do not publish one. Until certification completes there is no list price, and pre-launch pricing is available on request against a stated quantity and site.

Will ENEOS or Idemitsu accept it on site?

That decision belongs to each operator and follows the certificates rather than preceding them. TIIS certification and the underlying IECEx evidence are what their engineering review is built on.

Does it work in Japanese?

Yes. Android 16 runs the interface in Japanese or English on the same handset and the user manual will be issued in both.

Will it handle typhoon-season conditions?

It is designed to, with IP68 sealing and MIL-STD-810H testing behind it, covering the humidity and storm exposure typical of Japan’s coastal industrial sites.
