Explosion proof mobile phone for Roche in Switzerland

Roche is headquartered in Basel, and its Swiss pharmaceutical operations include sterile and solid-dose production at Kaiseraugst, a short way up the Rhine. This page is for the EHS, engineering and site-services staff who need an explosion proof mobile phone in Switzerland for the few areas where gas or dust is present. The sites · Basel and Kaiseraugst

What counts as classified when an explosion proof mobile phone goes to Roche sites

Roche's Basel base combines a headquarters, research and development, and pharmaceutical technical development with a very large office population. Kaiseraugst, about fifteen kilometres to the east, is described by Roche as a main manufacturing centre for sterile drug products, including monoclonal antibodies, and one of its worldwide centres of excellence for sterile parenteral manufacture. The site has also been expanded to produce solid dosage forms such as tablets and capsules. That tells you where the hazard sits. Sterile filling and biologics production are mostly not explosive environments, but the surrounding infrastructure is: solvent stores, alcohol-based cleaning and disinfection, laboratory solvents in analytical and development work, utilities and gas supplies, and powder handling in tablet and capsule manufacture, where fine dust and sometimes solvent granulation produce zone questions. A handset for these places is for a defined group of people: maintenance and engineering technicians, laboratory and process-development staff, utilities operators and construction contractors. Classification belongs to the site's explosion protection document, and the device has to match it.

Dust zones next to gas zones

Solid dosage manufacture can put a Zone 21 or 22 area near a vapour area. The EX-04Z1 is designed for Zone 1 and Zone 21 and is being certified with that combination in mind, so one handset covers both.

Data discipline around a camera

Pharmaceutical sites take photography seriously. Android Enterprise and the company's MDM let IT define where the camera may be used, and the 50 MP camera is available for the inspections that are permitted.

Surviving a wipe-down routine

IP68 sealing is designed so the device can be wiped down between rooms. The approved cleaning agents on a Roche site should be checked against the manual before routine use.

A Basel working week in several languages

German, French and Italian interfaces are available under Android 16, and information supplied with the product must be in the official language of the place of sale.

Questions · Roche

Frequently asked questions

Do Roche's sterile production areas need an Ex phone?

Generally not. Sterile filling and biologics production are controlled environments but are not usually classified as explosive atmospheres. The EX-04Z1 is for the solvent, powder and utility areas where classification exists, as decided by the site.

What does a Swiss pharmaceutical company need to see from a handset supplier?

We have not been through a Roche approval and will not describe one. Ask the site's procurement and EHS teams for the equipment approval requirements, the classification of the areas concerned and the documents they want alongside the ATEX or IECEx certificate.

Is the certificate European or Swiss?

Both, in effect. Switzerland applies the VGSEB, SR 734.6, which mirrors the ATEX directive, and the mutual recognition agreement with the EU means a notified body assessment is recognised. Certification of the EX-04Z1 is in progress and no certificate has been issued.

Can the handset be managed with our existing tools?

It runs Android 16 with Android Enterprise and is designed to be managed from existing MDM consoles. Compatibility with a particular console should be confirmed during an evaluation.
