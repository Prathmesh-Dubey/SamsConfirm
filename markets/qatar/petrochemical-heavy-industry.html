Why it fits petrochemicals

Built for fertiliser and polymer dust together with gas

Qatar's downstream sector spans ammonia synthesis, urea granulation and polyethylene production, each with a different dust profile.

Zone 21 dust across fertiliser and polymer lines

Urea granulation and polyethylene pelletising both generate combustible dust, alongside Zone 1 ammonia synthesis gas. One specification is designed to cover all three.

Built for Mesaieed's industrial density

Mesaieed packs multiple petrochemical and fertiliser plants into one compact zone, and a rugged device suits frequent movement between adjoining sites.

Full-shift battery for continuous process plants

Ammonia and polymer plants run continuous operations, and the 4,000 mAh cell is specified for a full shift without an in-area recharge.
