The sector · Petrochemicals and heavy industry

Explosion proof mobile phone for Qatar's petrochemical plants

Qatar's downstream industry is concentrated at Mesaieed Industrial Area, with processing at Ras Laffan alongside. At Mesaieed, QAPCO makes ethylene and low density polyethylene, Q-Chem produces polyethylene and normal alpha olefins, Qatar Vinyl Company runs a chlor-alkali and vinyl chain, QAFAC makes methanol and MTBE, and QAFCO makes ammonia and urea. Qatar Steel and Qatalum add direct reduction and aluminium smelting. What binds this group together is the dust. Polyethylene resin, urea, alumina and coke are all handled as powders, pellets or granules, and each needs its own dust assessment next to the flammable gas assessment. Site-level detail is on the Mesaieed page and Ras Laffan page.

What gas and dust together do to a handset

A device chosen only for gas is wrong in a bagging hall, and one chosen only for dust is wrong at a cracker. These are the requirements that decide a petrochemical specification.

Group IIB and IIC in one plant

Ethylene falls in group IIB and hydrogen in IIC. The ATEX marking target of II 2 G Ex ib IIC T4 covers both, which matters when a technician moves from a cracker to a hydrogen-bearing unit.

Polymer dust is a real explosion risk

Fine polyethylene powder can be ignitable in air. The Zone 21 design target is aimed at the silo, conveyor and bagging areas, subject to each plant's dust classification.

Washdown and powder together

Product is washed off equipment at the end of a task. IP68 sealing keeps powder out of the ports and allows the unit to be rinsed.

A shared perimeter, separate fleets

Several operators share Mesaieed's roads and permits. Android Enterprise lets each manage its own enrolment, camera policy and wipe without sharing an administrator.

How work is organised

Continuous plants, turnarounds and approval in petrochemicals

Crackers, polymer lines and fertiliser trains run for long periods between planned shutdowns, which are the busiest time for contractors.

Shift rounds
Operators walk a set route, and an enrolled handset can add a photograph and position to a defect report.
Turnarounds
A plant is shut for inspection and maintenance and contractors arrive in large numbers for a limited period.
Product changes
Polymer plants change grades. Handsets with consistent policies reduce the risk that a device wanders between areas with different rules.
Vendor approval
Each producer has its own supplier process. Ask its procurement and HSE teams what applies. Nothing here describes a specific programme.
National conformity
MOCI conformity and CRA type approval are in the launch programme and have not been granted.

Questions · Petrochemicals

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 certified for Qatar petrochemical use yet?

No. It is pre-launch, with a Q4 2026 launch date and certification in progress. Nothing has been issued, and each producer decides on its own review.

Does it cover urea and polymer dust?

The design target is Zone 21 dust, and the plant's own dust classification decides where it applies. Dust certification is part of the programme in progress.

Is an explosion proof phone the same as a flameproof one?

No. They are different protection concepts. The EX-04Z1 is designed to Ex ib, intrinsic safety, which limits circuit energy rather than containing an explosion.

Which plant page should I read first?

Read the page for your own site: QAFCO for fertiliser, Mesaieed for the wider industrial area, or Ras Laffan for the gas processing side.
