Why it fits

Designed toward QatarEnergy's engineering review

QatarEnergy's vendor process sits above MOCI national conformity, with its own technical evaluation before equipment reaches a classified site.

IECEx as the evidence base

QatarEnergy's engineering review works from the certificate. An IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the documented basis the vendor file is built around.

Built for North Field expansion scale

The North Field East and South expansions are adding capacity across multiple new trains, and a fleet-manageable device suits that scale of growth.

A Qatari partner for supply

Supply is being structured through a Qatar-registered partner able to hold the vendor documentation and carry spares near Ras Laffan.

The approval path

Two gates for QatarEnergy

National conformity and QatarEnergy's own vendor approval run on separate timelines.

National conformity
MOCI conformity marking, with an IECEx Certificate of Conformity as the technical basis.
QatarEnergy vendor approval
A separate engineering review through QatarEnergy's own tender prequalification process.
Typical environments
Upstream gas fields, the North Field expansion, and associated onshore processing.
