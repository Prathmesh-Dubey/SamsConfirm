The company · QatarEnergy

Explosion proof mobile phone across QatarEnergy's Qatar sites

QatarEnergy is Qatar's state energy company, and its footprint is wider than any single plant. Onshore, the Dukhan field and its processing facilities sit on the west coast. Offshore, the company's fields and the North Field platforms supply the gas that comes ashore at Ras Laffan. The two industrial cities, Ras Laffan and Mesaieed, are QatarEnergy-owned estates that host its own operations and many joint ventures. On top of that the North Field expansion is the largest LNG capacity growth programme in the world. A handset specification written for one of these will be read by people who also manage the others, so the same document may be asked to cover several site types.

What group-wide use asks of a device

When one organisation spans a desert field, a gas platform and an LNG city, the demand is for a device that works across all of them under one management policy.

Heat across all of it

Coastal and desert Qatar passes 45 degrees Celsius in summer. The rugged build covers temperature and humidity, and the T4 marking target is the figure engineers check against the area's permitted surface temperature.

Distances that favour a long battery

Dukhan and the industrial cities are measured in kilometres. A 4,000 mAh battery specified for a full shift matters more where there is nowhere to charge between facilities.

One management policy for many affiliates

Android Enterprise and the group's existing MDM console let telecoms apply camera, application and wipe policies by site or affiliate, rather than building a separate scheme for each.

Arabic and English on one device

The interface runs in either language, which suits a workforce and contractor pool that uses both, and the language can be set per user.

How buying works

Registration, local content and approval at QatarEnergy

Supplying a QatarEnergy operation is commercial as well as technical, and the commercial side starts early.

Technical review
Engineering works from the certificate. An IECEx Certificate of Conformity is the evidence base, and none has been issued for the EX-04Z1 yet.
Supplier registration
Ask QatarEnergy procurement what registration is needed for the category of equipment. We do not describe the requirements beyond that.
In-Country Value
QatarEnergy's Tawteen programme scores local contribution in bids. A supplier without a score is at a disadvantage, which is why a Qatar-registered partner is being appointed.
National conformity
MOCI conformity and CRA type approval are separate from the operator's review and are in the launch programme.
Trials
Evaluation units for handling and management trials in safe areas come first. Classified-area use waits for the certificate.

Questions · QatarEnergy

Frequently asked questions

Is the EX-04Z1 approved for QatarEnergy sites?

No. It is pre-launch with a Q4 2026 launch date. Certification is in progress, and QatarEnergy's own technical and supplier review follows the certificate.

Does a Qatari partner matter for supply?

Local-content scoring affects bid evaluation across QatarEnergy contracts, so a registered partner with a score is the practical route for volume. Evaluation quantities can be supplied before the channel is in place.

Which affiliate should we contact first?

Start with the operator that owns the area you work in, since its HSE and telecoms teams control what enters the plant. The group-level procurement process applies in addition.

Does QatarEnergy publish a handset specification?

We have not verified one and will not describe one. Ask the relevant procurement and engineering teams for the equipment requirements that apply to your scope.
